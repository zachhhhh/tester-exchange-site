Delete your Tester Exchange account

Tester Exchange (Android app and website) · developer: Zach Chen

How to request deletion

  1. Email gchen2036@gmail.com from the Google account you sign in to Tester Exchange with.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Tester Exchange account. No other details are needed.
  3. We confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile (email address, name, avatar)
  • Your credit balance and credit history
  • Your test participation and the daily usage records it produced
  • Your registered device (hashed device ID and model)
  • Apps and campaigns you listed, and feedback you wrote

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Aggregate counts already shown to other developers (for example "12 testers active on day 5" in their own report) stay as numbers without your identity. Deletion is permanent and unused credits are forfeited.

Deleting only some data

You can leave any test at any time in the app or website, and uninstalling the app or revoking Usage access stops all collection immediately. To delete specific records without closing your account, email the same address and say what to remove.
