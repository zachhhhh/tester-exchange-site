The two routes to testers

  • Free: test other developers' apps at https://testerexchange.com/earn on your phone. Each app you test brings one tester to your app, so you can fill your 12 seats without paying.
  • Paid and faster: pay once for a pack of testers; current prices are on https://testerexchange.com/llms.txt. Paid apps get testers before anyone else.

What Google requires

Google's Play Console Help says personal developer accounts created after 13 November 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in continuously for at least 14 days before they can apply for production access (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465). No AI tool can do those 14 days for you: they must be real people on real phones.

Let your AI tool do the setup

Tester Exchange has an MCP server, an HTTP API and an A2A agent. The full list of clients and config snippets is at https://testerexchange.com/agents/. The general guide is https://testerexchange.com/guide/automate-closed-testing-with-ai-agents/.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an AI tool to use Tester Exchange?

No. The website works on its own. The MCP server and API are optional shortcuts for people who already work with an AI tool.

Can the AI tool pay for testers?

No. It can return a checkout link, but payment happens in your browser. The free route is testing other apps at https://testerexchange.com/earn.

Is Tester Exchange an official integration of these tools?

No. It is an independent service. These pages only show how to connect a tool's standard MCP or HTTP support to Tester Exchange.
