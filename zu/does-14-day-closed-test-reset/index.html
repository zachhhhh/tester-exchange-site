Yini i-Google eyibalayo ngempela

Isidingo se-Google sama-akhawunti omuntu siqu adalwe ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023 siwukuhlola okuvaliwe okunabahloli abangu-12 okungenani abenze i-opt-in ngokuqhubekayo okungenani izinsuku ezingu-14. Imininingwane emithathu emazwini e-Google ibalulekile lapha:

  • Izinsuku ezingu-14 kufanele zilandelane. Akukho ukumisa bese uqhubeka.
  • Abahloli abenza i-opt-in, bahlola ngaphansi kwezinsuku ezingu-14 bese benza i-opt-out abababalwa esidingweni.
  • Okungenani abahloli abangu-12 kufanele babe sebenze i-opt-in lapho ufaka isicelo sokufinyelela ku-production.

Cabanga ngomhloli ngamunye: umuntu ngamunye unewashi lakhe eliqala lapho enza i-opt-in. Uyafaneleka uma okungenani abantu abangu-12 behlale benze i-opt-in izinsuku ezingu-14 ngokulandelana, futhi basenze i-opt-in.

Izimo ezivamile

  • Wawunabangu-15, oyedwa uhamba ngosuku lwe-8. Abanye abangu-14 baqhubeka bebalwa. Ngosuku lwe-14 usenabahloli abangu-14 abafanelekile. Akukho nkinga.
  • Wawunabangu-12 ncamashi, oyedwa uhamba ngosuku lwe-8. Manje abantu abangu-11 kuphela abangafinyelela ezinsukwini ezingu-14. Udinga umuntu ozongena esikhundleni, futhi izinsuku ezingu-14 zalowo muntu ziqala ngosuku enza ngalo i-opt-in. Usuku lakho lokuqala lokufaka isicelo luyaphindela emuva cishe iviki.
  • Umhloli wenza i-opt-out abuye enze i-opt-in. I-Google ithi ukwenza i-opt-out bese wenza i-opt-in futhi kuqala kabusha ukubalwa kwalowo mhloli okulandelanayo.
  • Umhloli uhlala enze i-opt-in kodwa uyeka ukuvula i-app. Usabalwa ku-12 emthethweni we-opt-in, kodwa uyakulimaza ekubambeni iqhaza, i-Google eliqaphelayo efomini le-production.
  • Umhloli ususa i-app kodwa akenzi i-opt-out. Cishe usenze i-opt-in, kodwa akahloli. Mphathe njengecala langaphambilini.

Kungani ucela abahloli abangu-15, hhayi abangu-12

Ukuhamba kwabahloli kuyinto evamile. Abantu bashintsha amafoni, balibala, baphelelwa isikhala noma baphelelwa yinjabulo. Ngabahloli abangu-12 ncamashi, noma yimuphi oyedwa ohambayo ngaphambi kosuku lwe-14 uphindisela emuva usuku lwakho lokufaka isicelo. Ngabangu-15, ungalahlekelwa abathathu futhi usafaka isicelo ngesikhathi.

Futhi kusiza ekubambeni iqhaza. I-Google ibheka indlela abahloli abayisebenzise ngayo i-app. Uma abathathu kwabayishumi nanhlanu bethule, abayishumi nambili abasele basabonisa ukusetshenziswa okuqhubekayo. Uma abathathu kwabayishumi nambili bethule, ingxenye yesine yokuhlola kwakho ibonakala ingasebenzi.

Ku-Tester Exchange, abahloli abangu-15 bakhokha $14.99 uma kuqhathaniswa no-$12.99 ngabangu-12, okuyi-inshuwalense ethengekayo ngokumelene neviki elengeziwe lokulinda.

Yini okufanele uyenze phakathi nokuhlola

  1. Hlola inani labahloli abenze i-opt-in ku-Play Console njalo ezinsukwini ezimbili noma ezintathu.
  2. Uma ubona ukwehla, engeza abangena esikhundleni ngokushesha. Usuku ngalunye olulindayo luyisuku elengezwa kwabangu-14 babo.
  3. Thumela umyalezo kubahloli abathule nomsebenzi owodwa oqondile ("zama isikrini esisha sokusesha ku-build 1.0.3") kunesikhumbuzo esijwayelekile.
  4. Khipha isibuyekezo ngesikhathi sokuhlola. I-build entsha inikeza abahloli isizathu sokuvula i-app futhi.
  5. Ungafaki isicelo uze ube nabahloli abangu-12 okungenani abadlule izinsuku ezingu-14 ezilandelanayo futhi abasenze i-opt-in. Umkhawulo wababili noma abathathu uphephile kakhulu.

Izinto ezingaqalisi kabusha inqubekela phambili yakho

  • Ukulayisha ama-build amasha ku-track efanayo yokuhlola okuvaliwe. Abahloli bahlala benze i-opt-in futhi bathola isibuyekezo.
  • Ukwengeza abahloli abaningi. Abantu abasha baqala izinsuku zabo ezingu-14; abahloli abakhona bagcina ezabo.
  • Ukuhlela uhlu lwesitolo noma amanothi okukhishwa.

Izinto okufanele ugweme: ukukhipha uhlu lwama-imeyili noma i-Google Group ku-track, noma ukususa i-track. Abahloli bangalahlekelwa ukufinyelela, futhi akucaci ukuthi i-Google ibaphatha kanjani ngemva kwalokho i-opt-in yabo, ngakho ungazami.

Indlela i-Tester Exchange eyiphatha ngayo ukuhamba kwabahloli

Ku-Tester Exchange, abahloli bangabantu bangempela emafonini abo e-Android futhi ubona ukusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke kwabo bonke. Uma umhloli eyeka, omusha uthatha isihlalo, futhi ukhokhela kuphela izinsuku abahloli abavula ngazo i-app yakho ngempela. Amakhredithi angasetshenzisiwe asala ebhalansini yakho. Ungakhokha kanye ngabahloli abangu-12, abangu-15 noma abangu-25, noma uthole abahloli mahhala ngokuhlola ama-app abanye onjiniyela ku-https://testerexchange.com/earn ku-browser yefoni yakho.

Uma umhloli oyedwa ehamba, izinsuku zami ezingu-14 ziqala kabusha?

Hhayi kwabanye. Iwashi lomhloli ngamunye lihlukile. Ulahlekelwa isikhathi kuphela uma wehlela ngaphansi kwabahloli abangu-12 abangaqeda izinsuku ezingu-14 ezilandelanayo.

Ingabe umhloli ongena esikhundleni aqala ngosuku loku-1?

Yebo. Izinsuku zakhe ezingu-14 ezilandelanayo ziqala lapho enza i-opt-in.

Kuthiwani uma umhloli enza i-opt-out ngephutha abuye ajoyine?

I-Google ithi ukwenza i-opt-out bese wenza i-opt-in futhi kuqala kabusha ukubalwa kwakhe okulandelanayo.

Ingabe abahloli abangasebenzi bayabalwa?

Uma behlala benze i-opt-in, babalwa ku-12. Kodwa ukubamba iqhaza okuphansi kuyisizathu esivamile sokuthi i-Google icele ukuhlola okwengeziwe.

Kufanele ngibe nabahloli abangaki abangenasidingo?
