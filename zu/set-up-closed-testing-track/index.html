Indlela yokusetha i-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe ku-Play Console, isinyathelo ngesinyathelo

Kubuyekezwe ngo-Novemba 2026

Ngaphambi kokuthi iwashi lakho lezinsuku ezingu-14 liqale, udinga i-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe eshicilelwe, enabahloli abanamathiselwe, futhi ekunikeza isixhumanisi se-opt-in esisebenzayo. Izinkinga eziningi kamuva, njengabahloli ababona "app not available", zivela esinyathelweni esiphutshuziwe lapha. Lo mhlahlandlela ulandela amamenyu amanje e-Play Console (Test and release, bese Testing, bese Closed testing) neSikhungo Sosizo se-Google.

Ngaphambi kokuqala

  • I-akhawunti yonjiniyela ye-Play Console eneziqinisekiso zobuwena eziqediwe.
  • I-app yakho idaliwe ku-Play Console, nohlu lwesitolo, i-content rating, izethameli ezihlosiwe, i-data safety nezinye izimemezelo ze-App content kugcwalisiwe. I-Play Console ayikuvumeli ukuthumela ukukhishwa ukuze kubuyekezwe ngenkathi kunezinto ezidingekayo ezingekho.
  • I-Android App Bundle (.aab) esayiniwe enegama le-package olijabulelayo ukuligcina unomphela. Awukwazi ukulishintsha kamuva.
  • Uhlelo lwabahloli bakho: okungenani abangu-12, kuhle abangu-15, abantu bangempela abanamafoni e-Android nama-Google Account.

1. Vula noma dala i-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe

  1. Ku-Play Console, khetha i-app yakho.
  2. Iya ku-Test and release, bese Testing, bese Closed testing.
  3. I-Play Console ikunikeza i-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe ezenzakalelayo (evame ukubizwa ngokuthi i-Alpha). Yisebenzise. Ungadala ezinye ama-track ezengeziwe, kodwa eyodwa yanele isidingo.
  4. Chofoza i-Manage track.

2. Khetha amazwe nezifunda

Vula ithebhu ye-Countries / regions bese wengeza amazwe lapho kuhlala khona abahloli bakho. Umhloli ezweni olingakhethanga ngeke akwazi ukufaka i-app.

I-Google iphawula ukuthi izinguquko zamazwe asatshalaliswa zisebenza kuwo wonke ama-track, nokuthi ama-track okuhlola okuvaliwe angeziwe awasekeli ukuqondiswa kwamazwe kwawo. Uma abahloli bakho basakazeke emhlabeni wonke, ukukhetha okulula ukwengeza yonke izwe ongathumela kulo ngokusemthethweni.

3. Engeza abahloli: uhlu lwama-imeyili noma i-Google Group

Vula ithebhu ye-Testers. Unezinketho ezimbili, futhi ungasebenzisa zombili ku-track eyodwa.

  • Uhlu lwama-imeyili: chofoza i-Create email list, lunike igama, bese uthayipha amakheli noma ulayishe i-CSV. I-Google ivumela abasebenzisi abafinyelela ku-2,000 ohlwini ngalunye. Ukulayisha i-CSV kubhala phezu kwamakheli asevele ohlwini. Ikheli ngalinye kufanele libe yi-Google Account umhloli ayisebenzisa ku-Play Store yefoni yakhe.
  • I-Google Group: faka ikheli leqembu, njenge-yourgroup@googlegroups.com. Amalungu kuphela eqembu angajoyina ukuhlola kwakho, futhi kufanele ajoyine iqembu ngaphambi kokwenza i-opt-in. Ukwengeza umuntu eqenjini yikho konke okwenzayo; awuphinde uthinte i-Play Console.

Uma usebenzisa i-Tester Exchange, engeza iqembu elithi testerexchange-testers@googlegroups.com ngaphansi kwe-Google Groups bese ugcina. Leli kheli elilodwa livumela wonke umhloli esimthumelayo ukuthi afake i-app yakho. Bheka futhi umhlahlandlela we-Google Group vs uhlu lwama-imeyili.

4. Engeza isiteshi sempendulo

Kuthebhu efanayo ye-Testers, engeza i-URL yempendulo noma ikheli le-imeyili. Abahloli bayibona lapho bejoyina. Uzodinga ukufinyeza impendulo yabahloli efomini lokufinyelela ku-production, ngakho kwenze kube lula: i-Google Form emfushane noma ikheli le-imeyili elizinikele liyasebenza.

5. Dala ukukhishwa bese ukuthumela ukuze kubuyekezwe

  1. Ku-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe, chofoza i-Create new release.
  2. Layisha i-.aab yakho. Vumela i-Play App Signing ilawule ukhiye wakho ngaphandle uma unesizathu sokungakwenzi.
  3. Engeza igama lokukhishwa namanothi amafushane okukhishwa, isibonelo "First closed test build".
  4. Chofoza i-Next, lungisa noma yiziphi izinkinga, bese ugcina.
  5. Iya ku-Publishing overview bese uthumela izinguquko ukuze zibuyekezwe. Uma i-managed publishing ivuliwe, kufanele futhi uchofoze i-Publish ngemva kokugunyazwa.

Ukukhishwa kokuhlola okuvaliwe kuma-akhawunti amasha kuyabuyekezwa ngaphambi kokuba kube bukhoma. Izikhathi ziyahlukahluka kusuka emahoreni kuya ezinsukwini ezimbalwa.

6. Yabelana ngesixhumanisi se-opt-in

I-Google ithi isixhumanisi se-opt-in sivela kuphela uma isimo se-app sithi Published. Sikopishe ethebhini ye-Testers. Sibukeka njengo-https://play.google.com/apps/testing/your.package.name.

Ngokokuqala uma ushicilela, isixhumanisi singathatha amahora amaningana ukuqala ukusebenza. Abahloli abakwazi ukuthola ukuhlola okuvaliwe ngokusesha ku-Google Play, ngakho badinga lesi sixhumanisi noma isixhumanisi se-Play Store se-app yakho.

  1. Umhloli ungena ku-Google Account efanayo oyengeze (noma esenhlwini ye-Google Group yakho).
  2. Uvula isixhumanisi se-opt-in bese ethephela ukuze abe ngumhloli.
  3. Ufaka i-app yakho ku-Google Play ngesixhumanisi esikhasini lelo.
  4. Uyivula, futhi aqhubeke ayivula phakathi nezinsuku ezingu-14.

7. Hlola ukuthi izinsuku ezingu-14 ziyabalwa

I-Dashboard yakho ibonisa isidingo sokuhlola okuvaliwe nokuthi bangaki abahloli abenze i-opt-in. Yihlole njalo ezinsukwini ezimbalwa. Uma inombolo yehlela ngaphansi kuka-12, thola abangena esikhundleni ngokushesha, ngoba isidingo sibala abahloli abangu-12 abahlale benze i-opt-in ngokuqhubekayo.

Ngesikhathi sokuhlola, layisha okungenani i-build eyodwa entsha ku-track efanayo. Ibonisa i-Google ukuthi usebenzise impendulo, futhi abahloli ku-track bathola isibuyekezo ngokuzenzakalelayo.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe ngingasebenzisa ukuhlola kwangaphakathi esikhundleni salokho?

Cha. Isidingo sidinga ukuhlola okuvaliwe. I-Google futhi ithi abantu abenze i-opt-in ekuhlolweni kwakho kwangaphakathi abakwazi ukujoyina ukuhlola okuvaliwe baze bakushiye lokho kwangaphakathi.

Kufanele ngisebenzise uhlu lwama-imeyili noma i-Google Group?

I-Google Group ilula uma abahloli beza futhi behamba, ngoba awuhleli i-track. Uhlu lwama-imeyili lulungile eqenjini elimisiwe labangane. Ungasebenzisa zombili.

Kungani ngingawuboni isixhumanisi se-opt-in?

Sivela kuphela uma ukukhishwa ku-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe sekushicilelwe. Hlola i-Publishing overview ukubuyekezwa okulindile noma izinguquko ezingakathunyelwa.

Ingabe umhloli udinga i-Google Account?

Yebo. I-Google ithi abasebenzisi badinga i-Google Account noma i-akhawunti ye-Google Workspace ukujoyina ukuhlola, futhi kufanele ibe yi-akhawunti ku-Play Store yefoni yabo.

Ingabe ngingayenza i-app yokuhlola okuvaliwe ikhokhelwe?

Ungakwenza, kodwa i-Google ithi abahloli kufanele bathenge ama-app akhokhelwayo ekuhlolweni okuvaliwe. Gcina i-app yokuhlola ingakhokhelwa ukugwema ukulahlekelwa abahloli.
