Kungani ukuzibandakanya kubalulekile

Kunezithiyo ezimbili. Okokuqala, iwashi lezinsuku ezingu-14 libala kuphela uma okungenani abahloli abangu-12 bebhalisile, ngakho abantu abashiya ukuhlola bangamisa inqubekela phambili yakho. Okwesibili, uma ufaka isicelo se-production, i-Google ibuyekeza ukuthi abahloli bayisebenzisile ngempela yini i-app phakathi nokuhlola.

Uhlu lwabahloli abangu-12 ababhalisile abayeka ukuvula i-app ngemuva kosuku lwesibili ludlula isithiyo sokuqala ephepheni kodwa luhluleke kwesesibili. Ukuzibandakanya yikho okuguqula ukuhlola okuvaliwe kube ukuhlola kwangempela.

Amacebo asebenzayo abuyisa abahloli

  1. Thola abahloli abangu-14–15, hhayi abangu-12. Kuhlale kukhona omatasa, oshintsha ifoni noma olahlekelwa yintshisekelo. Abahloli abengeziwe bayisivikelo sakho.
  2. Nika umsebenzi owodwa oqondile ngosuku. "Vula i-app" kulula ukukhohlwa. "Namuhla, dala uhlu bese wabelana ngalo" kunikeza isizathu sokubuya. Uhlu olufushane lwemisebenzi lwakho konke ukuhlola nalo luyasebenza.
  3. Khipha isibuyekezo phakathi nokuhlola futhi usho ukuthi yini eshintshile. Layisha i-build entsha ku-track evaliwe ngasekupheleni kwesonto lokuqala bese utshela abahloli ukuthi yini entsha nokuthi bazame ini.
  4. Cela impendulo ebhaliwe. Imibuzo emibili noma emithathu eqondile ithola izimpendulo ezingcono kuno-"unawo yini umbono?". Phendula, futhi ulungise lokho ongakwazi.
  5. Khumbuza ngosuku lwesi-3, lwesi-7 nolwe-12. Usuku lwesi-3 lubamba labo abangakaze baqale ngempela, usuku lwesi-7 luqala kabusha isonto lesibili, usuku lwe-12 ludlulisa wonke umuntu emgqeni wokuqeda.
  6. Shintsha abayekayo ngokushesha. Lapho umhloli ethula izinsuku ezimbalwa, mema omusha ukuze uhlale ngaphezu kwabangu-12.

Uhlelo olulula lwezinsuku ezingu-14

  • Usuku 1: umlayezo wokwamukela, isixhumanisi sokufaka nohlu lwemisebenzi.
  • Usuku 3: isikhumbuzo sokuqala nokuhlola okusheshayo kokuthi ngubani ongakayivuli i-app.
  • Izinsuku 5–8: layisha i-build entsha enokulungiswa bese umemezela ukuthi yini eshintshile.
  • Usuku 7: isikhumbuzo kanye nombuzo omfushane wempendulo.
  • Usuku 12: isikhumbuzo sokugcina nesicelo sempendulo ebhaliwe.
  • Usuku 14: bonga abahloli bakho futhi ubhale phansi izinombolo zakho zesicelo se-production.

Isiza kanjani i-Tester Exchange

  • Ukusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke kuqinisekiswa kumafoni: i-app ye-Android ye-Tester Exchange ihlola efonini yomhloli ngamunye ukuthi i-app yakho ivulwe ngalolo suku.
  • Umbiko wansuku zonke: bona ukuthi bangaki abahloli ababesebenza usuku ngalunye, kanye nempendulo abayithumele, ukuze ubone abayekayo kusenesikhathi.
  • Izimpendulo ze-production: thola izimpendulo ezisalungiswa zemibuzo yokufinyelela ku-production ezisekelwe kudatha yakho yangempela yokuhlola.

I-Google isenza isinqumo sokugcina mayelana nokufinyelela ku-production. La mathuluzi akusiza wenze ukuhlola okubonisa ukusetshenziswa kwangempela futhi ukuchaze ngokucacile.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Abahloli bami bayekile ukuvula i-app. Kufanele ngenzeni?

Bathumele umlayezo onomsebenzi owodwa oqondile, khipha isibuyekezo esincane ukuze kube khona okusha okuzanywayo, futhi umeme abazothatha indawo ngokushesha kunoma ubani ongabuyi.

Abahloli kufanele bayivule kangaki i-app?

I-Google ayishicileli inombolo. Ukusetshenziswa okuvamile ngabahloli abaningi kuzo zonke izinsuku ezingu-14 yiphethini ephephe kakhulu, futhi ukusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke kuyisibonakaliso esicace kakhulu.

Kwenzekani uma ngehla ngaphansi kwabahloli abangu-12?

Izinsuku ezingu-14 zibalwa kuphela uma okungenani abahloli abangu-12 bebhalisile. Thola abangu-14–15 ukuze abambalwa abayekayo bangamisi inqubekela phambili yakho.

Ngempela kufanele ngikhiphe isibuyekezo phakathi nokuhlola?

Yebo. I-build entsha elungisa izinkinga ezibikiwe ikhombisa i-Google ukuthi usebenzise ukuhlola ukuthuthukisa i-app, futhi inika abahloli isizathu sokuyivula futhi.

Ingabe impendulo ebhaliwe iyadingeka?

Uhlu lwemibuzo ye-production lubuza ukuthi abahloli bakutshele ini nokuthi yini oyishintshile. Impendulo ebhaliwe ikunika izibonelo zangempela ongakhomba kuzo.
