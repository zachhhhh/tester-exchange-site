Umthetho: abahloli abangu-12 × izinsuku ezingu-14

I-Google Play idinga ukuthi ama-akhawunti amasha onjiniyela bomuntu siqu enze ukuhlola okuvaliwe ngaphambi kokufaka isicelo sokufinyelela ku-production. Isidingo yilesi:

  • Okungenani abahloli abangu-12 abenze i-opt-in ku-track yakho yokuhlola okuvaliwe
  • Bahlala bebhalisile okungenani izinsuku ezingu-14 zilandelana
  • Ngemuva kwalokho, ufaka isicelo se-production ku-Play Console futhi uphendule imibuzo mayelana nokuhlola kwakho
  • Ama-akhawunti ezinhlangano (anenombolo ye-D-U-N-S) akhululiwe

Inani lalingabahloli abangu-20 kwaze kwaba nguDisemba 2024, lapho i-Google ilehlisela ku-12. Iwashi lezinsuku ezingu-14 libala kuphela izinsuku ezinabahloli abangu-12 noma ngaphezulu ababhalisile, ngakho uma abahloli behamba wehlela ku-11, ulahlekelwa yinqubekela phambili.

Kungani ama-app enqatshwa ngemuva kwezinsuku ezingu-14

Onjiniyela abaningi bafinyelela ezinsukwini ezingu-14 kodwa basenqatshwa ngomlayezo omayelana nokuhlola noma ukuzibandakanya okunganele. I-Google ibheka ukuthi abahloli bayivulile futhi bayisebenzisa ngempela yini i-app, hhayi nje ukuthi benze i-opt-in.

Ukufakwa okuvela kubantu abangayivuli neze i-app, noma ifoni eyodwa esetshenziswa ama-akhawunti amaningi, akubalwa njengokuhlola kwangempela. Okusizayo: abahloli abavula i-app njalo phakathi nezinsuku ezingu-14, impendulo ongayichaza, nama-build amasha akhombisa ukuthi wenze okuthile ngayo.

Setha ukuhlola okuvaliwe ku-Play Console

  1. Vula i-Play Console → i-app yakho → Test and release → Testing → Closed testing, bese udala i-track (noma usebenzise i-track ye-Alpha ezenzakalelayo).
  2. Ngaphansi kwe-Testers, khetha i-Google Group noma uhlu lwama-imeyili. I-Google Group ilula kakhulu: noma ubani ojoyina iqembu angenza i-opt-in.
  3. Layisha i-app bundle ku-track, khetha amazwe, bese uthumela ukukhishwa ukuze kubuyekezwe.
  4. Uma sekugunyaziwe, kopisha isixhumanisi se-opt-in (https://play.google.com/apps/testing/your.package.name). Abahloli basivula, bathephe ku-Become a tester, bese befaka ku-Google Play.
  5. Bheka ithebhu ye-Testers ku-Play Console ukuze ubone ukuthi bangaki abantu asebenze i-opt-in.

Ungabathola kuphi abahloli bangempela abangu-12

  • Abangane nomndeni. Mahhala, kodwa abantu abaningi abanabo abasebenzisi be-Android abangu-12 abazimisele ukuvula i-app nsuku zonke amasonto amabili.
  • Imiphakathi yokuhlolelana. Onjiniyela bahlolelana ama-app. Mahhala, futhi abahloli bayakuqonda okudingekayo. Ingozi ngabantu abenza i-opt-in bese bengayivuli neze i-app yakho.
  • Izinsizakalo zabahloli ezikhokhelwayo. Ngokuvamile u-US$10–35 nge-app ngayinye. Kuyashesha, kodwa uthemba ukuthi abahloli bangempela futhi bayasebenza.
  • I-Tester Exchange. Ukushintshana kokuhlola kwamahhala lapho ukusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke kuqinisekiswa yi-app esizayo efonini yomhloli ngamunye, futhi abahloli bakhokhelwa ngamakhredithi kuphela ngezinsuku eziqinisekisiwe.

Isebenza kanjani i-Tester Exchange

  1. Engeza i-app yakho: namathisela isixhumanisi sayo se-Google Play ku-/start.
  2. Khetha ukuthi uzobathola kanjani abahloli: mahhala (ifoni yakho inikezwa ama-app okuhlola, umzuzu ngosuku, futhi i-app ngayinye oyihlolayo iletha umhloli ku-app yakho) noma weqe ukuhlola ngamakhredithi (ama-app akhokhelwe athola abahloli kuqala).
  3. Faka i-app ye-Android ye-Tester Exchange efonini yakho bese uvumela i-Usage access. Ihlola kuphela ama-app owajoyinile.
  4. Engeza abahloli bakho ekuhlolweni kwakho okuvaliwe ku-Play Console.
  5. Abahloli bavula i-app yakho nsuku zonke izinsuku ezingu-14, futhi i-app esizayo emafonini abo iqinisekisa usuku ngalunye.
  6. Ngemuva kwezinsuku ezingu-14, vula umbiko wakho: abahloli abasebenzayo nsuku zonke, izimpendulo, nezimpendulo ezisalungiswa zemibuzo ye-Play Console yokufinyelela ku-production.

Gcina abahloli bezibandakanya izinsuku ezingu-14

  • Thola abahloli abangu-14–15, hhayi abangu-12 ncamashi, ukuze abambalwa abayekayo bangalisethi kabusha iwashi lakho.
  • Tshela abahloli ukuthi bazame ini: imisebenzi eyodwa noma emibili eqondile, hhayi "ngicela nihlole i-app yami".
  • Khipha okungenani isibuyekezo esisodwa phakathi nokuhlola, futhi ulungise lokho abahloli abakubikayo.
  • Cela impendulo ebhaliwe. Uzodinga ukuyifingqa uma ufaka isicelo se-production.

Faka isicelo sokufinyelela ku-production

Ngemuva kwezinsuku ezingu-14, iya ku-Play Console → Dashboard → Apply for production. I-Google ibuza ukuthi ubathole kanjani abahloli, bazibandakanye kanjani ne-app yakho, iyiphi impendulo oyitholile, yini oyishintshile, nokuthi kungani i-app isilungile. Phendula ngezinombolo zangempela: bangaki abahloli, bayisebenzise izinsuku ezingaki i-app, yini abayibikile, nokuthi yikuphi ukulungiswa okukhiphile. Ukubuyekezwa ngokuvamile kuthatha kufika ezinsukwini ezingu-7.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe abahloli abangu-12 kumele bayisebenzise i-app nsuku zonke?

I-Google ifuna bahlale bebhalisile izinsuku ezingu-14 futhi ibuyekeza ukuzibandakanya. Ukusetshenziswa kwangempela okuvamile yikho okugwema ukwenqatshwa ngenxa "yokuhlola okunganele".

Ngingasebenzisa amafoni ami noma ama-akhawunti ami njengabahloli?

I-Google iyakwazi ukubona uma ama-akhawunti amaningi esebenzisa idivayisi eyodwa, futhi lokho akubalwa njengokuhlola kwangempela. Sebenzisa abantu bangempela kumafoni abo.

Ingabe umhloli ohambayo usetha kabusha izinsuku ezingu-14?

Ukubala kuqhubeka kuphela ngezinsuku ezinabahloli okungenani abangu-12 ababhalisile. Thola abahloli abambalwa abengeziwe ukuze abayekayo bangakumisi.

Ingabe i-Tester Exchange imahhala?

Yebo. Ukuhlola kumahhala: i-app ngayinye oyihlolayo efonini yakho iletha umhloli ku-app yakho. Amalungu amasha athola amakhredithi okuqala amahhala. Ukuthenga amakhredithi akuphoqelekile futhi kukuvumela weqe ukuhlola; ama-app akhokhelwe athola abahloli kuqala.
