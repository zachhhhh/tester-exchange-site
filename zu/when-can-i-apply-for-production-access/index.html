Umthetho endimeni eyodwa

I-Google Play Console Help ithi ama-akhawunti abathuthukisi omuntu ngamunye adalwe ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023 kufanele aqhube ukuhlolwa okuvaliwe okungenani nabahloli abangu-12 abangenile ngokuqhubekayo okungenani izinsuku eziyi-14 ngaphambi kokuba bafake isicelo se-production. Uma uhlangabezana nezindlela zokulinganisa, inkinobho ethi Apply for production iyavela ku-Dashboard ku-Play Console.

Iwashi libalwa kumhloli ngamunye. Usuku lwakho lokuqala lokufaka isicelo yilolo lapho umhloli wesi-12 aqeda khona izinsuku eziyi-14 ezilandelanayo.

Isikhathi usuku nosuku

  1. Usuku 0: layisha i-build ethrekini lokuhlolwa okuvaliwe bese ushicilela inkululeko. Engeza abahloli nge-Google Group noma uhlu lwama-imeyili.
  2. Usuku 0 kuya ku-2: abahloli bamukela isimemo bese bejoyina ekhasini lokujoyina, base befaka uhlelo lokusebenza. Izinsuku eziyi-14 zomuntu ngamunye ziqala lapho ejoyina, ngakho abajoyina kancane badlulisela usuku lwakho ngemuva.
  3. Usuku 3 kuya ku-13: gcina bonke abangu-12 bejoyine futhi besebenzisa uhlelo lokusebenza. Hlola inani labajoyinile njalo ezinsukwini ezimbili noma ezintathu bese ubhekela ngokushesha noma ubani ophumile.
  4. Usuku 14 ngemva kokuba owokugcina kubahloli bakho abangu-12 ejoyine: bonke abangu-12 sebejoyine izinsuku eziyi-14 ngokulandelana. Lolu usuku lwakho lokuqala lokufaka isicelo.
  5. Usuku lokufaka isicelo: vula i-Dashboard, chofoza u-Apply for production bese ugcwalisa izingxenye ezintathu: About your closed test, About your app/game, kanye ne-About your production readiness. Fingqa impendulo yabahloli oyiqoqile nokuthi uyiqoqe kanjani.
  6. Ngemva kokufaka isicelo: I-Google ithi ukubuyekezwa kuvamise ukuthatha izinsuku eziyisikhombisa noma ngaphansi, kodwa ngezinye izikhathi kungathatha isikhathi eside. Uma i-Google inquma ukuthi uhlelo lokusebenza lidinga ukuhlolwa okwengeziwe, qhubeka nokuhlolwa okuvaliwe bese ulungisa isizathu.

Isibonelo esisebenzayo

Ushicilela ukuhlolwa okuvaliwe ngomhlaka-1 kuDisemba. Abahloli abayishumi bajoyina ngomhlaka-1 kuDisemba kanti abanye ababili bajoyina ngomhlaka-4 kuDisemba. Umuntu we-12 ufinyelela ezinsukwini eziyi-14 ngokulandelana ngomhlaka-18 kuDisemba, ngakho lolo lusuku lwakho lokuqala lokufaka isicelo, hhayi umhlaka-15 kuDisemba. Uma oyedwa kwabayishumi bokuqala ephuma ngomhlaka-10 kuDisemba futhi owangena esikhundleni sakhe ejoyina ngomhlaka-11 kuDisemba, owangena esikhundleni ufinyelela osukwini lwe-14 ngomhlaka-25 kuDisemba futhi usuku lwakho lushukela ku-25 kuDisemba, ngaphandle kokuba ube nabahloli abangaphezulu. Ngabahloli abangu-15, ukuphuma okufanayo akushintshi lutho.

Okudlulisela usuku ngemuva

  • Ukujoyina kancane. Umhloli omenyiwe kodwa angajoyini akanawo iwashi eliqhubekayo.
  • Ukuphuma bese ujoyina futhi. I-Google ithi izinsuku eziyi-14 kufanele zilandelane, ngakho umhloli ophumile futhi ajoyine qala kabusha.
  • Ukwehlela ngaphansi kwabangu-12. Abahloli abajoyinile, behlole izinsuku ezingaphansi kwe-14 base bephuma ababalwa.
  • Ukuhileleka okuphansi. I-Google ibala abahloli abangaphansi kwabangu-12 abajoyinile noma ukuhileleka kwabahloli okunganele njengezizathu zokuthi kungenzeka udinge ukuqhubeka nokuhlolwa okuvaliwe.
  • Ukufaka isicelo kusenesikhathi. Faka isicelo kuphela lapho abahloli abangu-12 ngamunye sebedlulile izinsuku eziyi-14 ngokulandelana.

Ungawenza kanjani usuku lube seduze ngangokunokwenzeka

  1. Qoqa abahloli abangu-15, hhayi abangu-12, ukuze ukuphuma kwomunye kungashukumisi usuku lwakho.
  2. Yenza bonke abahloli bajoyine ngosuku 0 noma ngosuku 1. Thumela isixhumanisi sokujoyina nesiqondiso esisodwa esicacile.
  3. Cela abahloli bavule uhlelo lokusebenza ezinsukwini eziningi futhi bazame isici esithile, ukuze ukuhileleka kubonakale njengokusetshenziswa kwangempela.
  4. Khipha isibuyekezo esincane ngesikhathi sokuhlolwa ukunika abahloli isizathu sokuvula uhlelo lokusebenza futhi.
  5. Lungisa izimpendulo zakho zefomu le-production ngenkathi ukuhlolwa kuqhubeka: okuhlolile, impendulo oyitholile nokushintshile.

Ukuthola abahloli abangu-12

Uma ungenabo abangani abanamafoni e-Android, ungathola abahloli ku-Tester Exchange. Abahloli bangabantu bangempela emafonini abo futhi usuku ngalunye abavula ngalo uhlelo lwakho lokusebenza luyabhalwa. Ungahlola izinhlelo zokusebenza zabanye abathuthukisi mahhala ku-https://testerexchange.com/earn kusiphequluli sefoni yakho, futhi uhlelo ngalunye lokusebenza oluhlolayo lilethela owakho umhloli, noma khokha kanye ngabahloli abangu-12, 15 noma 25. Abaphumile bayashintshwa ngokuzenzakalelayo, ngakho inani lakho lihlala ngaphezu kuka-12.

I-Google ithatha isikhathi esingakanani ukubuyekeza isicelo sokufinyelela i-production?

I-Google ithi ukubuyekezwa kuvamise ukuthatha izinsuku eziyisikhombisa noma ngaphansi, kodwa ngezinye izikhathi kungathatha isikhathi eside.

Ingabe lo mthetho usebenza ku-akhawunti yami?

Ikhasi losizo lwe-Google lisebenzisa lona kuma-akhawunti abathuthukisi omuntu ngamunye adalwe ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023.

Kuthiwani uma umhloli ephuma ngaphambi kosuku lwe-14?

Abahloli abajoyinile, behlole izinsuku ezingaphansi kwe-14 base bephuma ababalwa, ngakho wabeke abanye ngokushesha.
