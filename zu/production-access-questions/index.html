Ungayiphendula kanjani imibuzo ye-Google Play yokufinyelela ku-production

Kubuyekezwe ngo-Okthoba 2026

Uma ukuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe sekuphelile, i-Play Console ikucela ukuthi ufake isicelo se-production futhi ugcwalise uhlu olufushane lwemibuzo. Izimpendulo zakho, kanye nedatha yokuhlola i-Google engayibona, zinquma ukuthi uyakuthola yini ukufinyelela. Izimpendulo ezinhle ziqondile: izinombolo zangempela, impendulo yangempela nezinguquko zangempela.

Imibuzo mayelana nokuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe

  • Bekulula kangakanani ukuthola abahloli? Sho ukuthi bavelaphi (abangane, umphakathi wonjiniyela, ukushintshana kwabahloli) nokuthi bangaki abajoyine, abafakile nabahlalile.
  • Chaza ukuzibandakanya okuthole. Nikeza izinombolo: abahloli abasebenzayo ngosuku, inani lezinsuku-zabahloli, nokuthi ukuhlola kuthathe isikhathi esingakanani. Shono ukuthi ubagcine kanjani abahloli besebenza.
  • Fingqa impendulo. Shono izihloko ezimbili noma ezintathu eziqondile ("ukubhalisa bekudida", "iyaphahlazeka uma kujikwa isikrini") futhi usho ukuthi uyiqoqe kanjani.

Imibuzo mayelana ne-app yakho

  • Ngubani izethameli ezihlosiwe? Yiba ngqo: bangobani nokuthi banayiphi inkinga.
  • I-app yakho inikeza kanjani inzuzo? Umusho owodwa noma emibili mayelana nokuthi abasebenzisi bangenzani ababengakwazi ukukwenza ngaphambili.
  • Ulindele ukufakwa okungaki onyakeni wokuqala? Khetha ibanga eliqotho. Akekho olindele inombolo enkulu kunjiniyela omusha.

Imibuzo mayelana nokulungela i-production

  • Yiziphi izinguquko ozenzile ngokusekelwe ekuhlolweni? Bala ukulungiswa nokuthuthukiswa, futhi ushono ama-build owalayisha phakathi nokuhlola.
  • Unqume kanjani ukuthi i-app yakho isilungile? Sebenzisa imibandela: izindlela eziyinhloko ziyasebenza, ukuphahlazeka okubikiwe kulungisiwe, abahloli bayisebenzise isikhathi sonke.

Isibonelo sempendulo (ukuzibandakanya)

“Abahloli abangu-14 bafaka i-app ku-track evaliwe futhi bayisebenzisa izinsuku ezingu-15. Ngokwesilinganiso, abahloli abangu-12 babesebenza usuku ngalunye, izinsuku-zabahloli ezingu-186 sezizonke, cishe imizuzu emi-4 ngeseshini. Sathumela abahloli uhlu olufushane lwemisebenzi isonto ngalinye futhi sabacela ukuthi babike noma yini edidayo. Abahloli abangu-13 baqeda sonke isikhathi.”

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ngingaphendula ngolimi lwami?

Yebo, kodwa isiNgisi esicacile nesilula siphephe kakhulu, ngoba ababuyekezi bangase bangalufundi ulimi lwakho.

Izimpendulo zinde kangakanani?

Imisho emibili kuya kwemihlanu ngamunye yanele. Okuqondile kungcono kunokude.

Kuthiwani uma ngingenazo izinombolo?

Lapho mhlawumbe udinga ukuhlola okwengeziwe. Landelela abahloli abasebenzayo nsuku zonke nempendulo ngaphambi kokuthi ufake isicelo.
