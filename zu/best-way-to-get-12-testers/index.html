  • Ukushintshana kwamahhala (ukuhlola kwamahhala kwe-Tester Exchange, i-app ye-Testers Community): $0. Uhlola ama-app abanye onjiniyela futhi uzuze amakhredithi okuthola abahloli be-app yakho. Izindleko isikhathi sakho, cishe umzuzu owodwa ngosuku nge-app ngayinye. Isivinini sincike ekutheni bangaki onjiniyela abasebenzayo.
  • Isevisi ekhokhelwayo (i-Tester Exchange, uhlelo olukhokhelwayo lwe-Testers Community): I-Tester Exchange ishaja u-$12.99 ngabahloli abangu-12, u-$14.99 ngabangu-15 noma u-$22.99 ngabangu-25. I-Testers Community ibhala uhlelo lwayo olukhokhelwayo kusuka ku-$15. Abahloli baqala phakathi namahora ambalwa kuya osukwini olulodwa noma ezimbili.

Ukushintshana kwamahhala: kusebenza kanjani

Ekushintshaneni kwamahhala, unjiniyela ngamunye uphinde abe ngumhloli. I-Testers Community ichaza uhlelo lwamakhredithi lapho uhlola ama-app amathathu ukuze uthole amakhredithi, bese uwasebenzisa ukuthola abahloli be-app yakho, futhi ihlanganisa onjiniyela emaphaketheni asebenza ndawonye cishe amasonto amabili. Ku-Tester Exchange uvula ama-app abanye onjiniyela nsuku zonke futhi uzuze amakhredithi; onjiniyela abahlola abanye bathola abahloli be-app yabo ngokushesha.

Ukushintshana kwamahhala kusebenza kangcono uma uqala masisha, ngoba uthola abahloli ngokuhlola futhi izinsuku ezingu-14 ziqala kuphela lapho bonke abahloli bakho sebengene. Lindela ukuvula i-app ngayinye oyabelwe kanye ngosuku.

Ku-Tester Exchange, isinyathelo sansuku zonke siwukuthinta kanye nge-app ngayinye ku-browser yefoni yakho; akudingeki i-app eyengeziwe.

Amasevisi akhokhelwayo: yini okufanele uyihlole

Ukukhokha kuvunyelwe umthetho obaliwe we-Google, kodwa ukuhlola kusiza kuphela uma abahloli bengabantu bangempela futhi besala bengene (opted in). Ngaphambi kokukhokha, hlola ukuthi abahloli basebenzisa amafoni angempela, ukuthi abahloli abangenisiwe bangena esikhundleni salabo abashiyayo, nokuthi uthola ubufakazi bokusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke hhayi isethembiso kuphela.

Imigomo yokubuyisela imali iyahluka. I-Testers Community ithi ibuyisela imali uma i-Google ala i-app ngemva komjikelezo wayo. Ukuthenga ku-Tester Exchange kuyaphela: amakhredithi awaguquki abuyele emalini, futhi amakhredithi angasetshenzisiwe asala ebhalansini yakho.

I-Fiverr nezinye ama-gig e-freelance

Amakhulu abathengisi anikela ngabahloli abangu-12 noma abangu-20 izinsuku ezingu-14, ngokuvamile nosizo lwephepha lemibuzo le-Google Play. Buza umthengisi ukuthi basebenzisa amadivayisi angempela ahlukene amangaki nokuthi uthola yini ubufakazi bansuku zonke.

Yiliphi okufanele ulikhethe?

  1. Awunayo imali futhi kusele okungenani amasonto amathathu ngaphambi kosuku lwakho lokukhipha: joyina ukushintshana kwamahhala namuhla futhi uqhubeke uhlola ama-app amanye zonke izinsuku ezingu-14.
  2. Une-bhajethi ethile nesikhathi sokugcina: khokhela isevisi enobufakazi bansuku zonke futhi esingenisa abahloli esikhundleni salabo abashiyayo, futhi cela abahloli abangu-15 ukuze abambalwa abashiyayo bangaqali kabusha ukubala kwakho.
  3. Usunabangane nomndeni abasebenzisa i-Android: ubameme kuqala, bese ugcwalisa izihlalo ezisele ekushintshaneni noma ephaketheni elikhokhelwayo.
  4. Wake wanqatshwa ngo-"kudingeka ukuhlola okwengeziwe": lungisa inkinga yokubamba iqhaza kuqala. Bheka umhlahlandlela ngokubamba iqhaza kokuhlola okungenele.

Kufanele ucele abahloli abangaki?

Isilinganiso esiphansi se-Google singu-12, futhi umhloli ngamunye kufanele asale ngenile (opted in) izinsuku ezingu-14 ezilandelanayo. Cela abangu-15 noma ngaphezulu ukuze abambalwa abashiyayo bangakwehlisi ngaphansi kuka-12 osukwini lwe-10.
