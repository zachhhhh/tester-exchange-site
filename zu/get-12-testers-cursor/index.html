Wakhe uhlelo lwakho lwe-Android nge-Cursor? Thola abahloli bakho abangu-12 izinsuku eziyi-14

Kubuyekezwe ngoNovemba 2026

I-Cursor ingabhala futhi yakhe uhlelo lwakho lwe-Android, kodwa ayikwazi ukuhlinzeka abahloli abangu-12 bangempela abadingwa yi-Google Play. Uma i-akhawunti yakho yomthuthukisi yomuntu siqu yakhiwe ngemuva kwa-13 Novemba 2023, kufanele uqhube ukuhlolwa okuvaliwe nabahloli abangenani abangu-12 abajoyine izinsuku eziyi-14 ezilandelanayo ngaphambi kokucela ukukhiqizwa. Lo mhlahlandlela ubhekene nokwakha iphakheji, ukuqala ukuhlolwa okuvaliwe, ukuthola abahloli (mahhala kuqala, ngokukhokha kamuva) nokuxhuma i-Tester Exchange ku-Cursor ukuze umeleli enze imibhalo.

1. Akha iphakheji lokukhishwa (.aab)

  1. Cela umeleli we-Cursor asethe ukusayina kokukhishwa ku-build.gradle.kts yemojuli yakho (ibhlokhi ye-signingConfigs ene-storeFile, storePassword, keyAlias ne-keyPassword, esethwe ohlotsheni lwe-release build). Gcina amaphasiwedi ngaphandle kwe-git (imibhalo yokwakha ye-Android: https://developer.android.com/build/building-cmdline).
  2. Qhuba ./gradlew bundleRelease ku-terminal. I-Gradle yakha futhi isayine iphakheji; i-.aab ivame ukubonakala ku-app/build/outputs/bundle/release/, kodwa hlola umphumela wokwakha kwakho.
  3. Dala i-keystore nge-keytool uma ungenayo, bese uyigcina ikhophi. I-Google Play App Signing iyanikezwa lapho ulayisha ukukhishwa kwakho kokuqala.

2. Qala ukuhlolwa okuvaliwe ku-Play Console

  1. Vula i-Test and release > Testing > Closed testing, bese i-Create track (noma i-Manage track).
  2. Chofoza i-Create new release, layisha i-.aab, engeza igama nezinothi zokukhishwa, i-Save as draft, i-Next, bese i-Start rollout (izinyathelo ezivela ku-https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859348).
  3. Qala uphothule imisebenzi yokusetha uhlelo lokusebenza ku-Dashboard, uma kungenjalo inkinobho yokukhishwa ihlala ivaliwe.

3. Thola abahloli bakho abangu-12: mahhala kuqala, ngokukhokha kamuva

  • Indlela yamahhala: vula i-https://testerexchange.com/earn efonini lakho bese uhlola izinhlelo zokusebenza ozinikezwayo. Uhlelo lokusebenza ngalunye oluhlolayo lilethela owakho umhloli.
  • Indlela yokukhokha: khokha kanye futhi abahloli abelwa ngokuzenzakalelayo. Amanani wamanje abhalwe ku-https://testerexchange.com/llms.txt.
  • Cela abahloli abangu-15 esikhundleni sabangu-12 ukuze oyedwa ophumayo angakushiyi ngaphansi kobuncane be-Google.

4. Xhuma i-Tester Exchange ku-Cursor (kuyakhethwa)

Okokuqala dala ukhiye we-API ku-https://testerexchange.com/api. Bese ufaka lokhu ku-.cursor/mcp.json kuphrojekthi yakho, noma ku-~/.cursor/mcp.json ukuze usebenzise yonke indawo (izindawo zamafayela ezivela emibhalweni ye-MCP ye-Cursor, https://cursor.com/docs/context/mcp). Setha i-TESTER_EXCHANGE_KEY endaweni ye-shell yakho kuqala, ukuze ukhiye ungafakwa ku-commit:

{"mcpServers":{"tester-exchange":{"url":"https://vvrlxzbngxfxddywidoq.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp","headers":{"Authorization":"Bearer ${env:TESTER_EXCHANGE_KEY}"}}}}

I-Cursor ixazulula i-${env:NAME} emasimini e-url ne-headers. Uma isiphakeli singalayeki, vula iphaneli ye-Output bese ukhetha i-MCP Logs. Isiphakeli siyi-MCP ejwayelekile nge-Streamable HTTP ngaphandle kwe-OAuth; amathuluzi omphakathi (about, pricing, platform_stats) asebenza ngaphandle kokhiye.

5. Buza umeleli

  • “Sebenzisa i-Tester Exchange ukuthola abahloli abangu-15 be-com.example.app bese ungitshela engifanele ukukwenza ku-Play Console.”
  • “Hlola umkhankaso wami we-Tester Exchange we-com.example.app bese ungitshela ukuthi bangaki abahloli ababesebenza izolo.”
  • “Thola umbiko womkhankaso bese ubhala izimpendulo zami zokufinyelela ekukhiqizweni ezivela kunombolo zangempela.”

Ithuluzi eliyinhloko yi-get_testers: iyengeza uhlelo lokusebenza, ivule ukuhlolwa kwalo futhi ibuyise izinyathelo okungenziwa umuntu kuphela, njengokwengeza i-Google Group yabahloli ku-Play Console. Umeleli akakwazi ukukhokha noma ukuchofoza i-Apply for production esikhundleni sakho.

Awunayo i-MCP? Sebenzisa i-HTTP API

I-POST ku-https://vvrlxzbngxfxddywidoq.supabase.co/rest/v1/rpc/agent_call nenhloko ye-apikey yomphakathi ebhalwe ku-https://testerexchange.com/agents/ nomzimba {"p_token":"tx_YOUR_TOKEN","p_action":"get_testers","p_args":{"package_name":"com.example.app"}}. I-schema iku-https://testerexchange.com/openapi.json. Amanye amathuluzi: https://testerexchange.com/guide/ai-builders/.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe i-Cursor ingaqeda ukuhlolwa kwezinsuku eziyi-14 esikhundleni sami?

Cha. Izinsuku eziyi-14 zidinga abantu bangempela abasebenzisa uhlelo lwakho ezingcingweni zabo ze-Android. I-Cursor ingakha uhlelo lokusebenza iqhube nokusetha.

Ingabe i-Tester Exchange ukuhlanganiswa okusemthethweni kwe-Cursor?

Cha. Yisevisi ezimele esebenza nanoma iliphi iklayenti elisekela i-MCP engekho endaweni nge-HTTP, futhi i-Cursor iyakwenza.

I-AAB iya kuphi?

Yilayishe ekukhishweni kokuhlolwa okuvaliwe ku-Play Console, bese wengeza i-Google Group noma uhlu lwama-imeyili abahloli bakho ku-track leyo.

Ingabe kukhona indlela yamahhala yokuthola abahloli abangu-12?

Yebo. Hlola izinhlelo zokusebenza zabanye abathuthukisi ku-https://testerexchange.com/earn; uhlelo lokusebenza ngalunye oluhlolayo lilethela owakho umhloli.
