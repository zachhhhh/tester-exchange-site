Umthetho, ngamagama alula

Usizo lwe-Google Play Console luthi ama-akhawunti onjiniyela omuntu siqu avulwe ngemuva komhla ziyi-13 kuNovemba 2023 kufanele enze ukuhlola okuvaliwe nabahloli okungenani abangu-12 ababhalisile ngaphandle kokuyeka okungenani izinsuku ezingu-14 ngaphambi kokuthi bafake isicelo sokufinyelela ku-production. Umthetho uyafana kuwo wonke amazwe: ayikho inguqulo ehlukile ye-Afrika.

Izinsuku ezingu-14 zomhloli ngamunye ziqala lapho lowo muntu ebhalisa. Uma othile ehoxa, ukubala kwakhe kuqala phansi.

Kungani kunzima kubathuthukisi abaningi

  • Abantu obaziyo kungenzeka bangabi nabasebenzisi be-Android abangu-12 abazoqhubeka kuze kube sekugcineni. Abangane bavame ukufaka uhlelo kanye bese bekhohlwa, noma bahoxe ngephutha.
  • Abahloli kufanele babe ne-akhawunti ye-Google efonini ye-Android futhi bajoyine ukuhlola kwakho nge-Google Group noma uhlu lwama-imeyili olwengeza ku-Play Console.
  • Ukuzibandakanya kubalulekile. I-Google isho ukuzibandakanya okunganele kwabahloli njengesizathu sokuthi kungadingeka uqhubeke uhlola, ngakho-ke abantu abangu-12 abangalokothi bavule uhlelo bayingozi.
  • Ukukhokha phesheya kungaba nzima. Uma inkokhelo yekhadi lamazwe ngamazwe ingadluli, udinga enye indlela yokukhokha, noma indlela yamahhala.

Indlela yamahhala: hlola ezinye izinhlelo, uthole abahloli ngokubuyiselwa

  1. Efonini yakho ye-Android, vula i-https://testerexchange.com/earn esipheqululini bese ungena nge-Google. Akudingeki olunye uhlelo lokusebenza.
  2. Engeza uhlelo lwakho neqembu lokuhlola okuvaliwe. I-Tester Exchange ingaxhuma ku-Google Play futhi yengeze iqembu layo labahloli ku-track yakho, noma ulengeze ngesandla ku-Play Console ngaphansi kokuthi Closed testing, Testers.
  3. Nsuku zonke, thepha izinhlelo ezisohlwini lwakho bese uvula ngasinye. Kuthatha cishe umzuzu owodwa ngosuku.
  4. Uhlelo ngalunye oluhlolayo lukulethela umhloli oyedwa. Usuku ngalunye abahloli abavula ngalo uhlelo lwakho luyarekhodwa, futhi abayekayo bashintshwa ngokuzenzakalelayo.

Indlela ekhokhelwayo: khokha kanye, ungahloli lutho

Uma ungathandi ukuhlola ezinye izinhlelo, khokha kanye ku-https://testerexchange.com/ futhi izinhlelo ezikhokhelwe zithola abahloli kuqala. Amanani aboniswa ngohlobo lwemali yakho. Ungakhokha ngekhadi, i-Google Pay, i-Apple Pay noma i-PayPal, noma nge-USDT noma i-USDC ku-BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum noma Base ethunyelwa ngqo esikhwameni se-Tester Exchange ngaphandle kwemali yokusebenza. Okuthengiwe akubuyiselwa.

Ukufaka isicelo se-production

  1. Linda kuze kube yilapho abahloli abangu-12 ngamunye esedlule izinsuku ezingu-14 ezilandelanayo ebhalisile. Bala kusukela osukwini umhloli wakho we-12 ajoyina ngalo.
  2. Vula i-Dashboard ku-Play Console bese uchofoza ku-Apply for production.
  3. Phendula imibuzo emayelana nokuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe, uhlelo lwakho nokulungela kwakho i-production. Chaza impendulo oyiqoqile nalokho okushintshile.
  4. I-Google ithi ukubuyekezwa kuvame ukuthatha izinsuku eziyisikhombisa noma ngaphansi.

Amathiphu onga isikhathi

  • Hlose abahloli abangu-15, hhayi abangu-12, ukuze ukuhoxa komuntu oyedwa kungabuyiseli usuku lwakho emuva.
  • Khipha isibuyekezo esincane phakathi nalezo zinsuku ezingu-14 ukuze abahloli babe nesizathu sokuvula uhlelo futhi.
  • Uma uhlelo lwakho ludinga inombolo yocingo yasendaweni noma lusebenza ezweni elilodwa kuphela, kusho lokho kumanothi okuhlola wohlelo, ngaphandle kwalokho abahloli bakwezinye izindawo bangase bangadluli esikrinini sokungena.
  • Qhubeka nokuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe kuze kugunyazwe ukufinyelela ku-production.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe umthetho wabahloli abangu-12 uhlukile eNigeria noma eKenya?

Cha. I-Google isebenzisa umthetho ofanayo kuma-akhawunti onjiniyela omuntu siqu avulwe ngemuva komhla ziyi-13 kuNovemba 2023 kuwo wonke amazwe.

Ingabe abahloli kufanele bahlale ezweni lami?

Cha. Abahloli bangaba noma kuphi, inqobo nje uma ukuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe kutholakala ezweni labo futhi uhlelo lubasebenzela.

Ngingakwenza lokhu ngefoni kuphela?

Yebo. Indlela yamahhala isebenza esipheqululini sefoni yakho ye-Android ku-https://testerexchange.com/earn.

Ngingayithola imali ngokuhlola izinhlelo zokusebenza?

Ezinye izinhlelo ku-Tester Exchange zikhokhela abahloli abaqeda zonke izinsuku ezingu-14 ngemali. Ungakhipha imali uyise ku-PayPal, Binance Pay noma Payoneer.

Kuzothatha isikhathi esingakanani ngaphambi kokuthi ngishicilele?

Okungenani izinsuku ezingu-14 ngemuva kokuthi umhloli wakho we-12 ebhalise, kanye nokubuyekezwa kwe-Google, okuvame ukuthatha izinsuku eziyisikhombisa noma ngaphansi.
