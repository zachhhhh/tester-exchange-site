Yini i-Google ekudingayo ngempela

I-Play Console Help ye-Google ithi ama-akhawunti omuntu siqu adalwe ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023 kufanele enze ukuhlola okuvaliwe okunabahloli abangu-12 okungenani abahlale benze i-opt-in ngokuqhubekayo okungenani izinsuku ezingu-14. Uma uhlangabezana nalokhu, ungafaka isicelo sokufinyelela ku-production ku-Dashboard bese uphendula imibuzo ngeapp yakho, ukuhlola kwakho nokulungela kwakho.

Akukho okusemthethweni mayelana nokuthi wabathola kanjani abahloli. Okubalulekile ukuthi ama-opt-in ayiqiniso futhi aqhubekayo, nokuthi izimpendulo zakho zichaza ukuhlola owake wakwenza ngempela.

Izinto eziyisikhombisa ongazihlola ngaphambi kokuthenga

  1. Abantu bangempela emafonini angempela. Buza ukuthi umhloli ngamunye usebenzisa idivayisi yakhe ye-Android yini. Ama-emulator namadivayisi abelwanayo enza ukuhlola okubuthakathaka nempendulo ebuthakathaka ye-production.
  2. Ngaphezu kuka-12. Izinsuku ezingu-14 zibalwa kuphela ngenkathi okungenani abahloli abangu-12 behlala benze i-opt-in. Isevisi ekunika abangu-12 ncamashi ayishiyi ikamelo lomuntu oyedwa ohambayo. Cela abangu-14 noma abangu-15.
  3. I-opt-in eqhubekayo. Umthetho we-Google uthi ngokuqhubekayo. Buza ukuthi isevisi ibagcina kanjani abahloli benze i-opt-in isikhathi sonke.
  4. Ubufakazi bokusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke. Kufanele ukwazi ukubona, usuku ngosuku, ukuthi i-app yavulwa. Uma ungakwazi ukukubona, ungakwazi ukukuchaza emibuzweni ye-production.
  5. Abangena esikhundleni salabo abahambayo. Buza ukuthi kwenzekani uma umhloli ehamba ngosuku lwe-6. Impendulo enhle ngumuntu ongena esikhundleni, hhayi ukuthula.
  6. I-akhawunti yakho, ukulawula kwakho. Wengeza abahloli nge-Google Group yakho noma uhlu lwama-imeyili. Ungalokothi wabelane ngokungena kwakho ku-Play Console.
  7. Amagama aqotho. Akekho ongathembisa isinqumo se-Google. Qaphela izinkulumo-themba zokufinyelela ku-production.

Ngemva kokukhokha: gcina ukuhlola kuthembekile

Vula i-app wena, funda impendulo ethunyelwa abahloli, futhi ukhiphe okungenani isibuyekezo esisodwa esilungisa okuthile abakubikile. Imibuzo ye-production ibuza ukuthi abahloli bakutshele ini nokuthi ushintshe ini, ngakho izimpendulo zangempela ziyalula ukubhala uma ukuhlola bekuyiqiniso.

Indlela i-Tester Exchange esebenza ngayo

  • Abahloli bangempela: abantu abanamafoni abo e-Android bafaka i-app yakho ekuhlolweni kwakho okuvaliwe.
  • Ukusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke kuyarekhodwa: usuku ngalunye umhloli avula ngalo i-app yakho luyalogwa, futhi izihlalo zabahloli abayekayo zinikezwa abahloli abasha.
  • Umbiko wansuku zonke: bona ukuthi bangaki abahloli abebesebenza, kanye nempendulo yabo.

I-Google yenza isinqumo sokugcina sokufinyelela ku-production. Lokhu kukunikeza ukuhlola okubonisa ukusetshenziswa kwangempela nedatha yokukuchaza.
