Ijeneretha yezimpendulo zokufinyelela ku-production

Ifomu elithi "Faka isicelo sokufinyelela ku-production" ku-Play Console linezingxenye ezintathu: ukuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe, uhlelo lwakho, nokulungela i-production. Faka okwenzeke ekuhloleni kwakho ukuze uthole okusalungiswa kombuzo ngamunye obhalwayo, kanye nesiqondiso semibuzo emibili lapho ukhetha khona inketho. Okusalungiswa kusebenzisa kuphela lokho okufakayo; noma yini oyishiya ingenalutho iba [ubakaki] ozowugcwalisa. Konke kusebenza ku-browser yakho futhi akukho okuthunyelwayo. Okusalungiswa kungesiNgisi, abahloli abasifunda yonke indawo.

Izimpendulo zakho ezisalungiswa

Hlela lezi zimpendulo ukuze zifane ncamashi nalokho okwenzekile. I-Google ingayibona idatha yakho yangempela yokuhlola, ngakho izimpendulo eziqotho neziqondile zisebenza kangcono.

Ingxenye 1: Mayelana nokuhlolwa kwakho okuvaliwe

Bekulula kangakanani ukuthola abahloli?

Lapha i-Play Console icela ukuthi ukhethe inketho, hhayi ukubhala. Khetha eyiqiniso.

Ukubamba iqhaza kwabahloli ngesikhathi sokuhlolwa okuvaliwe

I-Google ifuna ukwazi ukuthi abahloli bazisebenzisile yini zonke izici, nokuthi ukusebenzisa kwabo kuyafana yini nendlela abasebenzisi bangempela abazolusebenzisa ngayo uhlelo.

Isifinyezo sempendulo yabahloli nokuthi uyiqoqe kanjani

Bala izihloko ezinkulu abahloli abaziphakamisile, bese usho ukuthi uyiqoqe kanjani impendulo.

Ingxenye 2: Mayelana nohlelo noma igeyimu yakho

Izilaleli eziqondiwe

Cacisa: kungubani, kuphi, futhi uhlelo lufeza siphi isidingo. Le mpendulo ayiboniswa emphakathini.

Inzuzo enikezwa uhlelo lwakho (kumageyimu: okwenza lihluke)

Yisho ukuthi abasebenzisi bangenzani ngohlelo nokuthi kungani lubasiza.

Inani elilinganiselwe lokufakwa onyakeni wokuqala

Lapha i-Play Console icela ukuthi ukhethe ibanga. Ukulinganisa okuthembekile nokulinganiselwe kwanele.

Ingxenye 3: Mayelana nokulungela i-production

Izinguquko ozenzile ngenxa yokuhlolwa okuvaliwe

Bhala ukulungiswa kwangempela, bese usho ama-build amasha owalayishe ku-track evaliwe.

Unqume kanjani ukuthi uhlelo selulungile

Yisho ukuthi yini oyihlolile ngaphambi kokufaka isicelo: amaphutha alungisiwe, akukho ukuphahlazeka, izinqubo eziyinhloko ziyasebenza.

Ngaphambi kokufaka isicelo

  • I-Google ibala abahloli abahlala bebhalisile ekuhlolweni kwakho okuvaliwe izinsuku ezingu-14 ngokulandelana; ophumayo abuye ajoyine uqala kabusha. I-Dashboard ye-Play Console ikhombisa ukuthi ungafaka nini isicelo.
  • Qala ngokulungisa ukuphahlazeka, izici ezonakele nezikrini ezingekho: i-Google ithi ukubuyekeza akusona isinyathelo sokuthola amaphutha.
  • Uma uhlelo ludinga ukungena, engeza imininingwane yokungena yokuhlola esebenzayo ku-Play Console ukuze ababuyekezi bavule zonke izici.
  • Hlola ukuthi iqembu leminyaka eliqondiwe nohlu lwesitolo kuhambisana nokuqukethwe kwangempela kohlelo.
  • Ukubuyekeza kuvame ukuthatha izinsuku ezingu-7 noma ngaphansi. Uma i-Google icela ukuhlolwa okwengeziwe, qhubeka nokuhlolwa okuvaliwe bese uphinda ufaka isicelo.
