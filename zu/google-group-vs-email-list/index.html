I-Google Group noma uhlu lwama-imeyili ekuhlolweni kwakho okuvaliwe?

Kubuyekezwe ngo-Okthoba 2026

Yonke i-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe idinga uhlu lwalabo abangafaka i-app. I-Play Console ikunika izinketho ezimbili: uhlu lwama-imeyili oluphatha ku-Play Console, noma i-Google Group. Zombili ziyasebenza esidingweni sabahloli abangu-12. Umehluko ngukuthi kunomsebenzi ongakanani ukwengeza abantu.

Ukuqhathanisa okusheshayo

  • I-Google Group: noma ubani onesixhumanisi seqembu angajoyina, ngakho akudingeki wengeze abantu ngamunye ngamunye. Kungcono kakhulu uma abahloli bevela ezindaweni eziningi.
  • Uhlu lwama-imeyili: unamathisela i-imeyili ye-akhawunti ye-Google yomhloli ngamunye ku-Play Console. Ulawula ngokuphelele ukuthi ngubani ongenayo, kodwa kumele uqhubeke ulubuyekeza njengoba abantu bejoyina.

Setha i-Google Group

  1. Iya ku-groups.google.com bese udala iqembu. Setha u-"Who can join" kunoma ubani ewebhu, ukuze abahloli bangalindi ukugunyazwa.
  2. Ku-Play Console → Testing → Closed testing → i-track yakho → Testers, khetha i-Google Groups bese wengeza ikheli le-imeyili leqembu.
  3. Yabelana ngezixhumanisi ezimbili nabahloli: isixhumanisi seqembu (bajoyina kuqala) nesixhumanisi se-opt-in (baba ngabahloli).
  4. Abahloli bafaka i-app ku-Google Play nge-akhawunti ye-Google efanayo abayisebenzise ukujoyina iqembu.

Setha uhlu lwama-imeyili

  1. Ku-Play Console → Testing → Closed testing → i-track yakho → Testers, dala uhlu lwama-imeyili.
  2. Namathisela ama-imeyili ama-akhawunti e-Google abahloli bakho, ehlukaniswe ngokhefana, bese ulondoloza.
  3. Yabelana ngesixhumanisi se-opt-in. Abahloli kumele basivule bengene nge-imeyili oyingezile.

Amaphutha avimba abahloli ukuthi bayibone i-app yakho

  • Umhloli ujoyine nge-akhawunti eyodwa ye-Google kodwa i-Play Store yefoni yakhe isebenzisa enye.
  • Iqembu lidinga ukugunyazwa, ngakho abahloli banamathela belindile.
  • Umhloli ujoyine iqembu kodwa akazange avule isixhumanisi se-opt-in futhi athephe ku-"Become a tester".
  • Ukukhishwa akukagunyazwa. Abahloli abakwazi ukufaka kuze kube yilapho i-track evaliwe isebenza.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe amalungu eqembu abalwa kwabahloli abangu-12?

Kubalwa kuphela abantu abenze i-opt-in ngesixhumanisi se-opt-in, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi usebenzisa luphi uhlobo lohlu.

Ngingasebenzisa kokubili?

Yebo. I-track ingaba nohlu lwama-imeyili nama-Google Group ngesikhathi esisodwa.

Ingabe abahloli bangahlala bengaziwa?

Nge-Google Group awudingi neze ukuqoqa ama-imeyili ngokwakho. Ngohlu lwama-imeyili udinga i-imeyili ye-akhawunti ye-Google yomhloli ngamunye.
