Ezimweni eziningi i-track yakho yokuhlola okuvaliwe, abahloli bakho ne-app yakho kuhlala kunjalo, futhi i-Google ifuna nje ukubona ukuhlola okwengeziwe noma okungcono ngaphambi kokuthi i-app iye emphakathini. Ukuthi ukwenqatshwa kusho ini

iSikhungo Sosizo se-Google sithi kungenzeka udinge ukuqhubeka nokuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe ngaphambi kokuthola ukufinyelela ku-production. Izizathu eziyibalayo zihlanganisa ukuba nabahloli abangaphansi kuka-12 abenze i-opt-in nabahloli abangabambi iqhaza ngokwanele nge-app yakho ngesikhathi sokuhlola.

Ngakho ukwenqatshwa ngokuvamile akuyona isihlambululo senqubomgomo ku-akhawunti yakho. Kungu-Google ethi ubufakazi bokuhlola kwakho abukakholisi okwamanje. Funda i-imeyili nomlayezo ku-Play Console ngokucophelela: ngokuvamile ikhomba ukuhlola, ukubamba iqhaza noma izimpendulo ozinikezile.

  • "More testing required" noma okufanayo: qhubeka nokuhlola okuvaliwe bese uqoqa ukusetshenziswa kwangempela okwengeziwe.
  • Abahloli abangaphansi kuka-12 abenze i-opt-in: abanye abahloli bahambile. Thola abaningi ngaphambi kokuphinda ufake isicelo.
  • Izinkinga zenqubomgomo noma zokuqukethwe: into ehlukile. Hlola ikhasi le-Policy status bese uzilungisa kuqala, noma isicelo esisha ngeke sisize.

Kuqala, ungakwenzi lokhu

  • Ungasusi i-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe noma ususe abahloli abasasebenza. Uyabadinga.
  • Ungadali i-app entsha enegama le-package elisha ukuze "uqale kabusha". Kuzodingeka wenze ukuhlola okuvaliwe okuphelele okusha noma kunjalo.
  • Ungaphindi ufake isicelo ngakusasa ngezimpendulo ezifanayo. Akukho okushintshile, ngakho lindela umphumela ofanayo.
  • Ungagcwalisi izihlalo ngamafoni akho angasetshenzisiwe noma ama-akhawunti angamanga. I-Google ingabona uma ama-akhawunti amaningi abelana ngedivayisi eyodwa.

Thola indawo ebuthaka

Vula i-track yakho yokuhlola okuvaliwe ku-Play Console nezibalo zesikhathi sokuhlola. Zibuze ngokweqiniso:

  • Bangaki abahloli abasenze i-opt-in ngosuku lapho ufaka isicelo? Ingabe babengaphezu kuka-12 ngokukhululekile?
  • Bangaki kubo abavule i-app ezinsukwini eziningi, hhayi ngosuku lokuqala kuphela?
  • Ingabe ulayishe okungenani i-build eyodwa entsha ngesikhathi sokuhlola?
  • Ingabe ungatsho impendulo yangempela evela kubahloli futhi uqambe ukuthi ushintshe ini ngenxa yayo?
  • Ingabe izimpendulo zakho efomini ziqukethe izinombolo, noma bekuyisigaba esisodwa noma ezimbili zombhalo ojwayelekile?

Izicelo eziningi ezenqatshiwe zehluleka ekubambeni iqhaza nasezimpendulweni ezingacacile. Kokubili kungalungiswa emasontweni amabili.

Uhlelo lwesicelo esilandelayo

  1. Lungisa kuqala noma yiziphi izinkinga zenqubomgomo, uma i-imeyili izishilo.
  2. Hlola inani lakho labahloli. Thola abaningi uze ube nabahloli bangempela abangu-15 noma ngaphezulu abenze i-opt-in, ukuze abahambayo bangakwehlisi ngaphansi kuka-12.
  3. Nikeza abahloli imisebenzi eqondile iviki ngalinye, njengokuthi "bhalisa, dala into yakho yokuqala, zama isikrini sezilungiselelo". Izicelo ezingacacile zikhiqiza ukusetshenziswa okungacacile.
  4. Qoqa impendulo ebhaliwe ngefomu elilula noma ikheli le-imeyili, futhi ugcine irekhodi.
  5. Lungisa izinkinga ezimbalwa ezibikiwe bese ulayisha ama-build amasha ku-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe ngesikhathi sokuhlola.
  6. Qhuba ukuhlola okungenani ezinye izinsuku ezingu-14 ngokusetshenziswa kwangempela kwansuku zonke. I-Google ayishicileli isikhathi esimisiwe sokulinda, ngakho vumela ubufakazi bukhule kunokuba ubuke ikhalenda.
  7. Bhala kabusha izimpendulo zakho ngezinombolo: abahloli abenze i-opt-in, bangaki abasebenzise i-app njalo, izinsuku zabahloli, amabhugi atholiwe nalungisiwe, nama-build akhishiwe.
  8. Phinda ufake isicelo ku-Dashboard. I-Google ithi ukubuyekezwa ngokuvamile kuthatha izinsuku eziyisikhombisa noma ngaphansi.

Bhala izimpendulo ezingcono

Ifomu le-Google libuza ukuthi kwakulula kangakanani ukuthola abahloli, abahloli bayibambe kanjani iqhaza, uthole mpendulo ini nokuthi uyiqoqe kanjani, ukuthi i-app ingeyabani, inani layo, ukufakwa okulindelekile, oshintshe ini ngemva kokuhlola nokuthi wazi kanjani ukuthi ilungile.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe kufanele ngiqale izinsuku ezingu-14 kusuka ekuqaleni?

I-Google ithi kungenzeka udinge ukuqhubeka nokuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe. Gcina i-track nabahloli abakhona, bese uhlela okungenani amanye amasonto amabili okusetshenziswa kwangempela ngaphambi kokuphinda ufake isicelo.

Kufanele ngilinde isikhathi esingakanani ngaphambi kokuphinda ngifake isicelo?

iSikhungo Sosizo se-Google asishoyo isikhathi esimisiwe sokulinda. Phinda ufake isicelo lapho unokuhlola okuningi nokungcono ongakubonisa ngokucacile.

Ingabe ukwenqatshwa kuzolimaza i-akhawunti yami yonjiniyela?

Isinqumo sokufinyelela ku-production mayelana nokuhlola asifani nokwephula inqubomgomo. Izinkinga zenqubomgomo ziboniswa ngokuhlukile ekhasini le-Policy status.

Ingabe ngingakhokha isikhalo esikhundleni salokho?

Ngesinqumo sokuhlola, indlela esebenzayo ukuhlola okwengeziwe nesicelo esiqinile. Ngokulandelwa kwenqubomgomo, landela isixhumanisi sesikhalo kuleyo i-imeyili ethile.

Ingabe kufanele ngishintshele ku-akhawunti yenhlangano?

Kuphela uma uyibhizinisi langempela elinenombolo ye-D-U-N-S. Ama-akhawunti enhlangano awafakwanga esidingweni sokuhlola okuvaliwe, kodwa ukuguqula kudinga ukuqinisekiswa nesikhathi. Bheka umhlahlandlela wama-akhawunti enhlangano nawomuntu siqu.
