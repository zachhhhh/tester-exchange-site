Izidingo zokuhlola okuvaliwe kwe-Google Play: umhlahlandlela ophelele we-2026

Kubuyekezwe ngo-Novemba 2026

Uma uvule i-akhawunti yonjiniyela we-Google Play yomuntu siqu ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023, awukwazi ukushicilela ngqo ku-production. I-Google kuqala ifuna ukuhlola okuvaliwe okunabahloli abangu-12 okungenani abahlala benze i-opt-in okungenani izinsuku ezingu-14 ngokulandelana. Ngemva kwalokho ugcwalisa ifomu elifushane lokufinyelela ku-production bese ulinda ukubuyekezwa yi-Google. Lo mhlahlandlela uchaza yonke ingxenye yomthetho njengoba i-Google iwushilo eSikhungweni Sosizo se-Play Console, okukwenza onjiniyela bakhubeke ngempela, nesikhathi esiphusile kusukela ekulayisheni kokuqala kuze kube ukukhishwa komphakathi.

Ubani okufanele enze ukuhlola okuvaliwe

iSikhungo Sosizo se-Google sithi isidingo sisebenza kuma-akhawunti onjiniyela bomuntu siqu adalwe ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023. Kuze kudlule lawa ma-app kuso, awafaneleki ukusakazwa ku-Google Play.

Uma i-akhawunti yakho yadalwa ngaphambi kwalolo suku, noma iyi-akhawunti yenhlangano (ibhizinisi eliqinisekisiwe ngenombolo ye-D-U-N-S), isidingo sokuhlola okuvaliwe asikusebenzeli. I-app ngayinye ku-akhawunti yomuntu siqu ephethwe yilokhu idlula kule nqubo ngaphambi kokuthi ithole ukufinyelela ku-production.

  • I-akhawunti yomuntu siqu, eyadalwa ngemva kuka-13 Nov 2023: ukuhlola okuvaliwe kuyadingeka.
  • I-akhawunti yomuntu siqu, eyadalwa ngaphambi kuka-13 Nov 2023: akudingeki.
  • I-akhawunti yenhlangano: akudingeki, kodwa udinga inombolo ye-D-U-N-S newebhusayithi yenhlangano eqinisekisiwe. Bheka umhlahlandlela wama-akhawunti enhlangano nawomuntu siqu.

Umthetho ngomusho owodwa

Enza ukuhlola okuvaliwe nge-okungenani abahloli abangu-12 abahlale benze i-opt-in ngokuqhubekayo okungenani izinsuku ezingu-14, bese ufaka isicelo sokufinyelela ku-production ku-Play Console Dashboard.

Amagama e-Google abalulekile. Izinsuku ezingu-14 kufanele zilandelane. Abahloli abenza i-opt-in, bahlola ngaphansi kwezinsuku ezingu-14 bese benza i-opt-out abababalwa. Futhi okungenani abahloli abangu-12 kufanele babe basenze i-opt-in ngosuku olufaka ngalo isicelo.

Inombolo yayiyi-20 yabahloli kwaze kwaba nguDisemba 2024, lapho i-Google iyehlisela ku-12. Izinsuku ezingu-14 azizange zishintshe.

Yini ebalwa njengomhloli

  • I-Google Account yangempela eyenze i-opt-in. Ukuba ohlwini lwakho lwama-imeyili noma ku-Google Group yakho akwanele. Umuntu ngamunye kufanele avule isixhumanisi se-opt-in athephe ukuze abe ngumhloli.
  • Ku-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe. Abantu abenze i-opt-in ekuhlolweni kwakho kwangaphakathi abafanelekile ekuhlolweni okuvaliwe baze bakushiye ukuhlola kwangaphakathi. Ukuhlola kwangaphakathi akubalwa esidingweni.
  • Benze i-opt-in ngaso sonke isikhathi. Uma umuntu eshiya bese ebuya, ukubalwa kwakhe okulandelanayo kuqala kabusha.
  • Besebenzisa i-app. I-Google ayishicileli inombolo ephansi yokuvula, kodwa ifomu le-production libuza ukuthi abahloli bayibambe kanjani iqhaza nokuthi ukusetshenziswa kuhambisane yini nalokho okulindele ku-production. Abahloli abafaka kanye bese bengabonakali yibo isizathu esiyinhloko sokwehluleka kwezicelo.

Isikhathi esiphusile

  1. Usuku 0: dala i-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe, engeza abahloli (uhlu lwama-imeyili noma i-Google Group), layisha i-app bundle bese uthumela ukukhishwa ukuze kubuyekezwe.
  2. Amahora kuya ezinsukwini ezimbalwa kamuva: ukukhishwa kushicilelwa futhi isixhumanisi se-opt-in siyasebenza. I-Google ithi isixhumanisi sokuqala sokuhlola singathatha amahora amaningana ukuze sitholakale.
  3. Izinsuku 1 kuya ku-14: okungenani abahloli abangu-12 bahlala benze i-opt-in futhi basebenzise i-app. Khipha okungenani isibuyekezo esisodwa ngokususelwe empendulweni yabo.
  4. Usuku lwe-15 noma kamuva: chofoza i-Apply for production ku-Dashboard bese uphendula imibuzo.
  5. Cishe ngemva kweviki: i-Google ithi ukubuyekezwa ngokuvamile kuthatha izinsuku eziyisikhombisa noma ngaphansi, kodwa kwesinye isikhathi kungathatha isikhathi eside. Uthola i-imeyili uma kuphelile.
  6. Ngemva kokugunyazwa: ikhasi le-Production liyavuleka. Ukukhishwa kwakho kokuqala kwe-production kusadlula ekubuyekezweni okujwayelekile kwe-app.

Hlela cishe amasonto amathathu kuya kwamane kusukela ekulayisheni kwakho kokuqala kokuhlola okuvaliwe kuze kube uhlu lomphakathi, uma kungekho okuphambuka.

Ifomu lokufinyelela ku-production

Ifomu le-Google linezingxenye ezintathu. Engxenyeni yokuqala ngokuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe: ukuthi kwakulula kangakanani ukuthola abahloli, ukuthi bayibambe kanjani iqhaza, nesifinyezo sempendulo yabo nokuthi uyiqoqe kanjani. Engxenyeni yesibili ngokuphathelene ne-app yakho: izethameli ozihlosile, inani ayinikeza abasebenzisi, nokuthi ulindele ukufakwa kangaki ngonyaka wokuqala. Engxenyeni yesithathu ngokulungela kwe-production: ushintshe ini ngemva kokuhlola nokuthi unqume kanjani ukuthi i-app ilungile.

Bhala izimpendulo ngaphambi kokuvula ifomu. I-Google iyaxwayisa ukuthi izimpendulo zakho zilahleka uma uphuma ekhasini ngaphambi kokuchofoza i-Next noma i-Apply. Umhlahlandlela wethu wemibuzo yokufinyelela ku-production nethuluzi lamahhala lezimpendulo ku-https://testerexchange.com/tools/production-answers/ kukusiza ukuzibhala ngezinombolo zangempela.

Kungani ama-app engadluli

iSikhungo Sosizo se-Google sibala izizathu ezimbili ongaqhubeka ngazo ukuhlola okuvaliwe: abahloli abangaphansi kuka-12 abenze i-opt-in, noma ukungabi nokubamba iqhaza okwanele kwabahloli. Empeleni onjiniyela bahlangana nalezi zinkinga:

  • Ukucela abantu abangu-12 ncamashi. Oyedwa ohambayo ngosuku lwe-10 futhi usuku lwakho lwehlela ku-11.
  • Abahloli abafaka i-app bese bengaphindi bayivule.
  • Ama-akhawunti amaningi efonini eyodwa, noma ama-akhawunti angebona abantu bangempela.
  • Ukungabi nezibuyekezo noma impendulo phakathi nezinsuku ezingu-14, ngakho akukho okuchazwa efomini.
  • Izimpendulo ezimfushane, ezijwayelekile embuzweni.

Uma usuthole impendulo ethi "more testing required", funda umhlahlandlela ngokubamba iqhaza kokuhlola okungenele nomhlahlandlela wokwenza uma ukufinyelela ku-production kwenqatshiwe.

Ungabathola kuphi abahloli abangu-12

I-Google ayisho ukuthi abahloli kufanele bavela kuphi. Abangane nomndeni bayasebenza uma wazi abasebenzisi be-Android abanele abazovula i-app yakho nsuku zonke amasonto amabili. Imiphakathi yokuhlolelana imahhala kodwa idinga isikhathi sakho. Amasevisi akhokhelwayo asheshe.

I-Tester Exchange yenza kokubili. Khokha kanye ($12.99 ngabahloli abangu-12, $14.99 ngabangu-15, $22.99 ngabangu-25), noma uthole abahloli mahhala ngokuhlola ama-app abanye onjiniyela ku-https://testerexchange.com/earn ku-browser yefoni yakho ye-Android. Umhloli ngamunye ungumuntu wangempela efonini yakhe, futhi ubona ukusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke kwabo bonke. Sincoma abahloli abangu-15 ukuze oyedwa noma ababili abahambayo bangakwehlisi ngaphansi kuka-12.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Kungabahloli abangu-12 noma abangu-20?

Abangu-12. I-Google yawehlisa kusuka ku-20 yaya ku-12 ngoDisemba 2024. Izinsuku ezingu-14 ezilandelanayo azizange zishintshe.

Ingabe isidingo sisebenza ku-akhawunti yami endala?

Ama-akhawunti omuntu siqu adalwe ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023 kuphela okufanele enze ukuhlola okuvaliwe. Ama-akhawunti omuntu siqu amadala nama-akhawunti enhlangano awafakwanga kuso.

Ingabe kudingeka ngiphinde ukuhlola okuvaliwe ku-app ngayinye?

Ukufinyelela ku-production kunikezwa nge-app ngayinye kuma-akhawunti omuntu siqu athintekayo, ngakho hlela ukuhlola okuvaliwe ku-app ngayinye entsha.

Ingabe ukuhlola kwangaphakathi kuyabalwa?

Cha. Kufanele kube ukuhlola okuvaliwe. I-Google futhi ithi abantu abenze i-opt-in ekuhlolweni kwakho kwangaphakathi abakwazi ukujoyina ukuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe baze bakushiye lokho kwangaphakathi.

Ingabe abahloli kufanele basebenzise i-app nsuku zonke?

I-Google ayishicileli inani eliphansi. Ibuza ngokubamba iqhaza efomini le-production, ngakho ukusetshenziswa kwangempela njalo ngabahloli abaningi kuyindlela ephephile kakhulu.

I-Google ithatha isikhathi esingakanani ukubuyekeza ukufinyelela ku-production?

I-Google ithi ngokuvamile izinsuku eziyisikhombisa noma ngaphansi, kodwa kwesinye isikhathi kungathatha isikhathi eside.
