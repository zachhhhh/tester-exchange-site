Izinketho zakho nezinzuzo nobubi bazo

Yonke inketho engezansi ingakunikeza amagama angu-12 ohlwini. Umbuzo wangempela ngukuthi labo bantu abangu-12 bazobe besayivula yini i-app yakho ngosuku lwe-10, ngoba i-Google ibheka ukuthi i-app isetshenziswe kanjani, hhayi nje ukuthi ngubani owenze i-opt-in.

  • Abangane nomndeni: mahhala futhi bathembekile, kodwa bambalwa abantu abanabangane abangu-12 abanamafoni e-Android abazovula i-app engakaqedwa nsuku zonke amasonto amabili. Lindela ukukhumbuza okuningi.
  • Imiphakathi yonjiniyela nokushintshana kokuhlolelana: mahhala, futhi abanye onjiniyela bayawuqonda umthetho. Inkinga wukuhlela: nawe uhlola ama-app abo, futhi ukushintshana okungahleliwe kuvame ukuphela ngemuva kwezinsuku ezimbalwa kungekho muntu olandelelayo.
  • Izinsizakalo zabahloli ezikhokhelwayo: kuyashesha, ngokuvamile cishe u-US$10–35 nge-app ngayinye. Ingozi ngabahloli abenza i-opt-in kodwa bangayivuli ngempela i-app, okuyikho kanye i-Google ekuphawulayo. Buza ukuthi ukusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke kuhlolwa kanjani ngaphambi kokuthi ukhokhe.
  • Imisebenzi ye-freelance eshibhile: inani eliphansi kakhulu, ingozi enkulu kakhulu. Ama-akhawunti mbumbulu noma angasebenzi kanye nama-akhawunti amaningi edivayisini eyodwa kuvamile, futhi i-Google ihlola ukuzibandakanya, ngakho laba bahloli bangase bangabalwa nhlobo.

I-Tester Exchange: ukushintshana kwamahhala okugcina wonke umuntu ethembekile

I-Tester Exchange iguqula ukushintshana kokuhlolelana kube yinto ongathembela kuyo. Ungasebenzisa indlela eyodwa, noma zombili ngesikhathi esisodwa.

  • Letha abahloli bakho: yabelana ngesixhumanisi se-app yakho nabangane nomndeni. Bafaka i-app ye-Tester Exchange yamahhala, ekuhlolela usuku ngalunye lwezinsuku zabo ezingu-14, ngakho akudingeki ujahe muntu.
  • Ukushintshana kokuhlola kwamahhala: xhuma ifoni yakho bese i-Tester Exchange ikunika ama-app abanye onjiniyela okuwahlola, umzuzu ngosuku. Yonke i-app oyihlolayo iletha umhloli ku-app yakho, futhi abangane obamemayo nabo bagcwalisa izihlalo.
  • Yeqa ukuhlola: thenga iphakethe lamakhredithi esikhundleni salokho. Ama-app akhokhelwe athola abahloli ngaphambi kwabanye bonke.
  • Ukusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke okuqinisekisiwe: i-app ye-Android ye-Tester Exchange ihlola efonini yangempela yomhloli ngamunye ukuthi i-app yakho ivulwe nsuku zonke, ngakho uyazi ukuthi ngubani ohlola ngempela.

Uhlu lokuhlola: yini eyenza abahloli babalwe

  • Abantu bangempela: umuntu oyedwa nge-akhawunti ngayinye, hhayi ama-akhawunti engeziwe owazidalela wona.
  • Amafoni abo: umhloli ngamunye usebenzisa idivayisi yakhe ye-Android, hhayi ifoni eyodwa esetshenziswa ama-akhawunti amaningi.
  • Ukusetshenziswa okuvamile izinsuku zonke ezingu-14: abahloli bavula i-app njalo kusukela osukwini 1 kuya osukwini 14, hhayi kanye nje ngemuva kokuyifaka.
  • Impendulo: abahloli bakutshela ukuthi yini ephukile noma ebadidile, futhi ukhipha ukulungiswa phakathi nokuhlola.

Akukho kulokhu okuqinisekisa ukufinyelela ku-production. I-Google yenza isinqumo sokugcina, kodwa ukuhlola okuhlangabezana nakho konke lokhu kukunika icala elinamandla kakhulu.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ngingabathola ngempela abahloli abangu-12 mahhala?

Yebo. Abangane, imiphakathi yonjiniyela nokushintshana kokuhlola akubizi lutho ngaphandle kwesikhathi. Ku-Tester Exchange ukuhlola kumahhala, futhi amakhredithi angawokweqa kuphela.

Ingabe izinsizakalo zabahloli ezikhokhelwayo ziyakufanele?

Kuphela uma zingakhombisa ukuthi abahloli bayivula ngempela i-app yakho nsuku zonke. Abahloli abenza i-opt-in bese bengayisebenzisi neze i-app yisizathu esivame kakhulu sokuthi ukuhlola okuvaliwe kungaphumeleli.

Ingabe abahloli abavela ekushintshaneni bayabalwa yi-Google?

Babalwa njenganoma yimuphi omunye umhloli uma bengabantu bangempela abasebenzisa amafoni abo futhi bevula i-app yakho njalo izinsuku zonke ezingu-14.

Kuthatha isikhathi esingakanani ukushintshana kwamahhala?

Uvula i-app ngayinye oyinikezwayo cishe imizuzwana engu-30 ngosuku izinsuku ezingu-14. Kuwumkhuba omncane wansuku zonke, futhi yiwo othola abahloli be-app yakho.

Ingabe ukuhlola okuvaliwe okuhle kuqinisekisa ukufinyelela ku-production?

Cha. I-Google iyanquma. Abahloli bangempela abasebenzayo nezimpendulo eziqotho ohlwini lwemibuzo ye-production bakunika ithuba elingcono kakhulu.
