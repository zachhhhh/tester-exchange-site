Kungani i-Google ithi i-app yakho idinga ukuhlolwa okwengeziwe

Ukwenza i-opt-in akufani nokuhlola. I-Google ingabona ukuthi bangaki abahloli abafake i-app, bayivule kangaki, nokuthi ukhiphe yini izibuyekezo phakathi nokuhlola. Ukuhlola lapho abantu bafake kanye bese bengabuyi kubukeka njengebhokisi elimakiwe nje, hhayi ukuhlola kwangempela.

  • Ukuzibandakanya okuphansi: abahloli bafaka i-app kodwa bayivula kancane noma abazange bayivule.
  • Abahloli abasebenzayo abambalwa kakhulu: abantu bashiya ukuhlola, futhi wehla ngaphansi kwabahloli abangu-12 ababhalisile.
  • Akukho kuthuthukisa: awekho ama-build amasha futhi akukho ukusabela okubonakalayo empendulweni phakathi nezinsuku ezingu-14.
  • Izimpendulo ezibuthakathaka: izimpendulo ezingacacile ohlwini lwemibuzo ye-production, ezingenazo izinombolo nezibonelo zempendulo.

Uhlelo oluphumelela emzameni wesibili

  1. Gcina ukuhlola okuvaliwe kuqhubeka. Ungayisusi i-track noma ukhiphe abahloli abasasebenza.
  2. Thola abahloli bangempela abangu-14–15 abazovula i-app nsuku zonke, ukuze abambalwa abayekayo bangakwehlisele ngaphansi kwabangu-12.
  3. Nika abahloli umsebenzi owodwa noma emibili eqondile ngesonto ("dala i-akhawunti, engeza into, shintsha isilungiselelo").
  4. Cela impendulo ebhaliwe, lungisa okungenani izinkinga ezimbalwa ezibikiwe, futhi ulayishe ama-build amasha ku-track evaliwe phakathi nokuhlola.
  5. Gcina irekhodi: abahloli abasebenzayo nsuku zonke, inani lezinsuku-zabahloli, impendulo etholiwe, nokuthi yini oyishintshile.
  6. Ngemuva kwezinye izinsuku ezingu-14 zokusetshenziswa kwangempela, faka isicelo futhi bese uphendula wonke umbuzo ngalezo zinombolo.

Khombisa ubufakazi bokuzibandakanya

Izimpendulo zakho kufanele zifundeke njengombiko omfushane wokuhlola: bangaki abahloli, bayisebenzise izinsuku ezingaki i-app, yini abayibikile, nokuthi yimaphi ama-build ayilungisile. I-Tester Exchange irekhoda ukusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke okuqinisekisiwe kwawo wonke umhloli futhi ibhala lezi zimpendulo isebenzisa idatha yakho yangempela, ngakho akudingeki uzakhe kabusha ngenkumbulo.

Okungafanele ukwenze

  • Ungasebenzisi amafoni akho engeziwe noma ama-akhawunti akho njengabahloli. Ama-akhawunti amaningi edivayisini eyodwa awabalwa njengokuhlola kwangempela.
  • Ungakhokheli "abahloli" abafaka kuphela bangayivuli neze i-app. Yilokho kanye okwenqatshwayo.
  • Ungakopishi izimpendulo ezijwayelekile ezivela ku-inthanethi. Sebenzisa izinombolo zakho nempendulo yakho.
