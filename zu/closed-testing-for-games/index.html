Umthetho ofanayo, nezingozi ezithile zemidlalo

I-Google iphatha imidlalo nama-app ngokufanayo lapha. Enza ukuhlola okuvaliwe okunabahloli abangu-12 okungenani abahlala benze i-opt-in izinsuku ezingu-14 ngokuqhubekayo, bese ufaka isicelo se-production. Ifomu le-production libuza ngokubamba iqhaza kwabahloli, impendulo nokuthi ushintshe ini, ngakho ukuhlola lapho kungekho owadlala ngale kokufundisa kwenza izimpendulo ezibuthakathaka.

Izingozi eziphatha imidlalo kakhulu kunamanye ama-app:

  • Ukuhambisana kwedivayisi: abahloli babona "your device isn't compatible" futhi abakwazi ukufaka.
  • Usayizi wokulanda: ukulanda kokuqala okukhulu ngedatha yefoni kwenza abahloli bangabi nentshisekelo.
  • Ubude besikhathi: abantu ngeke badlale izinga lemizuzu engama-20 nsuku zonke amasonto amabili.
  • Izikhala zokuqukethwe: isiqeshana esine-vertical slice enezinga elilodwa siphelelwa yizinto zokwenza ngosuku lwesithathu.

Izilungiselelo ze-build ongazihlola kuqala

  • Unity: akha i-Android App Bundle (.aab), sebenzisa i-IL2CPP ene-ARM64 evuliwe (i-Google Play idinga ukusekelwa kwe-64-bit), futhi usethe izinga eliphansi le-API elingasuli amafoni amaningi kunalokho okudingayo. Uma i-build yakho inkulu, sebenzisa i-Play Asset Delivery esikhundleni sokulanda okukodwa okukhulu.
  • Unreal Engine: pakisha i-Android njenge-App Bundle ene-arm64, hlola amazinga e-SDK okuqondiswe kuwo nawaphansi ku-Project Settings, futhi gcina ukulanda kokuqala kuncane ngokuthutha okuqukethwe okungakhethwa kuma-asset pack.
  • Godot: sebenzisa i-Android export template ene-Gradle build evuliwe bese uthekelisa njenge-AAB. Qiniseka ukuthi i-arm64-v8a ifakiwe, futhi uhlole i-renderer efonini ephansi, ngoba amanye amadivayisi aphatha i-Compatibility renderer kangcono kune-Forward+ noma i-Mobile.
  • Wonke ama-engine: qondisa izinga le-API i-Google Play eliyidingayo okwamanje kuma-app amasha, bese usayina nge-Play App Signing.

Ngemva kokulayisha, vula i-Device catalog ku-Play Console ukuze ubone ukuthi bangaki amadivayisi asekelwa yi-build yakho. Uma ingxenye enkulu ikhishiwe, kulungise abahloli bengakafiki.

Setha ukuhlola

  1. Dala noma vula i-track yokuhlola okuvaliwe: Test and release, bese Testing, bese Closed testing.
  2. Engeza amazwe lapho kuhlala khona abahloli bakho.
  3. Engeza abahloli ngohlu lwama-imeyili noma i-Google Group. Nge-Tester Exchange, engeza testerexchange-testers@googlegroups.com.
  4. Layisha i-.aab, thumela ukukhishwa ukuze kubuyekezwe bese ulinda kuze kuthi Published.
  5. Yabelana ngesixhumanisi se-opt-in. Ngokokuqala, singathatha amahora amaningana ukuqala ukusebenza.

Uma abahloli bebika "app not available", bheka umhlahlandlela wokukulungisa. Uhlanganisa ama-akhawunti angafanele, amazwe nama-track angashicilelwanga.

Yini ongacela abahloli ukuthi bayidlale

Nikeza abahloli imigomo emincane yansuku zonke engena emizuzwini embalwa. Ukudlala kwangempela nokuhlukahlukene kuyiko okukusizayo, futhi yikho oyoliphawula kamuva efomini le-production.

  • Izinsuku 1 kuya ku-3: qeda ukufundisa nezinga lokuqala. Bika noma yini edida.
  • Izinsuku 4 kuya ku-7: dlala isikhathi esisodwa esifushane nsuku zonke; zama izilungiselelo, umsindo, izilawuli nokumisa.
  • Izinsuku 8 kuya ku-11: zama i-build entsha enezilungiso; phinda udlale izinga ukuze ubone ukuthi liyaba ngcono yini.
  • Izinsuku 12 kuya ku-14: dlala okuqukethwe okunzima kakhulu okutholakalayo bese ubika ukuphahlazeka, ukulahlekelwa isivinini noma ukukhishwa kwebhethri.

Uma umdlalo wakho unemivuzo yansuku zonke, inselele emfushane yansuku zonke noma imodi esheshayo, khomba abahloli kuyo. Ihambisana ngokwemvelo nesigqi sansuku zonke.

Bonisa ukubamba iqhaza

  • Khipha okungenani isibuyekezo esisodwa ngesikhathi sokuhlola, kungaba kuhle ezimbili. Ukulungisa ukuphahlazeka okubikiwe kuyindaba engcono kakhulu efomini le-production.
  • Sebenzisa i-Android vitals nombiko we-pre-launch ku-Play Console ukuthola ukuphahlazeka nama-ANR emadivayisini angempela.
  • Qoqa impendulo ebhaliwe efomini elilula: imodeli yedivayisi, abakwenzile, okwenzekile.
  • Gcina irekhodi labahloli abasebenza nsuku zonke, izikhathi zokudlala, amabhugi atholiwe nama-build akhishiwe.

Imidlalo yabadlali abaningi neye-inthanethi

Uma umdlalo wakho udinga iseva, yigcine isebenza zonke izinsuku ezingu-14, futhi uqinisekise ukuthi umdlalo usadlalwa lapho abahloli bekuyedwa. I-lobby engagcwali iba sengathi ukuhlola okungenabantu. Engeza ama-bot noma imodi yodwa ngesikhathi sokuhlola uma ukwazi.

Uma kudingeka ukungena, nikeza abahloli imodi yeguest esebenzayo noma iziqondiso ezicacile. Izindonga zokungena zilahlekisa abahloli ngosuku lokuqala.

Ukuthola abadlali abanele

Cela abahloli abangu-15 kunokuba abangu-12, ngoba abanye abadlali bayayeka masinyane emidlalweni. I-Tester Exchange ithumela abantu bangempela abadlala emafonini abo e-Android futhi ibonisa ukusetshenziswa kwansuku zonke kumhloli ngamunye: $12.99 ngabangu-12, $14.99 ngabangu-15 noma $22.99 ngabangu-25, kukhokhwa kanye. Ungazuza futhi abahloli mahhala ngokuhlola ama-app nemidlalo yabanye onjiniyela ku-https://testerexchange.com/earn.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe imidlalo nayo idinga ukuhlola okuvaliwe?

Yebo. Isidingo sisebenza kuwo wonke ama-app avela kuma-akhawunti omuntu siqu adalwe ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023, kuhlanganise nemidlalo.

Ingabe umdlalo wami ungabe ungaphelele ngesikhathi sokuhlola okuvaliwe?

Yebo, ukuhlola okuvaliwe kwenzelwe umsebenzi oqhubekayo. Kodwa abahloli badinga okuqukethwe okwanele ukudlala amasonto amabili, futhi kufanele uqhubeke uwuthuthukisa ngesikhathi sokuhlola.

Kungani abanye abahloli bengakwazi ukufaka umdlalo wami?

Ngokuvamile ukuhambisana kwedivayisi: inguqulo ephezulu ephansi ye-Android, ukungabi nokusekelwa kwe-64-bit, noma izici zehardware ezidingekayo. Hlola i-Device catalog ku-Play Console.

Abahloli kufanele badlale isikhathi esingakanani nsuku zonke?

I-Google ayibeki inombolo. Isikhathi esifushane sangempela nsuku zonke singcono kunesikhathi esisodwa eside bese kungabi lutho.

Ingabe kufanele ngisebenzise i-Play Asset Delivery ekuhlolweni?

Uma umdlalo wakho mkhulu, yebo. Ukulanda kokuqala okuncane kusho ukuthi abahloli abaningi bayawufaka futhi baqale ukudlala.
