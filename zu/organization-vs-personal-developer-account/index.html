I-akhawunti yonjiniyela we-Google Play yenhlangano noma yomuntu siqu

Kubuyekezwe ngo-Novemba 2026

Umthetho wabahloli abangu-12, izinsuku ezingu-14 usebenza kuma-akhawunti onjiniyela bomuntu siqu adalwe ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023. Ama-akhawunti enhlangano awafakwanga kuwo. Lokho kwenza onjiniyela abaningi abangabodwa babuze ukuthi bangabhalisa yini njengebhizinisi esikhundleni salokho. Kwesinye isikhathi lokho kuyikhetho elilungile, kodwa i-akhawunti yenhlangano idinga inhlangano esemthethweni yangempela, inombolo ye-D-U-N-S newebhusayithi eqinisekisiwe. Lo mhlahlandlela uqhathanisa kokubili, usebenzisa iSikhungo Sosizo se-Google njengomthombo.

Izinhlobo ezimbili ze-akhawunti

  • Umuntu siqu: i-Google iyichaza njengeyenzelwe ukusetshenziswa komuntu siqu, njengabafundi, abathanda ukuzijabulisa nonjiniyela abangochwepheshe. Uqinisekisa igama lakho elisemthethweni nekheli.
  • Inhlangano: yamabhizinisi nezinhlangano, kuhlanganise nemisebenzi yezohwebo, yobungcweti neyohulumeni. Uqinisekisa inhlangano ngenombolo ye-D-U-N-S, inombolo yocingo esemthethweni newebhusayithi yenhlangano.

I-Google ithi zombili izinhlobo ze-akhawunti zinokusebenza okuyisisekelo okufanayo. Zombili zingathengisa ama-app nemikhiqizo ye-in-app uma iphrofayela yokukhokha isisethiwe.

Umehluko wokuhlola

I-Google idinga ama-akhawunti omuntu siqu adalwe ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023 enze ukuhlola okuvaliwe okunabahloli abangu-12 okungenani abahlala benze i-opt-in okungenani izinsuku ezingu-14 ngokuqhubekayo, bese befaka isicelo sokufinyelela ku-production. Ama-akhawunti enhlangano awanalesi sinyathelo: angakhipha ku-production uma i-app idlula ukubuyekezwa okujwayelekile.

Lokho kongela cishe amasonto amathathu nge-app ngayinye. Kodwa yiyona kuphela inzuzo yangempela kunjiniyela abaningi abazimele, futhi ukuthola i-akhawunti yenhlangano kungathatha isikhathi eside kunamasonto amathathu uma ungenayo inkampani nenombolo ye-D-U-N-S.

Lapho i-akhawunti yenhlangano idingeka

I-Google ithi ezinye izinhlobo zama-app kufanele zishicilelwe ku-akhawunti yenhlangano. Lezi zihlanganisa:

  • Imikhiqizo nezinsizakalo zezezimali, njengamabhange, izimali ezboleka, ukuhwebelana ngamasheya nama-wallet noma ama-exchange e-crypto.
  • Ama-app ezempilo, njengama-app ezokwelapha nama-app ocwaningo ngabantu.
  • Ama-app avunyelwe ukusebenzisa isigaba se-VpnService ye-Android.
  • Ama-app kahulumeni, enziwe yi-ejensi kahulumeni noma ngenxa yayo.

Uma i-app yakho ikulezi zigaba, udinga i-akhawunti yenhlangano noma ngabe kuyini ngokuhlola.

Okudingwa yi-akhawunti yenhlangano

  • Inombolo ye-D-U-N-S yenhlangano yakho esemthethweni evela ku-Dun & Bradstreet. I-Google ithi awukwazi ukudala i-akhawunti yenhlangano ngaphandle kwenombolo evumelekile. Ukufaka isicelo kumahhala; i-Google iyaxwayisa ngamasevisi ezinkampani zangaphandle ashaja amanani aphezulu. Ingathatha izinsuku noma amasonto ukufika.
  • Inhlangano esemthethweni igama nekheli layo kuhambisana nerekhodi le-D-U-N-S neprofayela yakho yokukhokha ye-Google.
  • Iwebhusayithi yenhlangano oyiqinisekisa ku-Play Console.
  • Inombolo yocingo esemthethweni yenhlangano, kanye namakheli ama-imeyili nezinombolo zocingo zoxhumana naye nezomphakathi zonjiniyela, ngayinye iqinisekiswe ngekhodi esetshenziswa kanye.
  • Imali yokubhalisa ekhokhwa kanye evela kuyo yonke i-akhawunti entsha yonjiniyela.

Ukuguqula i-akhawunti yomuntu siqu

iSikhungo Sosizo se-Google sichaza indlela yokushintsha i-akhawunti yomuntu siqu ibe i-akhawunti yenhlangano. Ngamafuphi:

  1. Qiniseka ukuthi i-akhawunti yakho yomuntu siqu iqinisekisiwe ngokuphelele, kungenazo izixwayiso ezilindile ekhasini lasekhaya le-Play Console.
  2. Iya ku-Manage account, bese Account details, bese About you, bese uqinisekisa iwebhusayithi yenhlangano yakho.
  3. Chofoza i-Change account type bese ukhetha noma udala iprofayela yokukhokha yenhlangano ngenombolo yakho ye-D-U-N-S.
  4. Faka imininingwane yenhlangano nabaxhumana nabo, bese uqinisekisa i-imeyili nocingo ngalunye.
  5. Qeda noma yikuphi ukuqinisekiswa kobuwena okucelwa yi-Google, bese uxhuma iprofayela yokukhokha bese ugcina.

I-Google ithi awukwazi ukuguqula i-akhawunti yenhlangano ibe yomuntu siqu futhi, nokuthi kufanele ulinde okungenani amahora angu-72 ngemva kokushintsha ngaphambi kokuthumela ama-app amasha. Ikhasi le-Google lokuguqula alichazi ukuthi ama-app asevele ekuhlolweni okuvaliwe aphathwa kanjani, ngakho hlola i-Dashboard yakho ngemva kokushintsha ngaphambi kokumisa ukuhlola okuqhubekayo.

Izinzuzo nezindleko

  • Izindleko: ukwakha inkampani kungasho izimali zokubhalisa, umbalo-mali nezikhathi zonyaka zokufaka amafomu, kuye ngezwe lakho. Abahloli abayishumi nambili bakhokhela kancane kakhulu.
  • Isikhathi: i-D-U-N-S kanye nokuqinisekiswa kwewebhusayithi kanye nokuhlolwa kwenhlangano kungathatha isikhathi eside kunokuhlola kwezinsuku ezingu-14.
  • Ukwethembeka: igama lenkampani lingabonakala lethembeke kakhulu kubasebenzisi nabahlanganyeli.
  • Akukho ukubuyela emuva: awukwazi ukuguqula i-akhawunti yenhlangano ibe yomuntu siqu.

Yiyiphi okufanele uyikhethe?

  1. I-app yakho ngezezimali, ezempilo, i-VPN noma kahulumeni: i-akhawunti yenhlangano.
  2. Usunayo inkampani enenombolo ye-D-U-N-S: i-akhawunti yenhlangano. Ikweqa ukuhlola.
  3. Ungunjiniyela ozimele noma othanda ukuzijabulisa ongenayo inkampani: hlala umuntu siqu bese wenza ukuhlola okuvaliwe. Kushesha futhi kushibhile kunokwakha inkampani ukuze uweqe nje lokhu.
  4. Uhlela ama-app amaningi nebhizinisi noma kunjalo: qala inqubo yenkampani ne-D-U-N-S, bese wenza ukuhlola okuvaliwe ku-akhawunti yakho yomuntu siqu ngenkathi ulinda.

Uma uhlala umuntu siqu

Udinga okungenani abahloli bangempela abangu-12 izinsuku ezingu-14, futhi sincoma abangu-15. I-Tester Exchange ithumela abantu bangempela emafonini abo e-Android: $12.99 ngabahloli abangu-12, $14.99 ngabangu-15, noma $22.99 ngabangu-25, kukhokhwa kanye. Noma uthole abahloli mahhala ngokuhlola ama-app abanye onjiniyela ku-https://testerexchange.com/earn.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe ama-akhawunti enhlangano adinga abahloli abangu-12?

Cha. Isidingo sokuhlola okuvaliwe sisebenza kuma-akhawunti omuntu siqu adalwe ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023.

Ingabe ngingathola inombolo ye-D-U-N-S njengomuntu oyedwa?

Izinombolo ze-D-U-N-S zinikezwa izinhlangano zebhizinisi. Amabhizinisi aphethwe ngumuntu oyedwa angabhalisiwe kwamanye amazwe; buza ku-Dun & Bradstreet ngezwe lakho. Ukufaka isicelo kumahhala.

Ingabe ngingashintsha ngisuke ku-akhawunti yomuntu siqu ngiye kweyenhlangano?

Yebo. I-Google ichaza inketho ethi Change account type ngaphansi kwe-Manage account, Account details. Idinga iwebhusayithi yenhlangano eqinisekisiwe nenombolo ye-D-U-N-S.

Ingabe ngingabuyela ku-akhawunti yomuntu siqu kamuva?

Cha. I-Google ithi ama-akhawunti enhlangano awakwazi ukuguqulwa abe awomuntu siqu. Udinga i-akhawunti entsha nokudlulisa i-app.

Ingabe i-akhawunti yenhlangano ifanele ukuthi ngiweqe ukuhlola kuphela?

Ngokuvamile cha kunjiniyela ozimele. Ukwakha inkampani nokuqinisekiswa ngokuvamile kubiza isikhathi nemali eyengeziwe kunokuhlola okuvaliwe kwezinsuku ezingu-14.
