Ngaphambi kokuchofoza i-Apply

  • Okungenani abahloli abangu-12 base benze i-opt-in manje. I-Google iyakudinga lokhu ngosuku olufaka ngalo isicelo, hhayi nje ngesinye isikhathi.
  • Labo bahloli bahlale benze i-opt-in okungenani izinsuku ezingu-14 ezilandelanayo.
  • Abahloli basebenzise i-app ngempela, futhi ungachaza ukuthi kanjani.
  • Unempendulo ongayifinyeza okungenani nenguquko eyodwa oyenzile ngenxa yayo.
  • Izimemezelo zakho ze-App content, uhlu lwesitolo ne-Policy status akunazinkinga ezivulekile.

Ungavali ukuhlola okuvaliwe noma ususe abahloli lapho ufaka isicelo. Uma i-Google ifuna ukuhlola okwengeziwe, uzobadinga.

Ukufaka isicelo sokufinyelela ku-production

  1. Vula i-Play Console bese uya ku-Dashboard ye-app yakho.
  2. Chofoza i-Apply for production. I-Google ikubonisa uma isidingo sokuhlola okuvaliwe sihlangabezaniwe.
  3. Phendula izingxenye ezintathu: mayelana nokuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe (ukuthola abahloli, ukubamba iqhaza, impendulo), mayelana ne-app yakho (izethameli, inani, ukufakwa okulindelekile onyakeni wokuqala) nokulungela kwe-production (oshintshe ini nokuthi wazi kanjani ukuthi ilungile).
  4. Chofoza i-Apply. I-Google iyaxwayisa ukuthi izimpendulo zilahleka uma uphuma ekhasini ngaphambi kwe-Next noma i-Apply, ngakho zibhale kwenye indawo kuqala.

Umhlahlandlela wemibuzo yokufinyelela ku-production uchaza umbuzo ngamunye, futhi ithuluzi lamahhala ku-https://testerexchange.com/tools/production-answers/ libhala izimpendulo ngezinombolo zakho zangempela.

Ukubuyekezwa kuthatha isikhathi esingakanani

iSikhungo Sosizo se-Google sithi ukubuyekezwa kwesicelo sokufinyelela ku-production ngokuvamile kuthatha izinsuku eziyisikhombisa noma ngaphansi, kodwa kwesinye isikhathi kungathatha isikhathi eside. Umnikazi we-akhawunti uthola i-imeyili uma ukubuyekezwa sekuphelile.

I-Google ayishicileli indlela yokusheshisa lokhu. Ukulinda izinsuku ezimbalwa ngaphezu kweviki akuyona into engajwayelekile, futhi ukuxhumana nosizo ngokuvamile akushintshi isikhathi. Sebenzisa isikhathi ukuqhubeka uhlola futhi ulungisa amabhugi; abahloli bahlala ku-track yakho yokuhlola okuvaliwe.

Imiphumela emibili engaba khona

  • Kuvunyiwe: ungafinyelela ekhasini le-Production futhi ushicilele i-app yakho ku-Google Play. Ukuhlola okuvulekile nakho kuyatholakala.
  • Kudingeka ukuhlola okwengeziwe: i-Google ithi kungenzeka udinge ukuqhubeka nokuhlola kwakho okuvaliwe, isibonelo uma ubunabahloli abangaphansi kuka-12 abenze i-opt-in noma ukubamba iqhaza okungenele. Bheka umhlahlandlela wokwenza uma ukufinyelela ku-production kwenqatshiwe.

Ukukhishwa kwakho kokuqala kwe-production

Ukufinyelela ku-production akufani nokuba bukhoma. Usadinga ukwakha ukukhishwa kwe-production, futhi kudlula ekubuyekezweni okujwayelekile kwe-app yi-Google njengoba kunjalo nanoma iyiphi enye i-app. I-Google ayithembisi isikhathi esimisiwe salokhu kubuyekeza. Ama-app amasha avela kuma-akhawunti amasha avame ukuthatha isikhathi eside kunezibuyekezo ezivela kwezisunguliwe, ngakho vumela izinsuku ezimbalwa futhi uhlele usuku lwakho lokuqala nomkhawulo.

  1. Iya ku-Test and release, bese Production, bese Create new release.
  2. Thuthukisa i-build isuka ku-track yakho yokuhlola okuvaliwe, noma layisha i-.aab entsha. Ukusebenzisa i-build abahloli bakho abasebenzise yona sekuyinketho ephephile.
  3. Bhala amanothi okukhishwa bese ukhetha amazwe.
  4. Cabanga ngokukhishwa ngezigaba (isibonelo u-20% wabasebenzisi) ukuze uthole izinkinga kusenesikhathi.
  5. Thumela izinguquko ukuze zibuyekezwe ku-Publishing overview. Uma i-managed publishing ivuliwe, chofoza i-Publish ngemva kokugunyazwa ukuze ushicilele uma uthanda.
  6. Uma sekugunyaziwe futhi kushicilelwe, i-app ingathatha isikhathi esincane ukuvela ekusesheni kwe-Play Store. Yabelana ngesixhumanisi esiqondile ngaleso sikhathi.

  • Ukuhlola okuvaliwe: okungenani izinsuku ezingu-14 uma abahloli abangu-12 sebenze i-opt-in.
  • Ukubuyekezwa kokufinyelela ku-production: ngokuvamile izinsuku eziyisikhombisa noma ngaphansi, ngokwe-Google.
  • Ukubuyekezwa kokukhishwa kokuqala kwe-production: ngokuvamile izinsuku ezimbalwa; kungathatha isikhathi eside.
  • Isamba: hlela cishe amasonto amane kusukela ekulayisheni kokuqala kokuhlola okuvaliwe kuze kube uhlu lomphakathi, nokuningi uma kukhona okudinga ukulungiswa.
