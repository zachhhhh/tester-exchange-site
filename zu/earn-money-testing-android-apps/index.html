Kungani onjiniyela bedinga abahloli

I-Google idinga ama-akhawunti onjiniyela bomuntu siqu adalwe ngemva kuka-13 Novemba 2023 enze ukuhlola okuvaliwe okunabahloli abangu-12 okungenani abahlala benze i-opt-in izinsuku ezingu-14 ngokulandelana ngaphambi kokuba i-app iye emphakathini. Onjiniyela abaningi abangabodwa abazi abasebenzisi be-Android abangu-12 abazokwenza lokho, ngakho badinga abahloli.

I-Google futhi ihlola ukuthi abahloli bayisebenzise ngempela i-app yini. Yingakho umsebenzi ulula kodwa kudingeka ube yiqiniso: vula i-app uyisebenzise, nsuku zonke, efonini yakho.

Okuzuzayo

  • US$0.02 ngosuku ngalunye ovula ngalo wonke ama-app asohlwini lwakho (okungenani ama-app angu-3 ngalolo suku). Usuku ngalunye lokuhlola luzuza namakhredithi, atholela i-app yakho abahloli.
  • Khipha kusuka ku-US$3. Lokho kucishe kube yizinyanga ezinhlanu zokuhlola nsuku zonke.
  • Izindlela zokukhokhelwa: PayPal, Binance Pay noma Payoneer.

I-app ngayinye ithatha umzuzu noma emibili kuphela ngosuku, ngakho ungaba sekuhlolweni okuningana ngesikhathi esisodwa. Uma uhlola nsuku zonke, uthola cishe u-US$0.60 ngenyanga. Lena yimali encane yephakethe, hhayi umsebenzi.

Indlela esebenza ngayo, isinyathelo ngesinyathelo

  1. Efonini yakho ye-Android, vula i-https://testerexchange.com/earn ku-browser yakho bese ungena.
  2. Uhlu lwakho lwama-app luzigcwalisa. Thepha inkinobho eluhlaza: ngokokuqala, ikucela ukuthi ujoyine iqembu labahloli (kanye), bese ube ngumhloli ku-Google Play.
  3. Faka i-app ku-Google Play ngesixhumanisi sokuhlola.
  4. Vula i-app uyisebenzise ngempela umzuzu noma emibili nsuku zonke izinsuku ezingu-14. Zama izici eziyinhloko, futhi uthumele impendulo uma okuthile kuphuka noma kukudida.
  5. Hlala ekuhlolweni zonke izinsuku ezingu-14. Ungaphumi ekuhlolweni noma ususe i-app ngaphambi kwesikhathi.
  6. Ngosuku ngalunye ovula ngalo wonke ama-app asohlwini lwakho, u-US$0.02 wengezwa ebhalansini yakho. Khipha uma ufinyelela ku-US$3.

Imithetho

  • Ifoni yangempela kuphela. Sebenzisa ifoni yakho ye-Android. Ama-emulator, amafoni asefu namapulazi amadivayisi akuvunyelwe.
  • I-akhawunti eyodwa ngomuntu. Ungadali ama-akhawunti engeziwe noma usebenzise ama-akhawunti amalungu omndeni ukuze uqoqe ukuhlolwa okuningi.
  • Sebenzisa i-app ngempela. Ukuyivula uyivale ngokushesha akusizi unjiniyela futhi akuyona into yokuhlola ngempela.
  • Qeda lokho okuqalile. Ukuhamba kusenesikhathi kungalimaza unjiniyela, ngoba i-Google ibala abahloli abahlala benze i-opt-in izinsuku ezingu-14 ngokuqhubekayo.
  • Ungashiyi izibuyekezo zomphakathi zezinguqulo zokuhlola. I-Google ayivumeli izibuyekezo zomphakathi zama-build okuhlola kunoma yikuphi; thumela impendulo kunjiniyela esikhundleni salokho.

Ama-akhawunti aphula le mithetho angasuswa ekuhlolweni. Imithetho ikhona ngoba onjiniyela bathembele kuwe ukuthi ube ngumhloli wangempela.

Indlela yokuba ngumhloli omuhle

  • Setha isikhumbuzo sansuku zonke ngesikhathi esifanayo, isibonelo nekhofi lakho lasekuseni.
  • Chitha umzuzu esikrinini esihlukile nsuku zonke, hhayi nje isikrini sokuvula.
  • Bhala impendulo emfushane, ecacile: ukwenzile, obukulindele, nokwenzekile.
  • Gcina ama-app uze uphele izinsuku ezingu-14, noma ungawathandi kakhulu.

Ingabe kuphephile?

Ufaka ama-app ku-Google Play, hhayi emafayelini angahleliwe, ngakho adlula ezivikelweni ezijwayelekile ze-Google. Awudingi ukufaka noma iyiphi i-app ye-Tester Exchange; konke kusebenza ku-browser yakho. Nokho, sebenzisa umqondo ojwayelekile: ungayinikezi i-app yokuhlola izimvume ebala ukuthi ayizidingi, futhi ungafaki imininingwane yangempela yokukhokha noma imininingwane yomuntu siqu ebucayi ku-app oyihlolayo.

Ukuthi lokhu kwabani

Kulungele abantu abasebenzisa ifoni yabo kakhulu futhi abathanda ukuzama ama-app amasha, abafundi abafuna okuncane okwengeziwe, nonjiniyela abafuna abahloli be-app yabo. Onjiniyela bangahlola ama-app abanye mahhala ukuze bathole abahloli be-app yabo, okuvame ukuba nenani elikhulu kunemali.

Uma udinga imali engenayo yangempela, lokhu akusona. Inkokhelo incane: cishe u-US$0.60 ngenyanga uma uhlola nsuku zonke. Kodwa kuyiqiniso, kuyashintsheka futhi kuthatha isikhathi esincane kakhulu.
