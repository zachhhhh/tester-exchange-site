Abangane nomndeni bayavunyelwa, futhi bangabahloli abathembeke kakhulu ozobathola. Okunzima akukhona ukubacela; ukwenza ukuthi ngamunye wabo aqhubeke evula i-app engakaqedwa amasonto amabili. Ingabe abangane nomndeni bangaba ngabahloli bokuhlola okuvaliwe?

Yebo. I-Google ayisho ukuthi abahloli bakho kumele babe ngobani. Okubuyekezayo ngukuthi abantu bangempela benze i-opt-in, bafaka i-app ku-track evaliwe futhi bayisebenzisa phakathi nokuhlola. Umngane efonini yakhe ye-Android ubalwa ngokufanayo nomuntu ongamazi.

  • Umhloli ngamunye udinga i-akhawunti yakhe ye-Google efonini ayisebenzisayo, eyenze i-opt-in nge-Google Group yakho noma uhlu lwama-imeyili.
  • Idivayisi yakhe: ama-akhawunti amahlanu efonini eyodwa yiphethini ebukeka iyimbumbulu. Umuntu oyedwa, ifoni eyodwa.
  • Kufakwe ku-Google Play: abahloli kumele bafake ngesixhumanisi se-opt-in, hhayi nge-APK obathumela yona.
  • Babhalisile izinsuku zonke ezingu-14: umuntu oshiya ukuhlola ngosuku lwe-9 akasabalwa kwabangu-12.

Ungabamema kanjani ngendlela esebenzayo

  • Cela abantu abangu-14–15, hhayi abangu-12. Kuhlale kukhona olahlekelwa yifoni, oshintsha ama-akhawunti noma okhohlwayo.
  • Sebenzisa i-Google Group. Abangane bajoyina ngokuchofoza kanye, futhi awusoze waphinda wanamathisela ama-imeyili ku-Play Console.
  • Thumela umlayezo owodwa onazo zonke izinyathelo: joyina iqembu, vula isixhumanisi se-opt-in, faka ku-Google Play, vula i-app kanye ngosuku.
  • Bheka ama-iPhone kuqala. Abangane abaningi abanayo ifoni ye-Android; buza ngaphambi kokuthi ubabale.

Ukugcina abangane besebenza izinsuku ezingu-14

Abangane banezinhloso ezinhle kodwa basakhohlwa ngosuku lwesine. Banike isizathu nesikhumbuzo sokuvula i-app nsuku zonke.

  • Umsebenzi omncane wansuku zonke: "thepha into eyodwa ungitshele uma ibididayo" kungcono kuno-"ngicela uhlole i-app yami".
  • Izikhumbuzo ezizenzakalelayo: i-app ye-Android ye-Tester Exchange ihlola nsuku zonke efonini yomhloli ukuthi i-app yakho ivuliwe yini, futhi imkhumbuze kusihlwa uma ingavulwanga.
  • Ukubuka inqubekela phambili kwakho: ubona ukuthi ngubani ososukwini lwe-6 nokuthi ngubani oyekile, ngakho uyazi ukuthi ngubani okufanele umkhumbuze ngaphambi kokuthi iwashi lisethwe kabusha.
  • Khipha isibuyekezo evikini lesibili: sinika wonke umuntu isizathu esisha sokuvula i-app futhi sikhombisa i-Google ukuhlola okusebenzayo.

Uma ungenabo abangane abangu-12

Onjiniyela abaningi banabantu abathathu kuya kwabayisishiyagalombili abangababuza. Akudingeki ubathole abasele phakathi kwabantu ongabazi ezinkundleni.

  • Ukushintshana kokuhlola kwamahhala: ku-Tester Exchange ifoni yakho ithola ama-app abanye onjiniyela okuwahlola ngokuzenzakalelayo, futhi yonke i-app oyihlolayo iletha umhloli ku-app yakho.
  • Yeqa ukuhlola: amakhredithi agcwalisa izihlalo abangane bakho abazishiya zingenalutho, futhi ama-app akhokhelwe athola abahloli kuqala. Amalungu athola amakhredithi ngokuhlola, noma ungathenga iphakethe elincane.
  • Imithetho efanayo kuwo wonke umuntu: wonke umhloli, kungaba ngumngane noma cha, ngumuntu wangempela efonini yakhe, futhi wonke usuku luqinisekiswa ngendlela efanayo.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ingabe abangane nomndeni babalwa njengabahloli be-Google Play?

Yebo, inqobo nje uma ngamunye esebenzisa i-akhawunti yakhe ye-Google efonini yakhe ye-Android, efaka ngesixhumanisi sakho se-opt-in sokuhlola okuvaliwe futhi ehlala ebhalisile izinsuku zonke ezingu-14.

Ngingasebenzisa i-akhawunti yami yesibili noma ifoni yomlingani wami enama-akhawunti amaningi?

Ungakwenzi. Ama-akhawunti amaningi edivayisini eyodwa, noma ama-akhawunti owazidalela wona, abukeka njengabahloli mbumbulu. Umuntu oyedwa, i-akhawunti eyodwa, ifoni eyodwa.

Abangane kufanele bayivule kangaki i-app?

I-Google ayishicileli inombolo, kodwa ukusetshenziswa okuvamile kukho konke ukuhlola yikho ekufunayo. Cela iseshini emfushane nsuku zonke ukuze izinsuku ezimbalwa ezeqiwe zingabi nandaba.

Kuthiwani uma abangane abahlanu kuphela bengasiza?

Basebenzise, bese ugcwalisa ezinye izihlalo ngokushintshana. Ku-Tester Exchange abangane bajoyina mahhala ngesixhumanisi sakho sokwabelana, futhi ukushintshana kugcwalisa okusele.

Ingabe ukuhlola nabangane kuqinisekisa ukufinyelela ku-production?

Cha. I-Google iyanquma. Abahloli bangempela abasebenzayo nezimpendulo eziqotho emibuzweni yokufinyelela ku-production bakunika ithuba elingcono kakhulu.
