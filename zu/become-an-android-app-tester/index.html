1. Ama-app okufinyelela kusenesikhathi nama-beta ku-Play Store

Ikhasi losizo le-Google Play uqobo lichaza izinhlelo ezimbili zomphakathi (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7003180). Ama-app okufinyelela kusenesikhathi awakakhululwa; ama-app e-beta yizinguqulo ezintsha, ezizamayo kakhulu zama-app asevele ekhona. Womabili angase angazinzi njengama-app evamile.

  • Ama-app angakakhululwa: vula i-Play Store, iya kuthebhu ethi “For you” bese ubheka ngaphansi kwe-“Apps in development”.
  • Imidlalo engakakhululwa: vula ithebhu ethi “New” bese ubheka ngaphansi kwe-“Be the first to play”.
  • Ama-beta ama-app onawo: thepha isithonjana sephrofayela yakho, bese uya ku-Manage apps & devices, vula ikhasi le-app efakiwe bese uthepha i-“Join” ngaphansi kwe-“Join the beta”. Uma ungayiboni, leyo app ayinayo i-beta yomphakathi njengamanje.

Lezi zinhlelo zivulelwe wonke umuntu futhi ezinye ziyagcwala, ngakho zingindlela enhle yokuzama izinto ezintsha, kodwa akekho olinde ukuthi wehle.

2. Amavivinyo avaliwe: lapho ezivivinyi zidingeka khona ngempela

I-Google Play idinga ukuthi ama-akhawunti omthuthukisi womuntu siqu adalwe ngemuva kwangomhla ziyi-13 kuNovemba 2023 enze ivivinyo elivaliwe nezivivinyi okungenani eziyi-12 ezihlala zijoyinile izinsuku eziyi-14 ngokulandelana ngaphambi kokuba i-app iye emphakathini (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465).

Amavivinyo avaliwe awavezi ku-Play Store. Ujoyina ngesixhumanisi sokungena esivela kumthuthukisi, ngakho abathuthukisi abangabodwa kumele bazifunele ezivivinyi, futhi abaningi abazi abasebenzisi be-Android abayi-12 abazosiza. Leso yisikhala esigcwalisiwe yisivivinyi.

3. Vivinya ama-app amavivinyo avaliwe nge-Tester Exchange

  • Akukho app eyengeziwe: vula i-https://testerexchange.com/earn kubhrawuza yefoni yakho ye-Android bese ungena nge-akhawunti ye-Google esetshenziswa yi-Play Store yakho. Uhlu lwama-app lwakho luzigcwalisa ngokwalo.
  • Cishe iminithi eyodwa ngosuku ku-app ngayinye: joyina ivivinyo, faka i-app kusuka ku-Google Play, uyivule bese uyisebenzisa ngempela.
  • Amakhredithi: uthola ikhredithi eli-1 ngosuku ngalunye ovula ngalo i-app oyivivinyayo. Amakhredithi afaka ezivivinyi ku-app yakho, uma ngelinye ilanga uyakha.
  • Imali kuma-app athile: ama-app aphawulwe nge-💵 angawamakhasimende akhokhayo futhi akhokha i-US$0.50 ngevivinyo ngalinye lezinsuku eziyi-14 eliphelele. Khipha imali kusukela ku-US$3 uyise ku-PayPal, Binance Pay noma Payoneer.

Le yimali encane yasephaketheni, hhayi imali engenayo: ukuthi uthola ama-app emali amangaki kuncike ekutheni kunamakhasimende akhokhayo amangaki adinga ezivivinyi kuleyo viki.

Isinyathelo ngesinyathelo

  1. Efonini yakho ye-Android, vula i-https://testerexchange.com/earn bese ungena.
  2. Thepha inkinobho eluhlaza. Ngokokuqala, ikucela ukuthi ujoyine iqembu lezivivinyi (kanye kuphela), bese kuba yisivivinyi ku-Google Play.
  3. Faka i-app kusuka ku-Google Play ngesixhumanisi sevivinyo bese uyivula.
  4. Yisebenzise iminithi noma amabili nsuku zonke izinsuku eziyi-14, esikrinini esihlukile suku ngalunye.
  5. Thumela impendulo emfushane uma okuthile kungasebenzi noma kukudida: okwenzile, okulindele, nokwenzekile.
  6. Hlala ujoyinile futhi ugcine i-app ifakiwe kuze kube yilanga le-14.

Ukunikeza impendulo umthuthukisi angayisebenzisa

Kuma-app okufinyelela kusenesikhathi nama-beta, i-Google Play inebhokisi elithi “Private feedback to developer” ekhasini le-app (isithonjana sephrofayela, Manage apps & devices, Beta). Ngumthuthukisi kuphela ongayibona. I-Google futhi yabelana nomthuthukisi ngedatha ethile yokusetshenziswa evela kuma-app okuvivinya, njengolwazi lwedivayisi nezehlakalo ezingaphakathi kwe-app, ukumsiza alungise izinkinga.

Imithetho

  • Ifoni yakho yangempela. Azikho ama-emulator, amafoni asefwini noma amapulazi amadivayisi.
  • I-akhawunti eyodwa ngomuntu. Ama-akhawunti engeziwe ayasuswa.
  • Sebenzisa i-app ngempela. Ukuyivula bese uyivala ngokushesha akusiko ukuvivinya.
  • Qeda lokho okuqalile. I-Google ibala ezivivinyi ezihlala zijoyinile izinsuku eziyi-14 ngokuqhubekayo, ngakho ukuhamba ngaphambi kwesikhathi kulimaza umthuthukisi.

Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

Ngidinga ukuhlangenwe nakho ukuze ngibe yisivivinyi se-app?

Cha. Abathuthukisi badinga abasebenzisi abavamile abavula i-app nsuku zonke futhi bathi uma okuthile kungasebenzi.

Ezivivinyi ze-app ziyakhokhelwa?

Ukufinyelela kusenesikhathi nama-beta e-Play Store akukhokhi. Ku-Tester Exchange, ama-app aphawulwe nge-💵 akhokha i-US$0.50 ngevivinyo ngalinye lezinsuku eziyi-14 eliphelele, futhi usuku ngalunye lokuvivinya lunikeza amakhredithi.

Ngingazivivinya ama-app ku-iPhone?

Lawa cha. Amavivinyo e-Google Play adinga ifoni ye-Android ene-akhawunti ye-Google.

Kuphephile ukufaka ama-app okuvivinya?

Uwafaka kusuka ku-Google Play, ngakho adlula ezivikelweni ezijwayelekile ze-Google. Izinguqulo zokuvivinya zingase zingazinzi, ngakho ungafaki imininingwane yokukhokha noma ulwazi olubucayi kuzo.

Ngingavivinya ama-app amangaki ngesikhathi esisodwa?

Amaningi. Ngayinye ithatha cishe iminithi eyodwa ngosuku, ngakho setha isikhumbuzi sansuku zonke ubheke uhlu lwakho.
