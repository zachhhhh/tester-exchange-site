Ịrịọ production access

Ntọhapụ production mbụ gị

Production access abụghị otu ihe dị ka ịdị ndụ. Ịchọrọ ka ịmepụta ntọhapụ production, ọ na-agafekwa nyocha app nkịtị nke Google dị ka app ọ bụla ọzọ. Google anaghị ekwe nkwa oge kpọmkwem maka nyocha a. App ọhụrụ sitere na akaụntụ ọhụrụ na-ewekarị ogologo oge karịa mmelite sitere na akaụntụ guzobere, ya mere kwe ka ụbọchị ole na ole gafee ma hazie ụbọchị mbido gị nwere ohere.

  1. Gaa na Test and release, wee Production, wee Create new release.
  2. Bufee build site na track emechiri emechi gị, ma ọ bụ bulite .aab ọhụrụ. Iji build ndị nnwale gị ejirila bụ nhọrọ kacha nchebe.
  3. Dee release notes ma họrọ mba.
  4. Tụlee staged rollout (dịka 20% nke ndị ọrụ) ka ị nwee ike ịchọta nsogbu n'oge.
  5. Zipu mgbanwe ndị a maka nyocha na Publishing overview. Ọ bụrụ na managed publishing na-arụ ọrụ, pịa Publish mgbe nkwado gasịrị ka ị pụta n'ime ndụ mgbe ị chọrọ.
  6. Ozugbo akwadoro ma bipụta ya, app ahụ nwere ike were obere oge tupu ọ apụta na nchọta Play Store. Kekọrịta njikọ ozugbo n'oge ahụ.
