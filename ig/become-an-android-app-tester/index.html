1. Ngwa ịbanye n'oge na beta na Play Store

Ibe enyemaka nke Google Play n'onwe ya na-akọwa mmemme abụọ n'ihu ọha (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7003180). Ngwa ịbanye n'oge adịbeghị ka a wepụtara; ngwa beta bụ ụdị ọhụrụ, nke nwale karịa, nke ngwa ndị e wepụtara ugbu a. Ha abụọ nwere ike ghara ịdị guzosie ike dị ka ngwa nkịtị.

  • Ngwa ndị a wepụtabeghị: meghee Play Store, gaa taabụ “For you” ma lee n'okpuru “Apps in development”.
  • Egwuregwu ndị a wepụtabeghị: meghee taabụ “New” ma lee n'okpuru “Be the first to play”.
  • Beta nke ngwa ị nwere: pịa akara profaịlụ gị, mgbe ahụ Manage apps & devices, meghee ibe ngwa ịtọrọ ma pịa “Join” n'okpuru “Join the beta”. Ọ bụrụ na ịhụghị ya, ngwa ahụ enweghị beta n'ihu ọha ugbu a.

Mmemme ndị a mepere emepe maka onye ọ bụla, ụfọdụ na-ejikwa ndị mmadụ nyochaa ha, ya mere ha bụ ụzọ ọma isi nwalee ihe ọhụrụ, mana enweghị onye na-eche ka ị bịa.

2. Ule mmechi: ebe a na-achọ ndị na-anwale n'ezie

Google Play chọrọ ka akaụntụ ndị mmepe nke onwe e kere mgbe 13 Nọvemba 2023 gachara na-eme ule mmechi nwere ndị na-anwale opekata mpe 12 ndị ga-anọgide n'ime ule ahụ ụbọchị 14 n'usoro tupu ngwa ahụ ekwe ka ọ pụta n'ihu ọha (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465).

Ule mmechi anaghị apụta na Play Store. Ị na-esonye site na njikọ ịbanye sitere n'aka onye mmepe, ya mere ndị mmepe naanị ha ga-achọrọ ndị na-anwale n'onwe ha, ọtụtụ ha amaghịkwa ndị ọrụ Android 12 ga-enyere ha aka. Nke ahụ bụ oghere onye na-anwale na-eju.

3. Nwalee ngwa ule mmechi na Tester Exchange

  • Enweghị ngwa ọzọ: meghee https://testerexchange.com/earn na browser ekwentị Android gị ma banye site na Google Account Play Store gị na-eji. Ndepụta ngwa gị na-ejupụta n'onwe ya.
  • Ihe dị ka nkeji otu kwa ụbọchị maka ngwa ọ bụla: sonye n'ule ahụ, wụnye ngwa ahụ site na Google Play, meghee ya ma jiri ya n'ezie.
  • Credits: ị na-enweta credit 1 maka ụbọchị ọ bụla ị meghere ngwa ị na-anwale. Credits na-enweta ndị na-anwale maka ngwa nke gị, ma ọ bụrụ na ị arụọ otu.
  • Ego maka ịnwale kwa ụbọchị: ị na-enwetakwa US$0.02 maka ụbọchị ọ bụla ị meghere ngwa niile dị na ndepụta gị (ma ọ dịkarịa ala ngwa 3 n'ụbọchị ahụ). Wepụ ego malite na US$3 gaa PayPal, Binance Pay ma ọ bụ Payoneer.

Nke a bụ ego akpa, ọ bụghị ntinye ego: ọ bụrụ na ị na-anwale kwa ụbọchị, ọ ruru ihe dịka US$0.60 kwa ọnwa.

Nzọụkwụ site na nzọụkwụ

  1. Na ekwentị Android gị, meghee https://testerexchange.com/earn ma banye.
  2. Pịa bọtịnụ ndụ akwụkwọ ndụ. Oge mbụ, ọ ga-agwa gị ka ị sonye na otu ndị na-anwale (otu ugboro), mgbe ahụ bụrụ onye na-anwale na Google Play.
  3. Wụnye ngwa ahụ site na Google Play site na njikọ ule ma meghee ya.
  4. Jiri ya nkeji otu ma ọ bụ abụọ kwa ụbọchị ruo ụbọchị 14, na ihuenyo dị iche kwa ụbọchị.
  5. Zipu echiche dị mkpirikpi mgbe ihe gbajiri ma ọ bụ mebie gị uche: ihe ị mere, ihe ị tụrụ anya ya, ihe mere.
  6. Nọgide n'ime ule ahụ ma debe ngwa ahụ ka ọ wụnyere ruo ụbọchị nke 14.

Inye echiche onye mmepe nwere ike iji

Maka ngwa ịbanye n'oge na beta, Google Play nwere igbe “Private feedback to developer” n'ibe ngwa ahụ (akara profaịlụ, Manage apps & devices, Beta). Naanị onye mmepe na-ahụ ya. Google na-ekekwa ụfọdụ data ojiji sitere na ngwa ule ya na onye mmepe, dị ka ozi ngwaọrụ na ihe omume dị n'ime ngwa, iji nyere ha aka idozi nsogbu.

Iwu ndị a

  • Ekwentị gị n'ezie nke gị. Enweghị emulators, ekwentị cloud ma ọ bụ ugbo ngwaọrụ.
  • Otu akaụntụ maka onye ọ bụla. A na-ewepụ akaụntụ ọzọ.
  • Jiri ngwa ahụ n'ezie. Imeghe ya ma mechie ya ozugbo abụghị ịnwale.
  • Mechaa ihe ị malitere. Google na-agụ ndị na-anwale nọgidere n'ime ule ụbọchị 14 n'usoro, ya mere ịhapụ n'oge na-emebi onye mmepe.

Ajụjụ a na-ajụkarị

Ọ dị mkpa ka m nwee ahụmahụ ka m bụrụ onye na-anwale ngwa?

Mba. Ndị mmepe chọrọ ndị ọrụ nkịtị ga-emeghe ngwa ahụ kwa ụbọchị ma kwuo mgbe ihe anaghị arụ ọrụ.

Ndị na-anwale ngwa ọ na-enweta ụgwọ?

Ịbanye n'oge na beta nke Play Store anaghị akwụ ụgwọ. Na Tester Exchange, ị na-enweta US$0.02 maka ụbọchị ọ bụla ị meghere ngwa niile dị na ndepụta gị (ma ọ dịkarịa ala 3), ụbọchị ule ọ bụla na-enyekwa credits.

M nwere ike ịnwale ngwa na iPhone?

Ọ bụghị ndị a. Ule Google Play chọrọ ekwentị Android nwere Google Account.

Ọ dị nchebe iwụnye ngwa ule?

Ị na-awụnye ha site na Google Play, ya mere ha na-agabiga nchebe nkịtị nke Google. Ụdị ule nwere ike ghara ịdị guzosie ike, ya mere abanyela nkọwa ịkwụ ụgwọ ma ọ bụ ozi nzuzo n'ime ha.

Ngwa ole ka m nwere ike ịnwale n'otu oge?

Ọtụtụ. Nke ọ bụla na-ewe ihe dị ka nkeji otu kwa ụbọchị, ya mere tọọ ncheta kwa ụbọchị ma gafee ndepụta gị.
