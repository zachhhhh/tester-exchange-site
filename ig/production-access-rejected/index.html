  5. Dozie ọtụtụ nsogbu a kọrọ ma bulite build ọhụrụ na track emechiri emechi n'oge nnwale ahụ.
  6. Mee nnwale ahụ opekata mpe ụbọchị 14 ọzọ nwere ojiji n'ezie kwa ụbọchị. Google anaghị ebipụta oge nchere kpọmkwem, ya mere hapụ ihe akaebe ka ọ gbakwunye kama ilele kalenda.
