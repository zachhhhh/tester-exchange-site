Tupu ị malite

  • Akaụntụ onye mmepe Play Console nke nkwado njirimara gị gwụchara.
  • App gị emepụtara na Play Console, nwere store listing, ọkwa ọdịnaya, ndị ga-eji ya, data safety na nkwupụta App content ndị ọzọ ejupụtara. Play Console agaghị ekwe ka ị zipu ntọhapụ maka nyocha ma ihe achọrọ efu.
  • App Bundle Android (.aab) edebanyere aha nwere aha package ị ga-enwe afọ ojuju idebe ruo mgbe ebighị ebi. Ị gaghị enwe ike ịgbanwe ya mgbe e mesịrị.

1. Meghee ma ọ bụ mepụta track emechiri emechi

  1. Na Play Console, họrọ app gị.
  2. Gaa na Test and release, wee Testing, wee Closed testing.
  3. Play Console na-enye gị track emechiri emechi ndabara (a na-akpọkarị Alpha). Jiri ya. Ị nwere ike ịmepụta track emechiri emechi ndị ọzọ, mana otu ezuru maka iwu ahụ.
  4. Pịa Manage track.

2. Họrọ mba na mpaghara

Meghee taabụ Countries / regions ma tinye mba ebe ndị nnwale gị bi. Onye nnwale nọ na mba ị họghị agaghị enwe ike itinye app ahụ.

Google na-ekwu na mgbanwe nke mba nkesa na-emetụta track niile, na track emechiri emechi ndị ọzọ anaghị akwado nkenke mba nke ha. Ọ bụrụ na ndị nnwale gị gbasaara n'ụwa niile, nhọrọ kacha dị mfe bụ itinye mba ọ bụla ị nwere ike iziga app n'iwu.

3. Tinye ndị nnwale: ndepụta email ma ọ bụ Google Group

Meghee taabụ Testers. Ị nwere nhọrọ abụọ, ị nwekwara ike iji abụọ na otu track.

  • Ndepụta email: pịa Create email list, kpọọ ya aha, wee pịnye adreesị ma ọ bụ bulite CSV. Google na-enye ohere ruo ndị ọrụ 2,000 kwa ndepụta. Ibulite CSV na-edochi adreesị dị na ndepụta ahụ. Adreesị ọ bụla ga-abụ Akaụntụ Google onye nnwale na-eji na Play Store ekwentị ya.
  • Google Group: tinye adreesị otu ahụ, dịka yourgroup@googlegroups.com. Naanị ndị otu nke otu ahụ nwere ike isonye na nnwale gị, ha ga-esonyekwa na otu ahụ tupu ha emee opt-in. Ịgbakwunye onye na otu ahụ bụ naanị ihe ị na-eme; ị naghị emetụ Play Console ọzọ.

Ọ bụrụ na ị na-eji Tester Exchange, tinye otu testerexchange-testers@googlegroups.com n'okpuru Google Groups ma chekwaa. Otu adreesị ahụ na-ekwe ka ndị nnwale anyị niile ziga ịtinye app gị. Lekwa ntuziaka banyere Google Group vs ndepụta email.

4. Tinye ọwa nzaghachi

Na otu taabụ Testers ahụ, tinye URL nzaghachi ma ọ bụ adreesị email. Ndị nnwale na-ahụ ya mgbe ha sonyere. Ị ga-achọ ichịkọta nzaghachi ndị nnwale na fọm production access, ya mere mee ka ọ dị mfe: obere Google Form ma ọ bụ adreesị email pụrụ iche na-arụ ọrụ.

5. Mepụta ntọhapụ ma zipu ya maka nyocha

  1. Na track emechiri emechi, pịa Create new release.
  2. Bulite .aab gị. Hapụ Play App Signing ka ọ na-achịkwa igodo gị ma ọ bụrụ na ị nweghị ihe kpatara ya ị ga-eji eme ihe ọzọ.
  3. Tinye aha ntọhapụ na release notes dị mkpirikpi, dịka "First closed test build".
  4. Pịa Next, dozie njehie ọ bụla, wee Save.
  5. Gaa na Publishing overview ma zipu mgbanwe maka nyocha. Ọ bụrụ na managed publishing na-arụ ọrụ, ịkwesịrị ịpị Publish mgbe nkwado gasịrị.

A na-enyocha ntọhapụ nnwale emechiri emechi na akaụntụ ọhụrụ tupu ha adị ndụ. Oge dịgasị site na awa ole na ole ruo ụbọchị ole na ole.

6. Kekọrịta njikọ opt-in

Google na-ekwu na njikọ opt-in na-apụta naanị mgbe ọkwa app bụ Published. Detuo ya site na taabụ Testers. Ọ dị ka https://play.google.com/apps/testing/your.package.name.

Oge mbụ ị na-ebipụta, njikọ ahụ nwere ike were awa ole na ole tupu ọ malite ịrụ ọrụ. Ndị nnwale enweghị ike ịchọta nnwale emechiri emechi site n'ịchọ na Google Play, ya mere ha chọrọ njikọ a ma ọ bụ njikọ Play Store gaa na app gị.

  1. Onye nnwale na-abanye na otu Akaụntụ Google ị tinyere (ma ọ bụ nke dị na Google Group gị).
  2. Ha na-emeghe njikọ opt-in ma pịa ka ha bụrụ onye nnwale.
  3. Ha na-etinye app gị site na Google Play site na njikọ dị na ibe ahụ.
  4. Ha na-emeghe ya, ma na-aga n'ihu na-emeghe ya n'ime ụbọchị 14.

7. Lelee na ụbọchị 14 na-agụ

Dashboard gị na-egosi iwu nnwale emechiri emechi na ndị nnwale ole mere opt-in. Lelee ya kwa ụbọchị ole na ole. Ọ bụrụ na ọnụọgụ ahụ daa n'okpuru 12, chọta ndị ga-edochi ozugbo, n'ihi na iwu ahụ na-agụ ndị nnwale 12 nọgidere na opt-in n'enweghị ọgbaghara.

N'oge nnwale ahụ, bulite opekata mpe otu build ọhụrụ na otu track ahụ. Ọ na-egosi Google na i mere ihe gbasara nzaghachi, ndị nnwale dị na track ahụ na-enwekwa mmelite n'onwe ya.

Ajụjụ a na-ajụkarị

M nwere ike iji nnwale internal kama?

Mba. Iwu ahụ chọrọ nnwale emechiri emechi. Google na-ekwukwa na ndị mere opt-in na nnwale internal gị enweghị ike isonye na nnwale emechiri emechi ruo mgbe ha hapụrụ nke internal.

Ekwesịrị m iji ndepụta email ka ọ bụ Google Group?

Google Group dị mfe mgbe ndị nnwale na-abịa na-agakwa, n'ihi na ị naghị edezi track ahụ. Ndepụta email dị mma maka otu ndị enyi guzosiri ike. Ị nwere ike iji abụọ.

Gịnị mere m anaghị ahụ njikọ opt-in?

Ọ na-egosi naanị mgbe a bipụtara ntọhapụ dị na track emechiri emechi. Lelee Publishing overview maka nyocha na-echere ma ọ bụ mgbanwe ezipụbeghị.

Onye nnwale ọ chọrọ Akaụntụ Google?

Ee. Google na-ekwu na ndị ọrụ chọrọ Akaụntụ Google ma ọ bụ akaụntụ Google Workspace iji sonye na nnwale, ọ ga-abụkwa akaụntụ dị na Play Store ekwentị ha.

M nwere ike ime app nnwale emechiri emechi ka ọ bụrụ nke a na-akwụ ụgwọ?

Ị nwere ike, mana Google na-ekwu na ndị nnwale ga-azụta app a na-akwụ ụgwọ n'ime nnwale emechiri emechi. Debe app nnwale n'efu ka ị ghara ịtụfu ndị nnwale.
