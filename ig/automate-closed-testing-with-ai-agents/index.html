Ihe agent nwere ike ime na ihe ọ na-enweghị ike ime

Google chọrọ ka akaụntụ onye mmepe nkeonwe e mepụtara mgbe 13 Nọvemba 2023 gachara mee nnwale emechiri emechi nwere opekata mpe ndị nnwale 12 mere opt-in ruo ụbọchị 14 n'usoro tupu ha arịọ maka production. Ọtụtụ ọrụ gbasara nke ahụ bụ nhazi, nke agents na-eme nke ọma.

  • Agent nwere ike: itinye app gị, imepe mkpọsa ndị nnwale, itinye kredit gị, ilele ndị nnwale ole na-arụ ọrụ, ịkwụsị ma ọ bụ imechi mkpọsa, ịdọta akụkọ ọrụ kwa ụbọchị ma nyere aka dee azịza production access site na ya.
  • Mmadụ ga-eme: ịgbakwunye Google Group nke ndị nnwale na track emechiri emechi na Play Console, ibipụta ntọhapụ ahụ, ịkwụ ụgwọ maka kredit (ịkwụ ụgwọ na-eme mgbe niile na ihe nchọgharị mmadụ) na ịpị Apply for production.
  • Onweghị onye nwere ike ime ya na-akpaghị aka: ụbọchị 14 nke ojiji n'ezie. Ndị mmadụ n'ezie na-eme ya na ekwentị Android nke ha, nke bụ isi ihe Iwu Google.

1. Mepụta token

  1. Banye na https://testerexchange.com/api.
  2. Mepụta token nkeonwe. Ọ na-amalite na tx_. Detuo ya ugbu a ma chekwaa ya dị ka paswọọdụ.
  3. Enwere ike ịkagbu token oge ọ bụla. Token nwere ike ịmefu kredit na mkpọsa, mana ọ nweghị ike ịkwụ ụgwọ, wepụta ego ma ọ bụ hichapụ akaụntụ gị.

2. Jikọọ MCP server

MCP server na-eji Streamable HTTP na https://vvrlxzbngxfxddywidoq.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp. Zipu token gị na header Authorization: Bearer tx_YOUR_TOKEN.

Claude Code: mee nke a otu ugboro na terminal gị.

claude mcp add --transport http tester-exchange https://vvrlxzbngxfxddywidoq.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp --header "Authorization: Bearer tx_YOUR_TOKEN"

Claude Desktop, Cursor, VS Code na ndị ọzọ nwere nhazi JSON: tinye server aha ya bụ tester-exchange nwere type http, URL dị n'elu, na ntinye headers nwere Authorization etinyere na Bearer tx_YOUR_TOKEN. Ebe faịlụ nhazi dị dabere na client gị; lelee akwụkwọ MCP ya.

Clients na-anabata naanị URL (ụfọdụ ChatGPT na connectors weebụ): akwụkwọ dị na https://testerexchange.com/agents/ depụtara ụdị URL maka ha. Na-enweghị token, ngwaọrụ ọha (ihe Tester Exchange bụ, ọnụ ahịa, ọnụọgụ ikpo okwu) ka na-arụ ọrụ.

3. Otu ngwaọrụ ị ga-amalite na ya: get_testers

Ọ dị gị mkpa ịmụ ndepụta ngwaọrụ niile. Isi ngwaọrụ bụ get_testers(package_name, name?). Otu oku na-atinye app gị, na-emepe nnwale ya, na-etinye kredit gị ma weghachite ọganihu gị yana nzọụkwụ naanị mmadụ nwere ike ime, dịka ịgbakwunye Google Group na Play Console. Kpọọ ya ọzọ oge ọ bụla iji lelee ọganihu.

Ngwaọrụ ndị ọzọ na-ekpuchi nkọwa: my_apps, campaign_report (ndị nnwale, ọrụ kwa ụbọchị, nzaghachi na data production-access), add_testers, set_campaign_status (kwụsị, gaa n'ihu ma ọ bụ mechie; a na-eweghachi kredit ejighị na ngụkọta gị), share_kit na buy_credits_links, nke na-enye njikọ checkout ka mmadụ kwụọ ụgwọ.

Prompts na-arụ ọrụ

  • "Get 15 testers for com.example.app and tell me what I need to do in Play Console."
  • "Check my Tester Exchange campaign for com.example.app. How many testers were active each day this week?"
  • "Pull the campaign report and draft my production access answers. Use only the real numbers."
  • "Close the campaign for com.example.app now that production access is approved."

Rịọ 15 ndị nnwale kama 12, ka onye hapụrụ ghara ịdọpụ gị n'okpuru opekata mpe nke Google.

HTTP API maka scripts na CI

Ọ bụrụ na ngwaọrụ gị anaghị asụ MCP, jiri HTTP endpoint: POST https://vvrlxzbngxfxddywidoq.supabase.co/rest/v1/rpc/agent_call. Ahụ ya bụ JSON nwere p_token (token tx_ gị), p_action (dịka help, my_apps ma ọ bụ start_campaign) na p_args (dịka package_name na ọnụọgụ ndị nnwale). Zipu apikey header ọha egosiri na akwụkwọ ahụ.

Buru ụzọ kpọọ action help: ọ na-eweghachi action niile na arguments ha. Schema zuru ezu dị na https://testerexchange.com/openapi.json, nke na-arụ ọrụ na ChatGPT custom GPT actions na ngwaọrụ OpenAPI ndị ọzọ.

Kaadị A2A agent

Maka nhazi agent-na-agent, Tester Exchange na-ebipụta kaadị A2A agent na https://testerexchange.com/.well-known/agent-card.json. Orchestrators na-akwado protocol A2A nwere ike ịchọpụta ihe agent na-enye na otu esi akpọ ya. Jiri otu token tx_ maka ihe ọ bụla jikọtara na akaụntụ gị.

Otu ọrụ zuru ezu, site na mmalite ruo ọgwụgwụ

  1. I butere .aab mbụ gị na track emechiri emechi na Play Console.
  2. Agent gị na-akpọ get_testers maka aha package gị ma gwa gị ka ị gbakwunye testerexchange-testers@googlegroups.com n'okpuru Google Groups na track emechiri emechi.
  3. Ị na-agbakwunye otu ahụ, chekwaa, ma hụ na e bipụtara ntọhapụ ahụ.
  4. Ọ bụrụ na ị nweghị kredit, agent na-enye gị njikọ checkout. Ị na-akwụ ụgwọ ($12.99 maka ndị nnwale 12, $14.99 maka 15, $22.99 maka 25) ma ọ bụ nweta kredit n'efu site n'ịnwale app ndị ọzọ na https://testerexchange.com/earn.
  5. Ndị nnwale n'ezie na-etinye ma jiri app gị ruo ụbọchị 14. Agent gị na-elele ọganihu kwa ụbọchị ma dọọ gị aka na ntị ma ndị nnwale na-arụ ọrụ na-ebelata.
  6. Na ụbọchị 15, agent na-adọta akụkọ mkpọsa ma dee azịza production access gị. Ị na-enyocha ha ma pịa Apply for production.

Ihe nchekwa

  • Hapụ tokens na git. Debe ha na ebe nchekwa nzuzo nke client gị ma ọ bụ na environment variable.
  • A na-echekwa tokens naanị dị ka hashes n'akụkụ anyị, ma ha dị oke 10 kwa akaụntụ. Kagbuo token ọ bụla ị na-eji ọzọ.
  • Gụọ ihe agent dere na fọm production gị tupu ị zipu ya. Google na-arịọ gị ka ị kọwaa nnwale gị n'eziokwu.

Ajụjụ a na-ajụkarị

AI agent nwere ike imecha nnwale ụbọchị 14 maka m?

Mba. Ụbọchị 14 chọrọ ndị mmadụ n'ezie na-eji app gị na ekwentị nke ha. Agents na-eme nhazi, nlekota na akụkọ.

Agent nwere ike ịkwụ ụgwọ maka ndị nnwale?

Mba. Ọ nwere ike weghachite njikọ checkout, mana ịkwụ ụgwọ na-eme mgbe niile na ihe nchọgharị mmadụ.

Kedu clients na-arụ ọrụ na MCP server?

Client ọ bụla na-akwado MCP dị anya site na Streamable HTTP nwere custom header, gụnyere Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor na VS Code. Ndị ọzọ nwere ike iji HTTP API.

Ebee ka m ga-enweta token?

Na https://testerexchange.com/api mgbe ị banyere. Tokens na-amalite na tx_ ma enwere ike ịkagbu ha oge ọ bụla.

Agent ọ na-agbanwe ihe ọ bụla na Play Console m?

Mba. Ọ nweghị ohere na Play Console gị. Ọ na-agwa gị nzọụkwụ ị ga-eme ebe ahụ.
