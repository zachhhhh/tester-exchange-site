Otu iwu ahụ, mana nwere ihe egwu pụrụ iche maka egwuregwu

Google na-ewere egwuregwu na app otu. Mee nnwale emechiri emechi nwere opekata mpe ndị nnwale 12 na-anọgide na opt-in ruo ụbọchị 14 n'usoro, wee rịọ maka production. Fọm production na-ajụ gbasara itinye aka ndị nnwale, nzaghachi na ihe ị gbanwere, ya mere nnwale ebe onweghị onye gwuru gafere tutorial na-eme ka azịza adịghị ike.

Ihe egwu ndị na-emetụta egwuregwu karịa app ndị ọzọ:

  • Ndakọrịta ngwaọrụ: ndị nnwale na-ahụ "your device isn't compatible" ma enweghị ike itinye ya.
  • Nha nbudata: nbudata mbụ buru ibu na data ekwentị na-eme ka ndị nnwale ghara ịmalite.
  • Ogologo oge ọgbakọ: ndị mmadụ agaghị egwu ọkwa nkeji 20 kwa ụbọchị ruo izu abụọ.
  • Oghere ọdịnaya: vertical slice nwere otu ọkwa na-agwụ ihe ị ga-eme site n'ụbọchị nke atọ.

Ntọala build ị ga-ebu ụzọ lelee

  • Unity: wuo Android App Bundle (.aab), jiri IL2CPP na ARM64 gbanyere (Google Play chọrọ nkwado 64-bit), ma tọọ API level kacha nta nke na-ewepụghị ekwentị karịa ihe ị chọrọ. Ọ bụrụ na build gị buru ibu, jiri Play Asset Delivery kama otu nbudata buru ibu.
  • Unreal Engine: kwakọba maka Android dị ka App Bundle nwere arm64, lelee target na minimum SDK levels na Project Settings, ma debe nbudata mbụ ka ọ dị obere site n'ịkpụga ọdịnaya nhọrọ n'ime asset packs.
  • Godot: jiri Android export template nwere Gradle build gbanyere ma bupụ dị ka AAB. Hụ na arm64-v8a so na ya, ma nwalee renderer na ekwentị dị ala, n'ihi na ụfọdụ ngwaọrụ na-ejikwa Compatibility renderer karịa Forward+ ma ọ bụ Mobile.
  • Engines niile: lekwasị anya na API level Google Play chọrọ ugbu a maka app ọhụrụ, ma binye aka site na Play App Signing.

Mgbe ị bugoro ya, meghee Device catalog na Play Console ka ịhụ ngwaọrụ ole build gị na-akwado. Ọ bụrụ na akụkụ buru ibu anaghị agụnye, dozie ya tupu ndị nnwale abịa.

Hazie nnwale ahụ

  1. Mepụta ma ọ bụ meghee track emechiri emechi: Test and release, wee Testing, wee Closed testing.
  2. Tinye mba ebe ndị nnwale gị bi.
  3. Tinye ndị nnwale site na ndepụta email ma ọ bụ Google Group. Na Tester Exchange, tinye testerexchange-testers@googlegroups.com.
  4. Bulite .aab, zipu ntọhapụ ahụ maka nyocha ma chere ruo mgbe ọ na-egosi Published.
  5. Kekọrịta njikọ opt-in. Oge mbụ, ọ nwere ike were awa ole na ole tupu ọ malite ịrụ ọrụ.

Ọ bụrụ na ndị nnwale kọọrọ "app not available", lee ntuziaka maka idozi nke ahụ. Ọ na-ekpuchi akaụntụ na-ezighi ezi, mba na track ndị a bipụtabeghị.

Ihe ị ga-agwa ndị nnwale ka ha gwuo

Nye ndị nnwale obere ebumnuche kwa ụbọchị nke dabara n'ime nkeji ole na ole. Egwuregwu n'ezie, dị iche iche bụ ihe na-enyere gị aka, ọ bụkwa ya ị ga-akọwa na fọm production n'oge ọzọ.

  • Ụbọchị 1 ruo 3: mechaa tutorial na ọkwa mbụ. Kọọ ihe ọ bụla na-adịghị nghọta.
  • Ụbọchị 4 ruo 7: gwuo otu obere ọgbakọ kwa ụbọchị; nwalee ntọala, olu, njikwa na ịkwụsị nwa oge.
  • Ụbọchị 8 ruo 11: nwalee build ọhụrụ nwere ndozi; gwuo ọkwa ọzọ ka ịhụ ma ọ na-adị mma.
  • Ụbọchị 12 ruo 14: gwuo ọdịnaya kacha sie ike dị ma kọọ ntahie, lag ma ọ bụ ọkụ batrị.

Ọ bụrụ na egwuregwu gị nwere ụgwọ ọrụ kwa ụbọchị, ọ bụ ihe ịma aka kwa ụbọchị dị mkpirikpi ma ọ bụ ụdị ngwa ngwa, gosi ndị nnwale ya. Ọ dabara n'ụkpụrụ kwa ụbọchị n'ụzọ okike.

Gosi itinye aka

  • Zipu opekata mpe otu mmelite n'oge nnwale ahụ, ka mma abụọ. Idozi ntahie a kọrọ bụ akụkọ kacha mma maka fọm production.
  • Jiri Android vitals na pre-launch report na Play Console ịchọta ntahie na ANRs na ngwaọrụ n'ezie.
  • Chịkọta nzaghachi edere n'ụdị dị mfe: ụdị ngwaọrụ, ihe ha mere, ihe mere.
  • Debe ndekọ nke ndị nnwale na-arụ ọrụ kwa ụbọchị, ọgbakọ, ahụhụ achọtara na build ndị e zipụrụ.

Ajụjụ a na-ajụkarị

Egwuregwu ọ chọkwara nnwale emechiri emechi?

Ee. Iwu ahụ na-emetụta app niile sitere na akaụntụ nkeonwe e mepụtara mgbe 13 Nọvemba 2023 gachara, gụnyere egwuregwu.

Egwuregwu m ọ nwere ike ịdị ka emechaghị n'oge nnwale emechiri emechi?

Ee, nnwale emechiri emechi bụ maka ọrụ na-aga n'ihu. Mana ndị nnwale chọrọ ọdịnaya zuru ezu iji gwuo izu abụọ, ị ga-aga n'ihu na-eme ya mma n'oge nnwale ahụ.

Gịnị mere ụfọdụ ndị nnwale enweghị ike itinye egwuregwu m?

Ọtụtụ mgbe ọ bụ ndakọrịta ngwaọrụ: ụdị Android kacha nta dị elu, enweghị nkwado 64-bit, ma ọ bụ atụmatụ hardware achọrọ. Lelee Device catalog na Play Console.

Ogologo oge ole ka ndị nnwale kwesịrị igwu kwa ụbọchị?

Google anaghị ekpebi ọnụọgụ. Obere ọgbakọ n'ezie kwa ụbọchị ka mma karịa otu ogologo ọgbakọ wee ghara ịdị.

Ọ bụrụ na egwuregwu gị buru ibu, ee. Nbudata mbụ ndị pere mpe pụtara na ndị nnwale ọzọ na-etinye ma bido igwu n'ezie.
