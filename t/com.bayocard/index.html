Questions

Is this allowed by Google?

Google requires real testers who use your app. Here, every tester is a real person on their own phone, and use is verified daily. Nothing is automated or faked.

What do I do in Play Console?

Upload your app to a closed test track. Then tap Connect Google Play and we add our testers group for you. Prefer to do it by hand? Add the group email we show under Testers → Google Groups.

Why a Google Group?

Google Play lets testers in either by a list of emails or by a Google Group. With our group you add one address once, and every tester we send can install. Members can't post, so there's no spam; it's only a guest list.

A tester sees “App not found” or “not available in your country”?

Closed tests are private, so Play shows this to anyone who isn't let in yet. Check three things: your closed test release is approved and the track is available in all countries; our group is added under Testers; the tester tapped “Become a tester” with the same Google account their Play Store uses. New changes can take a few hours to reach phones.

What if testers drop out?

Testers who never install or stop opening the app are released automatically, their slot reopens, and you're only charged for verified days. Ask for 14–15 testers to stay safe.

Does it work outside the US?

Yes. Testers and developers join from anywhere Google Play is available.

Can my AI agent use it?

Tester Exchange has an MCP server and a simple API, so Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other agents can find testers, run your campaign and pull the report for you.
