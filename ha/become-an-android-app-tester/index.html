Yadda ake zama mai gwajin manhajar Android

An sabunta Nuwamba 2026

Ba kwa buƙatar mukamin aiki ko na'ura ta musamman don gwada manhajoji. Duk wayar Android mai Google Account na iya shiga gwajin manhajojin da ba a fitar da su ga jama'a ba tukuna. Akwai hanyoyi uku na shiga: manhajojin shiga da wuri a Play Store, nau'ikan beta na manhajojin da kake amfani da su, da gwaje-gwajen rufaffu da masu haɓaka ke buƙatar ainihin masu gwaji a cikinsu. Na ƙarshen shi ne inda ake fi buƙatar masu gwaji, kuma za ka iya shiga ta browser ɗin wayarka a https://testerexchange.com/earn.

1. Manhajojin shiga da wuri da beta a Play Store

Shafin taimako na Google Play da kansa yana bayyana shirye-shirye biyu na jama'a (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7003180). Manhajojin shiga da wuri ba a fitar da su ba tukuna; manhajojin beta sabbin nau'ikan gwaji ne na manhajojin da aka riga aka fitar. Duka biyun na iya zama marasa kwanciyar hankali fiye da manhajoji na yau da kullum.

  • Manhajojin da ba a fitar ba: buɗe Play Store, je shafin “For you” ka duba ƙarƙashin “Apps in development”.
  • Wasannin da ba a fitar ba: buɗe shafin “New” ka duba ƙarƙashin “Be the first to play”.
  • Betas na manhajojin da kake da su: taɓa alamar profile ɗinka, sai Manage apps & devices, buɗe shafin manhajar da aka shigar ka taɓa “Join” ƙarƙashin “Join the beta”. Idan ba ka gan shi ba, wannan manhajar ba ta da beta na jama'a a yanzu.

Waɗannan shirye-shirye a buɗe suke ga kowa kuma wasu suna cika, don haka hanya ce mai kyau ta gwada sababbin abubuwa, amma babu wanda yake jiran ka zo.

2. Gwaje-gwajen rufaffu: inda ake buƙatar masu gwaji da gaske

Google Play yana buƙatar asusun masu haɓaka na kai da aka buɗe bayan 13 ga Nuwamba 2023 su gudanar da gwaji rufaffe da masu gwaji aƙalla 12 da suka ci gaba da kasancewa a cikin gwajin kwana 14 a jere kafin manhajar ta fita ga jama'a (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465).

Gwaje-gwajen rufaffu ba sa fitowa a Play Store. Kana shiga ta hanyar haɗin shiga daga mai haɓaka, don haka masu haɓaka na kansu dole ne su nemo masu gwaji da kansu, kuma da yawa ba su san masu amfani da Android 12 da za su taimaka ba. Wannan gibin shi ne mai gwaji yake cikewa.

3. Gwada manhajojin gwaji rufaffe da Tester Exchange

  • Babu ƙarin manhaja: buɗe https://testerexchange.com/earn a browser ɗin wayarka ta Android ka shiga da Google Account da Play Store ɗinka yake amfani da shi. Jerin manhajojinka yana cika da kansa.
  • Kusan minti ɗaya a rana ga kowace manhaja: shiga gwajin, shigar da manhajar daga Google Play, buɗe ta ka yi amfani da ita da gaske.
  • Credits: kana samun credit 1 ga kowace rana da ka buɗe manhajar da kake gwadawa. Credits suna samo masu gwaji don manhajarka, idan ka taɓa ƙirƙirar guda.
  • Kuɗi don gwaji kowace rana: kana kuma samun US$0.02 a kowace rana da ka buɗe dukkan manhajojin da ke jerinka (aƙalla manhajoji 3 a ranar). Cire kuɗi daga US$3 zuwa PayPal, Binance Pay ko Payoneer.

Wannan kuɗin aljihu ne, ba samun shiga ba: idan ka gwada kowace rana, yana kai kusan US$0.60 a wata.

Mataki-mataki

  1. A wayarka ta Android, buɗe https://testerexchange.com/earn ka shiga.
  2. Taɓa maɓallin kore. A karo na farko, zai nemi ka shiga ƙungiyar masu gwaji (sau ɗaya), sannan ka zama mai gwaji a Google Play.
  3. Shigar da manhajar daga Google Play ta hanyar haɗin gwajin ka buɗe ta.
  4. Yi amfani da ita minti ɗaya ko biyu kowace rana har tsawon kwana 14, a wani allo daban kowace rana.
  5. Aika taƙaitaccen ra'ayi idan wani abu ya lalace ko ya dame ka: abin da ka yi, abin da kake tsammani, abin da ya faru.
  6. Ci gaba da kasancewa a cikin gwajin ka bar manhajar a shigar har zuwa rana ta 14.

Bayar da ra'ayin da mai haɓaka zai iya amfani da shi

Ga manhajojin shiga da wuri da beta, Google Play yana da akwatin “Private feedback to developer” a shafin manhajar (alamar profile, Manage apps & devices, Beta). Mai haɓaka ne kaɗai yake ganinsa. Google kuma yana raba wasu bayanan amfani daga manhajojin gwaji da mai haɓaka, kamar bayanin na'ura da abubuwan da suka faru a cikin manhajar, don taimaka musu gyara matsaloli.

Ƙa'idoji

  • Wayarka ta gaske. Babu emulators, wayoyin cloud ko gonakin na'urori.
  • Asusu ɗaya ga kowane mutum. Ana cire ƙarin asusu.
  • Ka yi amfani da manhajar da gaske. Buɗewa da rufewa nan take ba gwaji ba ne.
  • Ka kammala abin da ka fara. Google yana ƙirga masu gwajin da suka ci gaba da kasancewa a cikin gwajin kwana 14 a jere, don haka barin gwajin da wuri yana cutar da mai haɓaka.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Shin ina buƙatar gogewa don zama mai gwajin manhaja?

A'a. Masu haɓaka suna buƙatar masu amfani na yau da kullum da za su buɗe manhajar kowace rana su faɗi lokacin da wani abu bai yi aiki ba.

Shin ana biyan masu gwajin manhaja?

Shiga da wuri da betas na Play Store ba sa biya. A Tester Exchange, kana samun US$0.02 a kowace rana da ka buɗe dukkan manhajojin da ke jerinka (aƙalla 3), kuma kowace ranar gwaji tana samar da credits ma.

Zan iya gwada manhajoji a iPhone?

Ba waɗannan ba. Gwaje-gwajen Google Play suna buƙatar wayar Android mai Google Account.

Shin shigar da manhajojin gwaji lafiya ne?

Kana shigar da su daga Google Play, don haka suna wucewa ta kariyar Google ta yau da kullum. Nau'ikan gwaji na iya zama marasa kwanciyar hankali, don haka kada ka shigar da bayanan biyan kuɗi ko bayanai masu muhimmanci a cikinsu.

Manhajoji nawa zan iya gwadawa a lokaci guda?

Da dama. Kowacce tana ɗaukar kusan minti ɗaya a rana, don haka saita tunatarwa ta yau da kullum ka bi jerinka.
