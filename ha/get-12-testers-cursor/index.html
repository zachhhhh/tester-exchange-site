Ka gina app ɗinka na Android da Cursor? Samo masu gwaji 12 na kwana 14

An sabunta a Nuwamba 2026

Cursor na iya rubutawa da gina app ɗinka na Android, amma ba zai iya samar da masu gwaji 12 na gaske da Google Play ke buƙata ba. Idan an ƙirƙiri asusun mai haɓaka na kai-da-kai bayan 13 Nuwamba 2023, dole ne ka gudanar da gwajin rufaffe da masu gwaji akalla 12 da suka shiga na kwana 14 a jere kafin ka nemi production. Wannan jagorar ta shafi gina bundle, fara gwajin rufaffe, samun masu gwaji (kyauta tukuna, biya daga baya) da haɗa Tester Exchange da Cursor don wakili ya yi aikin takardu.

1. Gina bundle na release (.aab)

  1. Ka roƙi wakilin Cursor ya saita sa hannun release a build.gradle.kts na module ɗinka (toshe signingConfigs mai storeFile, storePassword, keyAlias da keyPassword, an saita shi akan release build type). Kada ka ajiye kalmomin sirri a git (takardun build na Android: https://developer.android.com/build/building-cmdline).
  2. Gudanar da ./gradlew bundleRelease a terminal. Gradle yana gina bundle ɗin kuma yana sa masa hannu; .aab yawanci yana bayyana a app/build/outputs/bundle/release/, amma duba sakamakon build ɗinka.
  3. Ƙirƙiri keystore da keytool idan ba ka da shi, kuma ka yi masa ajiya. Ana ba da Google Play App Signing lokacin da ka ɗora release ɗinka na farko.

2. Fara gwajin rufaffe a Play Console

  1. Buɗe Test and release > Testing > Closed testing, sannan Create track (ko Manage track).
  2. Danna Create new release, ɗora .aab, ƙara sunan release da bayanai, Save as draft, Next, sannan Start rollout (matakai daga https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859348).
  3. Kammala ayyukan saitin app a Dashboard tukuna, in ba haka ba maɓallin release zai ci gaba da kasancewa a kashe.

3. Samo masu gwaji 12: kyauta tukuna, biya daga baya

  • Hanyar kyauta: buɗe https://testerexchange.com/earn a wayarka ka gwada apps da aka ba ka. Kowane app da ka gwada yana kawo mai gwaji zuwa naka.
  • Hanyar biya: biya sau ɗaya kuma ana ba da masu gwaji ta atomatik. Ana jera farashin yanzu a https://testerexchange.com/llms.txt.
  • Nemi masu gwaji 15 maimakon 12 don kada ɗaya da ya fita ya bar ka ƙasa da mafi ƙarancin da Google ke buƙata.

4. Haɗa Tester Exchange da Cursor (na zaɓi)

Da farko ƙirƙiri API key a https://testerexchange.com/api. Sannan sa wannan a .cursor/mcp.json a cikin project ɗinka, ko a ~/.cursor/mcp.json don amfani a ko'ina (wuraren fayil daga takardun MCP na Cursor, https://cursor.com/docs/context/mcp). Saita TESTER_EXCHANGE_KEY a muhallin shell ɗinka tukuna, don kada a commit ɗin key ɗin:

{"mcpServers":{"tester-exchange":{"url":"https://vvrlxzbngxfxddywidoq.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp","headers":{"Authorization":"Bearer ${env:TESTER_EXCHANGE_KEY}"}}}}

Cursor yana warware ${env:NAME} a filayen url da headers. Idan sabar ta kasa lodawa, buɗe panel ɗin Output ka zaɓi MCP Logs. Sabar MCP ce kawai ta Streamable HTTP ba tare da OAuth ba; kayan aikin jama'a (about, pricing, platform_stats) suna aiki ba tare da key ba.

5. Tambayi wakili

  • “Yi amfani da Tester Exchange don samo masu gwaji 15 na com.example.app ka gaya mini abin da dole ne in yi a Play Console.”
  • “Duba kamfen ɗina na Tester Exchange na com.example.app ka gaya mini masu gwaji nawa ne suka yi aiki jiya.”
  • “Ɗauko rahoton kamfen ɗin ka rubuta amsoshin neman damar production daga ainihin lambobin.”

Babban kayan aikin shi ne get_testers: yana ƙara app ɗin, yana buɗe gwajinsa kuma yana mayar da matakan da mutum ne kaɗai zai iya yi, kamar ƙara Google Group na masu gwaji a Play Console. Wakili ba zai iya biya ko danna Apply for production maimakonka ba.

Babu MCP? Yi amfani da HTTP API

POST zuwa https://vvrlxzbngxfxddywidoq.supabase.co/rest/v1/rpc/agent_call da apikey header na jama'a da aka jera a https://testerexchange.com/agents/ da kuma body {"p_token":"tx_YOUR_TOKEN","p_action":"get_testers","p_args":{"package_name":"com.example.app"}}. Schema yana a https://testerexchange.com/openapi.json. Ƙarin kayan aiki: https://testerexchange.com/guide/ai-builders/.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Cursor zai iya kammala gwajin kwana 14 maimakonna?

A'a. Kwana 14 suna buƙatar mutane na gaske masu amfani da app ɗinka a wayoyinsu na Android. Cursor na iya gina app ɗin ya kuma jagoranci saitin.

Shin Tester Exchange haɗin gwiwa ne na hukuma na Cursor?

A'a. Sabis ne mai zaman kansa wanda ke aiki da kowane client mai goyon bayan MCP na nesa ta HTTP, kuma Cursor yana yi.

Ina AAB yake shiga?

Ɗora shi zuwa release na gwajin rufaffe a Play Console, sannan ƙara Google Group ko jerin imel na masu gwajinka a wannan track ɗin.

Akwai hanyar kyauta ta samun masu gwaji 12?

Eh. Gwada apps na wasu masu haɓaka a https://testerexchange.com/earn; kowane app da ka gwada yana kawo mai gwaji zuwa naka.
