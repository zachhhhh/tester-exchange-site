Kwatance cikin sauri

Kuɗin sau ɗaya ne kowace manhaja sai idan an faɗi akasin haka.

  • Musaya kyauta (gwaji kyauta na Tester Exchange, manhajar Testers Community): $0. Kana gwada manhajojin wasu developers kuma kana samun kiredit don testers ga naka. Kuɗin shi ne lokacinka, kusan minti ɗaya a rana kowace manhaja. Gudun ya dogara da yawan developers masu aiki.
  • Sabis na biya (Tester Exchange, shirin biya na Testers Community): Tester Exchange yana caji $12.99 don testers 12, $14.99 don 15 ko $22.99 don 25. Testers Community ya lissafa shirinsa na biya daga $15. Testers suna farawa cikin sa'o'i zuwa rana ɗaya ko biyu.

  • Rubuce-rubucen Reddit, Discord, Telegram da "test for test": $0, amma kai ne za ka bi kowane tester, kuma da yawa suna tafiya kafin rana ta 14.

Musayar kyauta: yadda take aiki

A musayar kyauta, kowane developer tester ne kuma. Testers Community yana bayyana tsarin kiredit inda za ka gwada manhajoji uku don samun kiredit, sannan ka kashe su don samun testers don naka, kuma yana haɗa developers cikin fakiti da ke gudana tare na kusan makonni biyu. A Tester Exchange kana buɗe manhajojin wasu developers kowace rana kuma kana samun kiredit; developers da ke gwada na wasu suna samun testers ga manhajarsu da wuri.

Musayar kyauta tana aiki mafi kyau idan ka fara da wuri, domin kana samun testers ta hanyar gwaji kuma kwanaki 14 suna farawa ne kawai bayan duk testers ɗinka sun shiga. Ka tsammaci buɗe kowace manhajar da aka ba ka sau ɗaya a rana.

A Tester Exchange, matakin yau da kullum shi ne taɓawa ɗaya kowace manhaja a browser na wayarka; ba a buƙatar ƙarin manhaja.

Sabis na biya: abin da za ka duba

Dokar da Google ya bayyana ta yarda da biya, amma gwajin yana taimakawa ne kawai idan testers mutane ne na gaske kuma suna ci gaba da opt-in. Kafin ka biya, duba cewa testers suna amfani da wayoyi na gaske, testers na ajiya suna maye gurbin masu tafiya, kuma kana samun shaidar amfani ta yau da kullum maimakon alkawari kawai.

Sharuɗɗan mayar da kuɗi sun bambanta. Testers Community ya ce yana mayar da kuɗi idan Google ya ƙi manhajar bayan zagayensa. Sayayyar Tester Exchange na ƙarshe ne: kiredit ba sa komawa kuɗi kuma kiredit da ba a yi amfani da su ba suna nan a balance ɗinka.
