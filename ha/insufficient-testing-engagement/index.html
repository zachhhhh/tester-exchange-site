Dalilin da Google ke cewa manhajarka tana buƙatar ƙarin gwaji

Shiga (opt in) ba ɗaya yake da gwadawa ba. Google yana iya ganin testers nawa suka sauke manhajar, sau nawa suka buɗe ta, da ko ka fitar da sabuntawa a lokacin gwajin. Gwajin da mutane suka sauke sau ɗaya kuma ba su dawo ba yana kama da cike fom ne kawai, ba gwaji na gaske ba.

  • Ƙarancin aiki: testers sun sauke manhajar amma ba safai suke buɗe ta ba, ko ba su taɓa buɗe ta ba.
  • Testers masu aiki kaɗan ne: mutane sun bar gwajin, kuma ka faɗi ƙasa da testers 12 da suka shiga.
  • Babu ci gaba: babu sababbin builds kuma babu martani da ake gani ga ra'ayoyi a cikin kwanaki 14.
  • Amsoshi marasa ƙarfi: amsoshi marasa bayyani a takardar tambayoyin production, babu lambobi kuma babu misalan ra'ayoyi.

Tsarin da zai wuce a ƙoƙari na biyu

  1. Ci gaba da closed test ɗin. Kada ka goge track ko ka cire testers da har yanzu suke aiki.
  2. Ɗauki testers na gaske 14–15 da za su buɗe manhajar kowace rana, don kada fitar 'yan kaɗan ta kai ka ƙasa da 12.
  3. Ba testers ayyuka ɗaya ko biyu bayyanannu kowane mako ("ƙirƙiri account, ƙara abu, canza saiti").
  4. Nemi ra'ayi a rubuce, gyara aƙalla wasu daga cikin matsalolin da aka bayar da rahoto, ka ɗora sababbin builds a closed track a lokacin gwajin.
  5. Riƙe rajista: testers masu aiki kowace rana, jimillar kwanakin-testers, ra'ayoyin da aka samu, da abin da ka canza.
  6. Bayan ƙarin kwanaki 14 na amfani na gaske, sake nema ka amsa kowace tambaya da waɗannan lambobi.

Nuna hujjar aiki

Amsoshinka su kasance kamar gajeren rahoton gwaji: testers nawa, kwanaki nawa suka yi amfani da manhajar, me suka bayar da rahoto, da wane builds suka gyara shi. Tester Exchange yana rubuta tabbataccen amfani na yau da kullum ga kowane tester kuma yana tsara waɗannan amsoshi daga bayananka na gaske, don kada ka sake gina su daga ƙwaƙwalwa.

Abin da bai kamata ka yi ba

  • Kada ka yi amfani da ƙarin wayoyinka ko accounts ɗinka a matsayin testers. Accounts da yawa a na'ura ɗaya ba a ƙirga su a matsayin gwaji na gaske.
  • Kada ka biya "testers" da ke sauke kawai ba sa taɓa buɗe manhajar. Wannan ne ainihin abin da ake ƙi.
  • Kada ka kwafi amsoshi na gama-gari daga intanet. Yi amfani da lambobinka da ra'ayoyinka.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Dole in sake fara kwanaki 14?

Closed test ɗinka yana nan, amma Google yana son ganin ƙarin gwaji na gaske kafin ka sake nema. Shirya aƙalla ƙarin kwanaki 14 na amfani mai aiki.

Sau nawa testers za su buɗe manhajar?

Google bai wallafa adadi ba. Amfani na yau da kullum daga yawancin testers shi ne tsari mafi aminci, kuma shi ne Tester Exchange ke tabbatarwa.

Shin ra'ayoyi suna da muhimmanci?

Eh. Takardar tambayoyin tana tambaya abin da testers suka faɗa da abin da ka canza. Ra'ayoyi na gaske da gyare-gyare na gaske suna sa amsoshinka su zama abin yarda.
