Tambayoyi game da closed test ɗinka

  • Yaya sauƙin ɗaukar testers ya kasance? Faɗi daga ina suka zo (abokai, al'ummar developers, musayar testers) da nawa suka shiga, suka sauke kuma suka zauna.
  • Bayyana aikin da ka samu. Ba da lambobi: testers masu aiki kowace rana, jimillar kwanakin-testers, da tsawon lokacin gwajin. Ambaci yadda ka kiyaye testers su yi aiki.
  • Taƙaita ra'ayoyin. Ambaci jigogi biyu ko uku takamaimai ("yin rajista ya rikitar", "manhajar ta rufe kanta lokacin juya allo") ka faɗi yadda ka tattara su.

Tambayoyi game da manhajarka

  • Su wane ne masu sauraron da aka nufa? Ka zama takamaimai: su wane ne kuma wace matsala suke da ita.
  • Ta yaya manhajarka ke ba da amfani? Jimla ɗaya ko biyu kan abin da masu amfani za su iya yi da ba su iya yi a da ba.
  • Shigarwa nawa kake tsammani a shekarar farko? Zaɓi kewayon gaskiya. Babu wanda ke tsammanin babban adadi daga sabon developer.

Tambayoyi game da shirin production

  • Wane canje-canje ka yi bisa gwajin? Jera gyare-gyare da ingantawa, ka ambaci builds da ka ɗora a lokacin gwajin.
  • Ta yaya ka yanke shawarar cewa manhajarka a shirye take? Yi amfani da ma'auni: manyan matakai suna aiki, an gyara rufewar manhaja da aka bayar da rahoto, testers sun yi amfani da ita a duk tsawon lokacin.

Misalin amsa (aiki)

"Testers 14 sun sauke manhajar daga closed track kuma sun yi amfani da ita a cikin kwanaki 15. A matsakaici testers 12 suna aiki kowace rana, jimillar kwanakin-testers 186, tare da kusan mintuna 4 a kowane zama. Mun aika wa testers gajeren jerin ayyuka kowane mako kuma mun roƙe su su bayar da rahoton duk abin da ya rikitar. Testers 13 sun kammala cikakken lokacin."
