Samu masu gwajin Google Play kyauta ta gwada manhajojin wasu masu haɓakawa

An sabunta a Nuwamba 2026

Google Play na buƙatar sabbin asusun masu haɓakawa na kai da kai su gudanar da rufaffen gwaji da masu gwaji akalla 12 waɗanda suka ci gaba da kasancewa a ciki na kwana 14 a jere. Idan ba za ka iya biyan masu gwaji ba, za ka iya samun su: ka gwada manhajojin wasu masu haɓakawa daga wayar Android ɗinka, su ma su gwada naka. Wannan jagorar ta nuna daidai yadda kiredit ke haɗuwa, tsawon lokacin da take ɗauka, da yadda za ka sami masu gwajinka da wuri.

Dokar da dole ka cika

Taimakon Google Play Console ya ce asusun masu haɓakawa na kai da kai da aka buɗe bayan 13 ga Nuwamba 2023 dole su gudanar da rufaffen gwaji da masu gwaji akalla 12 da suka kasance a ciki ba tare da yankewa ba na akalla kwana 14 kafin su nemi izinin fitar da manhaja ga jama'a (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465).

Sauran masu haɓakawa suna da irin wannan matsala, saboda haka gwada manhajojin juna na aiki: kowane mai haɓakawa a Tester Exchange na buƙatar masu gwaji, kuma kowannensu zai iya zama mai gwaji.

Yadda kiredit ke aiki

  • Za ka sami kiredit 1 a duk ranar da ka buɗe manhajar da kake gwadawa na akalla daƙiƙa 30.
  • Mai gwaji ɗaya na kashe kiredit 15: kwanakin gwaji 14 da kuma kyautar ra'ayi.
  • Masu gwaji 12 na kashe kiredit 180, masu gwaji 15 kuma 225. Neman 14–15 ya fi aminci, domin wasu masu gwaji na ficewa.
  • Sabbin mambobi na fara da kiredit 14 (mambobi 500 na farko na samun 90).
  • Ana riƙe kiredit ne, ba a kashe shi tun da farko ba: ana biyan mai gwaji ne kawai bisa kwanakin da ya buɗe manhajarka, kuma kiredit ɗin da ba a yi amfani da shi ba na komawa ma'aunin ka.

Tsawon lokacin da take ɗauka

Kowace manhaja na ɗaukar kusan minti ɗaya a rana, kuma za ka iya gwada manhajoji da dama a lokaci ɗaya. Idan ka buɗe manhajoji 12 a rana, za ka sami kiredit 12 a rana: tare da kiredit 14 da kake farawa da su, hakan ya zama 180, wanda ya isa masu gwaji 12, cikin kusan kwana 14. Idan ka buɗe ƙasa da haka, za ta ɗauki lokaci mai tsawo; yawan manhajojin da za ka samu a kowace rana ya dogara da yawan waɗanda ke buƙatar masu gwaji.

Ba sai ka jira har sai ka kai 180 ba: masu gwajin da ka kawo da kanka ta shafin rabawa na manhajarka ba sa kashe kiredit, don haka kiredit kake buƙata ne kawai ga wuraren da ba su cika ba.

Samu masu gwajinka da wuri: ci gaba da gwaji

  • Masu mallaka da ke gwaji na gaba. Manhajojin da masu su suka buɗe akalla wasu manhajoji 3 yau ko jiya na samun masu gwaji kafin manhajojin da masu su ba sa gwaji.
  • Manhajojin kyauta na samun sababbin masu gwaji ne kawai yayin da mai su ke gwaji. Idan ka daina buɗe wasu manhajoji, manhajarka za ta daina ci gaba a layi.
  • Minti ɗaya a rana ya isa. Buɗe manhajojin da ke jerinka, ka bar kowacce a buɗe na daƙiƙa 30, ka dawo gobe.

Samu da sauri: gayyaci mutane

Kowace manhaja na da shafin rabawa. Ka sanya hanyar haɗinsa a wuraren da kake neman masu gwaji. Duk wanda ya shiga ta hanyar haɗinka kuma ya kammala ranar gwaji ɗaya zai ba ka kiredit na kyauta 30, wato masu gwaji biyu, kuma zai iya gwada manhajarka nan take.

Mataki-mataki

  1. Shirya rufaffen gwajinka a Play Console, ka ajiye hanyar haɗin shiga a hannu.
  2. A wayar Android ɗinka, buɗe https://testerexchange.com/earn a burauza ka shiga da asusun Google da Play Store ɗinka ke amfani da shi.
  3. Danna koren maɓallin don shiga gwajin manhaja ta farko, ka sanya ta daga Google Play, ka buɗe ta na daƙiƙa 30. Ka dawo ka maimaita da manhaja ta gaba.
  4. Ƙara manhajarka ta kanka ka raba shafinta da duk wanda zai iya taimakawa.
  5. Yi haka kowace rana. Idan kiredit ɗinka ya isa, fara kamfen ɗinka; ci gaba da gwaji don manhajarka ta kasance kusa da gaba.
  6. Bayan kwana 14 tare da masu gwaji 12 ko fiye a ciki, nemi izinin fitar da manhaja ga jama'a a Play Console.

Dokokin gaskiya

  • Wayarka ta gaske. Babu na'urorin kwaikwayo, wayoyin gajimare ko gonakin na'urori.
  • Asusu ɗaya ga kowane mutum. Ana cire ƙarin asusu.
  • Yi amfani da manhajar da gaske. Duba wani allo daban kowace rana, ka aika ra'ayi idan wani abu ya lalace.
  • Ka tsaya cikin cikakkun kwana 14. Ficewa da wuri na cutar da mai haɓakawa, kamar yadda zai cutar da kai.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Da gaske kyauta ne?

Eh. Kana biya da lokacinka: kusan minti ɗaya a rana ga kowace manhajar da ka gwada. Biya na zaɓi ne kuma yana ƙara sauri ne kawai.

Kiredit nawa nake buƙata don masu gwaji 12?

Kiredit 180: 15 ga kowane mai gwaji (kwana 14 da kyautar ra'ayi). Masu gwaji 15 na buƙatar 225.

Tsawon lokaci nawa zan ɗauka kafin in same su?

Kusan kwana 14 idan ka buɗe manhajoji 12 a rana, tare da kiredit 14 da kake farawa da su. Gayyata na ƙara sauri: kowane wanda aka gayyata ya kammala ranar gwaji ɗaya na ba ka kiredit 30.

Ina buƙatar wayar Android?

Eh. Waɗannan rufaffun gwaje-gwajen Google Play ne, don haka za ka gwada a wayar Android da ka shiga da asusun Google da Play Store ɗinka ke amfani da shi.

Idan na daina gwaji fa?

Kana riƙe kiredit ɗin da ka samu, amma manhajar kyauta na samun sababbin masu gwaji ne kawai yayin da mai ita ke gwaji, don haka gwajinka zai ja baya.
