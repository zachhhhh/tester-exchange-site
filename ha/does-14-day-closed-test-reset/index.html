Abin da Google ke ƙirgawa a zahiri

Buƙatar Google ga asusun kashin kai da aka ƙirƙira bayan 13 Nuwamba 2023 ita ce closed test da aƙalla testers 12 da suka ci gaba da opt in na aƙalla kwana 14 ba tare da tsayawa ba. Bayanai uku a kalmomin Google suna da muhimmanci a nan:

  • Dole kwanaki 14 su zama a jere. Babu dakatarwa da ci gaba.
  • Testers da suka yi opt in, suka yi gwaji ƙasa da kwana 14, sannan suka yi opt out ba a ƙirga su ba a cikin buƙatar.
  • Dole aƙalla testers 12 su kasance a opt in lokacin da kake nema production access.

Ka yi tunani a kan kowane tester: kowane mutum yana da agogonsa da ke farawa lokacin da ya yi opt in. Ka cancanta idan aƙalla mutane 12 sun yi opt in na kwana 14 a jere kowannensu, kuma har yanzu suna opt in.

Yanayi na gama-gari

  • Kana da 15, ɗaya ya fita a rana ta 8. Sauran 14 suna ci gaba da ƙirgawa. A rana ta 14 har yanzu kana da testers 14 masu cancanta. Babu matsala.
  • Kana da 12 daidai, ɗaya ya fita a rana ta 8. Yanzu mutane 11 ne kaɗai za su iya kaiwa kwana 14. Kana buƙatar wanda zai maye gurbinsa, kuma kwanaki 14 na wannan mutumin suna farawa a ranar da ya yi opt in. Ranar farko da za ka iya nema tana komawa baya da kusan mako ɗaya.
  • Tester ya yi opt out sannan ya sake opt in. Google ya ce yin opt out da sake opt in yana sake fara ƙirgar kwanakinsa na a jere.
  • Tester yana ci gaba da opt in amma ya daina buɗe manhajar. Har yanzu ana ƙirga shi cikin 12 don dokar opt-in, amma yana cutar da kai a engagement, wanda Google ke tambaya game da shi a fom ɗin production.
  • Tester ya goge manhajar amma bai yi opt out ba. Wataƙila har yanzu yana opt in, amma ba ya gwaji. Ɗauke shi kamar yanayin da ya gabata.

Me yasa ake neman testers 15, ba 12 ba

Ficewa abu ne na al'ada. Mutane suna canza waya, suna mantawa, wurin ajiya yana ƙare ko sha'awa tana ƙarewa. Da 12 daidai, kowane mai fita guda kafin rana ta 14 yana jinkirta ranar neman ka. Da 15, za ka iya rasa mutane uku kuma ka nema a kan lokaci.

Hakan yana taimakawa engagement ma. Google yana duba yadda testers suka yi amfani da manhajar. Idan uku cikin goma sha biyar sun yi shiru, sauran goma sha biyu har yanzu suna nuna amfani na kullum. Idan uku cikin goma sha biyu sun yi shiru, kwata na gwajinka yana kama da ba shi da aiki.

A Tester Exchange, testers 15 suna kashe $14.99 idan aka kwatanta da $12.99 don 12, wanda inshora ne mai arha daga ƙarin makon jira.

Abin da za ka yi a tsakiyar gwaji

  1. Duba adadin testers masu opt in a Play Console kowace rana biyu ko uku.
  2. Idan ka ga raguwa, ƙara masu maye gurbi nan take. Kowace rana da ka jira rana ce da aka ƙara a kwanakinsu 14.
  3. Aika saƙo ga testers masu shiru da aiki guda ɗaya bayyananne ("gwada sabon allon bincike a build 1.0.3") maimakon tunatarwa ta gaba ɗaya.
  4. Fitar da sabuntawa yayin gwajin. Sabon build yana ba testers dalilin sake buɗe manhajar.
  5. Kada ka nema har sai kana da aƙalla testers 12 da kowannensu ya wuce kwana 14 a jere kuma har yanzu suna opt in. Ragi na biyu ko uku ya fi aminci.

Abubuwan da ba sa sake fara ci gabanka

  • Ɗora sababbin builds a closed track ɗaya. Testers suna ci gaba da opt in kuma suna samun sabuntawar.
  • Ƙara ƙarin testers. Sababbin mutane suna fara kwanaki 14 nasu; testers na yanzu suna riƙe nasu.
  • Gyara bayanin store listing ko release notes.

Abubuwan da za a guje wa: cire jerin email ko Google Group daga track ɗin, ko share track ɗin. Testers na iya rasa damar shiga, kuma ba a bayyana yadda Google ke ɗaukar opt-in ɗinsu a lokacin ba, don haka kada ka yi haɗari.
