Google kuma yana dubawa ko waɗannan testers sun yi amfani da manhajarka da gaske. Wannan jagora yana bayyana dukkan tsarin kuma yana nuna hanyar samun testers na gaske kyauta.

Ka'ida: testers 12 × kwana 14

Google Play yana buƙatar sabbin asusun developer na kashin kai su yi closed test kafin su nemi production access. Sharaɗin shi ne:

  • Aƙalla testers 12 sun shiga (opt in) closed testing track ɗinka
  • Suna ci gaba da kasancewa a ciki na aƙalla kwana 14 ba tare da tsayawa ba
  • Bayan haka, kana neman production a Play Console kuma ka amsa tambayoyi game da gwajinka
  • Asusun kamfanoni (masu lambar D-U-N-S) ba su shafa ba

Adadin ya kasance testers 20 har zuwa Disamba 2024, lokacin da Google ya rage shi zuwa 12. Ƙirgar kwana 14 tana ƙirga kwanakin da ke da testers 12 ko fiye a ciki ne kawai, don haka idan testers suka fita ka koma 11, za ka rasa ci gaban da ka samu.

Me yasa ake ƙin manhajoji bayan kwana 14

Developers da yawa suna kai kwana 14 amma har yanzu ana ƙin su da saƙo game da rashin isasshen gwaji ko engagement. Google yana duba ko testers sun buɗe kuma sun yi amfani da manhajar da gaske, ba wai kawai sun shiga ba.

Saukewa daga mutanen da ba sa taɓa buɗe manhajar, ko waya ɗaya da ake amfani da asusu da yawa a kanta, ba a ɗaukar su a matsayin gwaji na gaske. Abin da ke taimakawa: testers da ke buɗe manhajar a kai a kai cikin kwanaki 14, ra'ayoyin da za ka iya bayyanawa, da sababbin builds da ke nuna ka yi aiki da su.

Saita closed test a Play Console

  1. Buɗe Play Console → manhajarka → Test and release → Testing → Closed testing, ka ƙirƙiri track (ko ka yi amfani da Alpha track na asali).
  2. A ƙarƙashin Testers, zaɓi Google Group ko jerin email. Google Group shi ne mafi sauƙi: duk wanda ya shiga group ɗin zai iya shiga gwajin.
  3. Ɗora app bundle a track ɗin, zaɓi ƙasashe, ka aika release don a duba shi.
  4. Da zarar an amince, kwafi link na opt-in (https://play.google.com/apps/testing/your.package.name). Testers suna buɗe shi, su taɓa Become a tester, sannan su sauke daga Google Play.
  5. Duba shafin Testers a Play Console don ganin mutane nawa suka shiga.

Inda za ka samu testers 12 na gaske

  • Abokai da iyali. Kyauta ne, amma yawancin mutane ba su da masu amfani da Android 12 da za su buɗe manhaja kowace rana har sati biyu.
  • Al'ummomin gwaji-da-gwaji. Developers suna gwada manhajojin juna. Kyauta ne, kuma testers sun fahimci abin da ake buƙata. Haɗarin shi ne mutanen da ke shiga amma ba sa taɓa buɗe manhajarka.
  • Ayyukan testers na biya. Yawanci US$10–35 kan kowace manhaja. Da sauri ne, amma kana dogara cewa testers ɗin na gaske ne kuma suna aiki.
  • Tester Exchange. Musayar gwaji-da-gwaji kyauta inda ake tabbatar da amfani na yau da kullum ta manhajar taimako a wayar kowane tester, kuma ana biyan testers kiredit ne kawai don kwanakin da aka tabbatar.

Yadda Tester Exchange ke aiki

  1. Saka manhajarka: liƙa link ɗinta na Google Play a /start.
  2. Zaɓi yadda za ka samu testers: kyauta (ana ba wayarka manhajojin da za ka gwada, minti ɗaya a rana, kuma kowace manhaja da ka gwada tana kawo tester ɗaya ga naka) ko ka tsallake gwaji da kiredit (manhajojin da aka biya suna samun testers da farko).
  3. Sauke manhajar Android ta Tester Exchange a wayarka ka ba da izinin Usage access. Tana duba manhajojin da ka shiga kawai.
  4. Ƙara testers ɗinka a closed test ɗinka a Play Console.
  5. Testers suna buɗe manhajarka kowace rana har kwana 14, kuma manhajar taimako a wayoyinsu tana tabbatar da kowace rana.
  6. Bayan kwana 14, buɗe rahotonka: testers masu aiki kowace rana, ra'ayoyi, da daftarin amsoshi don tambayoyin production access na Play Console.

Ka sa testers su ci gaba da aiki har kwana 14

  • Ka nemi testers 14–15, ba daidai 12 ba, don idan wasu kaɗan suka fita ƙirgarka ba za ta sake farawa ba.
  • Gaya wa testers abin da za su gwada: aiki ɗaya ko biyu bayyanannu, ba "don Allah ka gwada manhajata" ba.
  • Fitar da aƙalla sabuntawa ɗaya yayin gwajin, ka gyara abin da testers suka bayar da rahoto.
  • Nemi ra'ayi a rubuce. Za ka buƙaci taƙaita shi idan ka nemi production.

Nemi production access

Bayan kwana 14, je Play Console → Dashboard → Apply for production. Google yana tambaya game da yadda ka samo testers, yadda suka yi amfani da manhajarka, ra'ayoyin da ka samu, abin da ka canza, da dalilin da yasa manhajar ta shirya. Amsa da lambobi na gaske: testers nawa, kwana nawa suka yi amfani da manhajar, me suka bayar da rahoto, da waɗanne gyare-gyare ka fitar. Bincike yawanci yana ɗaukar har kwana 7.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Dole testers 12 su yi amfani da manhajar kowace rana?

Google yana buƙatar su ci gaba da kasancewa a ciki na kwana 14 kuma yana duba engagement. Amfani na gaske a kai a kai shi ne ke hana ƙin amincewa saboda "rashin isasshen gwaji".

Zan iya amfani da wayoyina ko asusuna a matsayin testers?

Google yana iya gane lokacin da asusu da yawa suke amfani da na'ura ɗaya, kuma ba a ƙirga hakan a matsayin gwaji na gaske ba. Yi amfani da mutane na gaske a wayoyinsu.

Idan tester ya fita, kwanaki 14 suna sake farawa?

Ƙirgar tana ci gaba ne kawai a kwanakin da ke da aƙalla testers 12 a ciki. Nemi ƙarin testers biyu ko uku don waɗanda suka fita kar su tsayar da kai.

Tester Exchange kyauta ne?

Eh. Gwaji kyauta ne: kowace manhaja da ka gwada a wayarka tana kawo tester ga naka. Sabbin mambobi suna samun kiredit na farawa kyauta. Sayen kiredit ba dole ba ne kuma yana ba ka damar tsallake gwaji; manhajojin da aka biya suna samun testers da farko.
