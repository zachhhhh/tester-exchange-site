Dokar ɗaya ce ga kowane kayan aiki

Google yana duba account ɗinka na developer, ba kayan aikinka ba. Manhajojin da aka yi da MIT App Inventor, Kodular, Niotron, Sketchware, Thunkable, FlutterFlow, Flutter, React Native, Expo, Unity ko Godot duk suna bi ta closed test iri ɗaya.

Idan ka buɗe account na developer na kai bayan 13 ga Nuwamba 2023, kana buƙatar aƙalla testers 12 da suka shiga (opt in) har kwanaki 14 a jere kafin ka iya neman damar production. Accounts na ƙungiya ba su da wannan sharaɗi.

Mataki na 1: fitar da release build da kayan aikinka zai iya sa hannu a kai

  1. Fitar da Android App Bundle (.aab). Google Play yana buƙatar .aab don sababbin manhajoji. Yawancin kayan aiki da injuna za su iya fitar da shi; idan naka APK kawai yake bayarwa, duba takardunsa don zaɓin App Bundle.
  2. Ka adana signing key ɗinka lafiya. Kayan aiki kamar App Inventor da Kodular suna ƙirƙirar maka keystore. Sauke kwafinsa na ajiya. Kana buƙatar key ɗin nan dai don kowane sabuntawa.
  3. Zaɓi sunan package a hankali. Ba za a taɓa iya canza shi ba da zarar manhajar ta hau Google Play.
  4. Cika matakin target API. Google Play yana karɓar builds ne kawai da ke nufin sabon sigar Android. Idan an ƙi ɗorawa saboda matakin target API, sabunta kayan aikinka ko injinka ka sake fitarwa.

Mataki na 2: saita closed test

  1. A Play Console, buɗe Test and release → Testing → Closed testing ka ƙirƙiri track.
  2. Ƙara testers ta Google Group maimakon jerin email: testers suna shiga group ɗin da kansu, kuma ba za ka sake liƙa email ba.
  3. Ɗora .aab ɗinka a closed track, ƙara bayanan release, ka aika don a duba.
  4. Kwafi link na opt-in. Testers suna shiga group ɗin, su buɗe link na opt-in, su taɓa "Become a tester", sannan su sauke daga Google Play.

Mataki na 3: nemo testers 12 da za su zauna kwanaki 14

Sunaye goma sha biyu a jeri ba su isa ba. Google yana duba ko testers sun yi amfani da manhajarka da gaske, kuma kwanaki 14 suna ƙirguwa ne kawai idan aƙalla testers 12 suna cikin gwajin. Ɗauki mutane 14 ko 15, don kada fitar mutum ɗaya ko biyu ta tsayar da agogo.

  • Abokai da iyali masu wayoyin Android: kyauta ne, amma sau da yawa suna daina buɗe manhajar bayan 'yan kwanaki.
  • Al'ummomi da dandalin kayan aikinka: sauran masu amfani sun fahimci dokar, kuma da yawa suna musayar gwaji.
  • Musayar gwaji: a Tester Exchange ana ba wayarka manhajojin wasu developers don gwadawa, minti ɗaya a rana, kuma kowace da ka gwada tana kawo tester ɗaya ga manhajarka.

Mataki na 4: kiyaye testers su yi aiki, ka fitar da sabuntawa

  • Ba testers ƙaramin aiki ɗaya kowace rana, don su sami dalilin buɗe manhajar.
  • Ɗora sabon build mai gyare-gyare kusan rana ta 7, ka faɗi abin da ya canza. Da waɗannan kayan aiki abu ne mai sauri: fitarwa, ɗorawa a closed track ɗin nan, shi ke nan.
  • Nemi ra'ayi a rubuce, ka gyara abin da za ka iya. Hakan kuma yana ba ka amsoshi na gaske don tambayoyin production.

Mataki na 5: nemi damar production

Bayan kwanaki 14, nemi damar production a dashboard na Play Console. Google yana tambaya yadda ka samo testers, yadda suka yi amfani da manhajar da abin da ka canza bisa ra'ayoyinsu. Amsa da lambobi na gaske daga gwajinka. Google yawanci yana amsawa cikin kusan mako ɗaya.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Shin manhajojin da aka yi da App Inventor ko Kodular ma suna buƙatar testers 12?

Eh. Sharaɗin ya dogara ne da account ɗinka na developer, ba kayan aikin ba. Sababbin accounts na kai suna buƙatar testers 12 da suka shiga har kwanaki 14, ko da da me aka gina manhajar.

Testers ɗina za su iya shigar da APK da na aika musu maimakon haka?

A'a. Shigarwa daga closed test a Google Play ne kawai ake ƙirgawa. Dole testers su shiga da link daga Play Console su sauke daga Play Store.

Ina buƙatar sabon build don fara closed test?

Kana buƙatar aƙalla build ɗaya da aka ɗora a closed track. Ɗora build na biyu a lokacin gwajin mai gyare-gyare daga ra'ayoyin testers kyakkyawan tunani ne.

Akwai hanyar samun testers 12 kyauta?

Eh. Musayar gwaji kyauta ce: kai kana gwada manhajojin wasu developers, su kuma suna gwada naka. Tester Exchange yana tabbatar da amfani na yau da kullum a wayar kowane tester, don haka gwajin na gaske ne.
