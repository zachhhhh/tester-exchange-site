An sabunta Nuwamba 2026

Ka yi closed test ɗinka, ka nemi production, ka jira kusan mako ɗaya, sai Google ya ce a'a. Yana jin kamar farawa daga farko, amma yawanci ba haka ba ne. A mafi yawan lokuta closed track ɗinka, testers ɗinka da manhajarka duka suna nan, kuma Google yana son ganin ƙarin gwaji ko mafi kyau kafin ya bar manhajar ta fita ga jama'a. Wannan jagora yana bayyana abin da shawarar ke nufi kuma yana ba ka tsari na aikace-aikacen gaba.

Abin da ƙin ke nufi

Help Center na Google ya ce kana iya buƙatar ci gaba da gudanar da closed test ɗinka kafin ka sami production access. Dalilan da ya lissafa sun haɗa da kasancewar ƙasa da testers 12 masu opt in da testers ba su yi isasshen engagement da manhajarka ba yayin gwajin.

Don haka ƙi yawanci ba gargaɗin policy ba ne a kan asusunka. Google ne yake cewa shaidar gwajinka ba ta gamsar ba tukuna. Karanta email ɗin da saƙon da ke Play Console a hankali: yawanci yana nuna gwaji, engagement ko amsoshin da ka bayar.

  • "More testing required" ko makamancin haka: ci gaba da closed test ɗin ka tara ƙarin amfani na gaske.
  • Ƙasa da testers 12 masu opt in: wasu testers sun fita. Nemo ƙarin kafin ka sake nema.
  • Matsalolin policy ko abun ciki: batu ne daban. Duba shafin Policy status ka gyara waɗannan da farko, ko sabon aikace-aikace ba zai taimaka ba.

Da farko, kada ka yi waɗannan

  • Kada ka share closed track ko ka cire testers da har yanzu suke aiki. Kana buƙatar su.
  • Kada ka ƙirƙiri sabuwar manhaja da sabon package name don "farawa sabo". Za ka yi cikakken sabon closed test ko ta yaya.
  • Kada ka sake nema washegari da amsoshi iri ɗaya. Babu abin da ya canza, don haka tsammaci sakamako iri ɗaya.
  • Kada ka cika gurbi da wayoyinka na ajiya ko asusu na ƙarya. Google na iya ganin lokacin da asusu da yawa ke raba na'ura ɗaya.

Nemo wurin rauni

Buɗe closed track ɗinka a Play Console da ƙididdigar lokacin gwajin. Tambayi kanka da gaskiya:

  • Testers nawa ne suke opt in a ranar da ka nema? Shin adadin ya wuce 12 da kyau?
  • Nawa daga cikinsu ne suka buɗe manhajar a yawancin kwanaki, ba kawai a rana ta farko ba?
  • Shin ka ɗora aƙalla sabon build ɗaya yayin gwajin?
  • Shin za ka iya ambaton ra'ayoyin testers na gaske ka ambaci abin da ka canza saboda su?
  • Shin amsoshinka a fom sun ƙunshi lambobi, ko jumla ɗaya ko biyu ne na rubutu na gama-gari?

Yawancin aikace-aikacen da aka ƙi suna faɗuwa ne a engagement da amsoshi marasa tabbas. Duka biyun ana iya gyara su cikin makonni biyu.

Tsari don aikace-aikacen gaba

  1. Gyara duk matsalolin policy da farko, idan email ɗin ya ambace su.
  2. Duba adadin testers ɗinka. Nemo har sai kana da testers 15 ko fiye na gaske masu opt in, don masu tafiya kada su kai ka ƙasa da 12.
  3. Ba testers takamaiman ayyuka kowane mako, kamar "yi rajista, ƙirƙiri abu na farko, gwada allon saituna". Buƙatu marasa tabbas suna haifar da amfani maras tabbas.
  4. Tara ra'ayoyi a rubuce da fom mai sauƙi ko adireshin email, ka ajiye rajista.
  5. Gyara matsaloli da dama da aka ba da rahoto kuma ka ɗora sababbin builds a closed track yayin gwajin.
  6. Ci gaba da gwajin na aƙalla ƙarin kwana 14 da amfani na gaske na yau da kullum. Google bai wallafa ƙayyadadden lokacin jira ba, don haka bari shaida ta taru maimakon kallon kalanda.
  7. Sake rubuta amsoshinka da lambobi: testers da suka yi opt in, nawa ne suka yi amfani da manhajar a kai a kai, tester-days, kurakurai da aka samu aka gyara, builds da aka fitar.
  8. Sake nema daga Dashboard. Google ya ce duba yawanci yana ɗaukar kwana bakwai ko ƙasa.

Rubuta mafi kyawun amsoshi

Fom ɗin Google yana tambaya yadda nemo testers ya kasance sauƙi, yadda testers suka yi engagement, wane ra'ayi ka samu da yadda ka tattara su, ga wa manhajar take, darajarta, saukewar da ake tsammani, abin da ka canza bayan gwajin da yadda ka san ta shirya.

Ɗauke shi kamar gajeren rahoton gwaji. "Testers 15, 13 sun yi amfani da manhajar a aƙalla kwana 10 cikin 14, saƙonnin ra'ayi 21, rushewa 3 da aka gyara a builds 1.0.2 da 1.0.3, onboarding ya gajarta daga allo 4 zuwa 2" ya fi "testers sun ji daɗin manhajar" ƙarfi sosai. Kayan aikinmu na kyauta a https://testerexchange.com/tools/production-answers/ yana mayar da lambobinka zuwa amsoshin zayyana, kuma jagorar tambayoyin production access tana rufe kowace tambaya.

Idan testers ɗinka ne matsalar

Idan yawancin testers ɗinka na farko sun sauke sau ɗaya suka ɓace, zagaye na biyu da mutane iri ɗaya zai yi kama da na farko. Maye gurbinsu da mutanen da za su buɗe manhajar kowace rana.

Tester Exchange yana aika mutane na gaske da ke amfani da manhajarka a wayoyinsu na Android kuma yana nuna amfani na kowace rana ga kowane tester, don ka ga engagement yayin da gwajin ke gudana maimakon ka gano daga ƙi. Za ka iya biya sau ɗaya ($12.99 don testers 12, $14.99 don 15, $22.99 don 25) ko ka samu testers kyauta ta gwada wasu manhajoji a https://testerexchange.com/earn. Idan tester ya daina, sabo yana ɗaukar gurbin.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Shin sai na sake fara kwanaki 14 daga sifili?

Google ya ce kana iya buƙatar ci gaba da gudanar da closed test ɗinka. Ci gaba da riƙe track da testers na yanzu, kuma ka shirya don aƙalla ƙarin makonni biyu na amfani na gaske kafin ka sake nema.

Har yaushe sai na jira kafin in sake nema?

Help Center na Google bai bayyana ƙayyadadden lokacin jira ba. Sake nema lokacin da kake da ƙarin gwaji mafi kyau da za ka nuna a fili.

Shin ƙi zai cutar da asusun developer na?

Shawarar production access game da gwaji ba ɗaya take da keta policy ba. Ana nuna matsalolin policy daban a shafin Policy status.

Zan iya yin ƙara maimakon haka?

Ga shawarar gwaji, hanya mai amfani ita ce ƙarin gwaji da aikace-aikace mafi ƙarfi. Ga aiwatar da policy, bi link ɗin ƙara a wannan email ɗin.

Shin ya kamata in koma asusun ƙungiya?

Kawai idan kai kasuwanci ne na gaske mai lambar D-U-N-S. Asusun ƙungiya ba sa cikin buƙatar closed testing, amma canzawa yana buƙatar tabbatarwa da lokaci. Duba jagorar asusun ƙungiya da na kashin kai.
