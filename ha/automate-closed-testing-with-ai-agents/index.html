Abin da agent zai iya yi da wanda ba zai iya ba

Google yana buƙatar asusun developer na kashin kai da aka ƙirƙira bayan 13 Nuwamba 2023 su yi closed test da aƙalla testers 12 da suka yi opt in na kwana 14 a jere kafin neman production. Mafi yawan aikin da ke kewaye da hakan haɗin kai ne, wanda agents ke yi da kyau.

  • Agent zai iya: ƙara manhajarka, buɗe kamfen na testers, yin amfani da kiredit ɗinka, duba testers nawa ke aiki, dakatarwa ko rufe kamfen, ɗauko rahoton aikin yau da kullum da taimakawa wajen rubuta amsoshin production access daga gare shi.
  • Mutum dole ya yi: ƙara Google Group na testers a closed track a Play Console, wallafa release, biyan kiredit (biyan kuɗi koyaushe yana faruwa a browser na mutum) da danna Apply for production.
  • Babu wanda zai iya sarrafawa: kwanaki 14 na amfani na gaske. Mutane na gaske ne ke yin hakan a wayoyinsu na Android, kuma shi ne ainihin manufar dokar Google.

1. Ƙirƙiri token

  1. Shiga a https://testerexchange.com/api.
  2. Ƙirƙiri token na kanka. Yana farawa da tx_. Kwafe shi yanzu ka adana shi kamar kalmar sirri.
  3. Za a iya soke tokens a kowane lokaci. Token zai iya kashe kiredit a kan kamfen, amma ba zai iya biyan kuɗi ba, cire kuɗi ko share asusunka.

2. Haɗa MCP server

MCP server yana amfani da Streamable HTTP a https://vvrlxzbngxfxddywidoq.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp. Aika token ɗinka a header Authorization: Bearer tx_YOUR_TOKEN.

Claude Code: gudanar da wannan sau ɗaya a terminal ɗinka.

claude mcp add --transport http tester-exchange https://vvrlxzbngxfxddywidoq.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp --header "Authorization: Bearer tx_YOUR_TOKEN"

Claude Desktop, Cursor, VS Code da sauran clients masu JSON config: ƙara server mai suna tester-exchange mai type http, URL ɗin da ke sama, da shigarwar headers da Authorization aka saita zuwa Bearer tx_YOUR_TOKEN. Ainihin wurin fayil ɗin config ya dogara da client ɗinka; duba takardun MCP ɗinsa.

Clients da ke karɓar URL kaɗai (wasu ChatGPT da web connectors): takardun da ke https://testerexchange.com/agents/ sun lissafa tsarin URL musu. Ba tare da token ba, kayan aikin jama'a (menene Tester Exchange, farashi, ƙididdigar dandamali) suna ci gaba da aiki.

3. Kayan aiki ɗaya na farawa: get_testers

Ba sai ka koyi dukkan jerin kayan aiki ba. Babban kayan aikin shi ne get_testers(package_name, name?). Kira ɗaya yana ƙara manhajarka, yana buɗe gwajinta, yana amfani da kiredit ɗinka kuma yana dawo da ci gabanka tare da matakan da mutum ne kaɗai zai iya yi, kamar ƙara Google Group a Play Console. Kira shi kuma a kowane lokaci don duba ci gaba.

Sauran kayan aikin suna rufe cikakkun bayanai: my_apps, campaign_report (testers, aikin yau da kullum, ra'ayoyi da bayanan production-access), add_testers, set_campaign_status (dakatarwa, ci gaba ko rufewa; kiredit da ba a yi amfani da su ba suna komawa balance ɗinka), share_kit da buy_credits_links, wanda ke dawo da links na biyan kuɗi don mutum ya biya.

Prompts da ke aiki

  • "Samo mini testers 15 don com.example.app ka gaya mini abin da zan yi a Play Console."
  • "Duba kamfen na Tester Exchange don com.example.app. Testers nawa ne suka yi aiki kowace rana a wannan makon?"
  • "Ɗauko rahoton kamfen ka rubuta amsoshin production access na. Yi amfani da ainihin lambobi kawai."
  • "Rufe kamfen na com.example.app yanzu da aka amince da production access."

Nemi testers 15 maimakon 12, don mai tafiya ɗaya kada ya kai ka ƙasa da mafi ƙarancin Google.

HTTP API don scripts da CI

Idan kayan aikinka ba sa magana da MCP, yi amfani da HTTP endpoint: POST https://vvrlxzbngxfxddywidoq.supabase.co/rest/v1/rpc/agent_call. Jikin saƙon JSON ne mai p_token (token ɗinka na tx_), p_action (misali help, my_apps ko start_campaign) da p_args (misali package_name da adadin testers). Aika public apikey header da aka nuna a takardun.

Kira action na help da farko: yana dawo da kowane action da hujjojinsa. Cikakken schema yana a https://testerexchange.com/openapi.json, wanda ke aiki da ChatGPT custom GPT actions da sauran kayan aikin OpenAPI.

A2A agent card

Don saitin agent-zuwa-agent, Tester Exchange yana wallafa A2A agent card a https://testerexchange.com/.well-known/agent-card.json. Orchestrators da ke tallafawa A2A protocol za su iya gano abin da agent ɗin yake bayarwa da yadda ake kiransa. Yi amfani da token ɗin tx_ iri ɗaya don duk abin da ya shafi asusunka.

Cikakken tsari, daga farko zuwa ƙarshe

  1. Ka ɗora .aab ɗinka na farko a closed track a Play Console.
  2. Agent ɗinka yana kiran get_testers don package name ɗinka ya gaya maka ka ƙara testerexchange-testers@googlegroups.com a ƙarƙashin Google Groups a closed track.
  3. Ka ƙara group ɗin, ka adana, ka tabbatar an wallafa release ɗin.
  4. Idan ba ka da kiredit, agent ya ba ka link na biyan kuɗi. Ka biya ($12.99 don testers 12, $14.99 don 15, $22.99 don 25) ko ka samu kiredit kyauta ta gwada wasu manhajoji a https://testerexchange.com/earn.
  5. Testers na gaske suna sauke manhajarka su yi amfani da ita na kwana 14. Agent ɗinka yana duba ci gaba kowace rana kuma yana gargaɗinka idan testers masu aiki sun ragu.
  6. A rana ta 15, agent yana ɗauko rahoton kamfen ya rubuta amsoshin production access ɗinka. Ka duba su ka danna Apply for production.

Bayanan tsaro

  • Ka nisanta tokens daga git. Saka su a wurin adana sirri na client ɗinka ko environment variable.
  • Ana adana tokens ne kawai a matsayin hashes a gefenmu kuma an iyakance su zuwa 10 ga kowane asusu. Soke duk token da ba ka amfani da shi.
  • Karanta abin da agent ya rubuta a fom ɗin production kafin ka aika. Google yana neman ka bayyana gwajin da gaskiya.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Shin AI agent zai iya kammala gwajin kwana 14 maimakon ni?

A'a. Kwanaki 14 suna buƙatar mutane na gaske da ke amfani da manhajarka a wayoyinsu. Agents suna kula da saiti, sa ido da rahoto.

Shin agent zai iya biyan kuɗin testers?

A'a. Zai iya dawo da links na biyan kuɗi, amma biyan koyaushe yana faruwa a browser na mutum.

Wane clients ne ke aiki da MCP server?

Duk client da ke tallafawa remote MCP ta Streamable HTTP da custom header, ciki har da Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor da VS Code. Sauran za su iya amfani da HTTP API.

A ina zan samu token?

A https://testerexchange.com/api bayan shiga. Tokens suna farawa da tx_ kuma ana iya soke su a kowane lokaci.

Shin agent yana canza wani abu a Play Console ɗina?

A'a. Ba shi da damar shiga Play Console ɗinka. Yana gaya maka matakan da za ka yi a can.
