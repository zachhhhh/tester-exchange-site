Neman production access

  1. Buɗe Play Console ka je Dashboard na manhajarka.
  2. Danna Apply for production. Google yana nuna wannan da zarar an cika buƙatar closed testing.
  3. Amsa sassa uku: game da closed test ɗinka (nemo testers, engagement, ra'ayoyi), game da manhajarka (masu amfani, daraja, saukewar da ake tsammani a shekarar farko) da game da shirin production (abin da ka canza da yadda ka san ta shirya).
  4. Danna Apply. Google yana gargaɗi cewa amsoshi suna ɓacewa idan ka bar shafin kafin Next ko Apply, don haka ka rubuta su a wani wuri da farko.

Har yaushe duban yake ɗauka

Help Center na Google ya ce duban aikace-aikacen production access yawanci yana ɗaukar kwana bakwai ko ƙasa, amma wani lokaci yana iya ɗaukar tsawon lokaci. Mai asusun yana samun email idan an kammala duban.

Production release ɗinka na farko

Production access ba ɗaya yake da fitowa kai tsaye ba. Har yanzu kana buƙatar ƙirƙirar production release, kuma yana wuce duban manhaja na yau da kullum na Google kamar kowace manhaja. Google bai yi alkawarin lokaci tabbatacce ga wannan duban ba. Sababbin manhajoji daga sababbin asusu sukan ɗauki tsawon lokaci fiye da sabuntawa daga asusu da aka kafa, don haka ba da kwanaki da dama ka tsara ranar ƙaddamarwa da ragi.

  1. Je Test and release, sannan Production, sannan Create new release.
  2. Ɗaga build daga closed track ɗinka, ko ɗora sabon .aab. Yin amfani da build ɗin da testers ɗinka suka gudanar shi ne zaɓi mafi aminci.
  3. Rubuta release notes ka zaɓi ƙasashe.
  4. Yi la'akari da staged rollout (misali 20% na masu amfani) don ka kama matsaloli da wuri.
  5. Aika canje-canjen don duba a Publishing overview. Idan managed publishing yana kunne, danna Publish bayan amincewa don fitowa kai tsaye lokacin da ka zaɓa.
  6. Da zarar an amince da shi aka wallafa, manhajar na iya ɗaukar ɗan lokaci kafin ta bayyana a binciken Play Store. Raba link kai tsaye a halin yanzu.

  • Duban production release na farko: sau da yawa 'yan kwanaki; yana iya ɗaukar tsawon lokaci.
  • Jimilla: shirya don kusan makonni huɗu daga ɗorawa ta farko ta closed test zuwa fitowa ga jama'a, da ƙari idan akwai abin da ake bukatar gyarawa.

Bayan ƙaddamarwa

Google yana ba da shawarar ci gaba da gwada manhajarka kafin wallafa sabuntawar production. Ajiye closed track a matsayin tashar beta ga testers ɗinka na farko, kuma ka yi la'akari da open testing don mafi yawan masu sauraro.
