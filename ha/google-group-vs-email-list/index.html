Kowane closed testing track yana buƙatar jerin waɗanda za su iya sauke manhajar. Play Console yana ba ka zaɓi biyu: jerin email da kake sarrafawa a Play Console, ko Google Group. Duka biyun suna aiki don sharaɗin testers 12. Bambancin shi ne yawan aikin da ake yi don ƙara mutane.

Kwatanci a taƙaice

  • Google Group: duk wanda ke da link na group zai iya shiga, don haka ba sai ka ƙara mutane ɗaya bayan ɗaya ba. Ya fi dacewa idan testers suna zuwa daga wurare da yawa.
  • Jerin email: kana liƙa email na account na Google na kowane tester a Play Console. Cikakken iko kan wanda ya shiga, amma dole ka ci gaba da sabunta shi yayin da mutane ke shiga.

Saita Google Group

  1. Je zuwa groups.google.com ka ƙirƙiri group. Saita "Who can join" zuwa kowa a yanar gizo, don kada testers su jira amincewa.
  2. A Play Console → Testing → Closed testing → track ɗinka → Testers, zaɓi Google Groups ka ƙara adireshin email na group ɗin.
  3. Raba links biyu da testers: link na group (a shiga tukuna) da link na opt-in (zama tester).
  4. Testers suna sauke manhajar daga Google Play da account na Google ɗin da suka yi amfani da shi wajen shiga group ɗin.

Saita jerin email

  1. A Play Console → Testing → Closed testing → track ɗinka → Testers, ƙirƙiri jerin email.
  2. Liƙa email na accounts na Google na testers ɗinka, a raba su da waƙafi, ka adana.
  3. Raba link na opt-in. Dole testers su buɗe shi yayin da suka shiga da email ɗin da ka ƙara.

Kurakuran da ke hana testers ganin manhajarka

  • Tester ya shiga da wani account na Google amma Play Store na wayarsa yana amfani da wani daban.
  • Group ɗin yana buƙatar amincewa, don haka testers suna jira babu motsi.
  • Tester ya shiga group amma bai taɓa buɗe link na opt-in ya taɓa "Become a tester" ba.
  • Ba a amince da release ɗin ba tukuna. Testers ba za su iya sauke ba har sai closed track ya fara aiki.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Shin membobin group ana ƙirga su cikin testers 12?

Mutanen da suka shiga ta link na opt-in ne kawai ake ƙirgawa, ko wane irin jeri kake amfani da shi.

Zan iya amfani da duka biyun?

Eh. Track ɗaya zai iya samun jerin email da Google Groups a lokaci ɗaya.

Testers za su iya ɓoye sunansu?

Da Google Group ba za ka taɓa buƙatar tattara email da kanka ba. Da jerin email kana buƙatar email na account na Google na kowane tester.
