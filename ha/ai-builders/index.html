An sabunta a Nuwamba 2026

Ko ka gina app ɗinka na Android ta vibe coding ko ka rubuta shi da hannu da editan AI, Google Play har yanzu yana buƙatar masu gwaji 12 da suka shiga na kwana 14 kafin sabon asusun mai haɓaka app na kai-da-kai ya iya wallafa zuwa production. Wannan shafi yana jera jagorar Tester Exchange ga kowane kayan aikin AI: yadda ake ƙirƙirar Android App Bundle, yadda ake fara gwajin rufaffe, da yadda ake samun masu gwaji, kyauta ta hanyar gwada wasu apps tukuna, ko da biyan kuɗi kuma cikin sauri. Kowane shafi kuma yana nuna ainihin tsarin kwafa-da-manna don kayan aikin AI ɗinka ya iya sarrafa Tester Exchange da kansa.

Jagorori bisa kayan aiki

Ana ƙara ƙarin kayan aiki akai-akai. A yau:

  • Cursor: https://testerexchange.com/guide/get-12-testers-cursor/ - gina AAB da Gradle, sannan haɗa Tester Exchange da Cursor ta .cursor/mcp.json.

Hanyoyi biyu na samun masu gwaji

  • Kyauta: gwada apps na wasu masu haɓaka a https://testerexchange.com/earn a wayarka. Kowane app da ka gwada yana kawo mai gwaji ɗaya zuwa naka, don haka za ka iya cike gurbi 12 ba tare da biya ba.
  • Biyan kuɗi kuma cikin sauri: biya sau ɗaya don fakitin masu gwaji; farashin yanzu yana a https://testerexchange.com/llms.txt. Apps masu biya suna samun masu gwaji kafin kowa.

Abin da Google ke buƙata

Taimakon Play Console na Google ya ce asusun mai haɓaka na kai-da-kai da aka ƙirƙira bayan 13 Nuwamba 2023 dole ne su gudanar da gwajin rufaffe da masu gwaji akalla 12 da suka shiga ba tare da yankewa ba na akalla kwana 14 kafin su iya neman damar production (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465). Babu kayan aikin AI da zai iya yin waɗannan kwana 14 maimakonka: dole ne mutane na gaske masu wayoyi na gaske.

Bar kayan aikin AI ɗinka ya yi saitin

Tester Exchange yana da sabar MCP, HTTP API da wakili na A2A. Cikakken jerin clients da samfurin saiti yana a https://testerexchange.com/agents/. Jagorar gabaɗaya ita ce https://testerexchange.com/guide/automate-closed-testing-with-ai-agents/.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Ina buƙatar kayan aikin AI don amfani da Tester Exchange?

A'a. Shafin yanar gizon yana aiki da kansa. Sabar MCP da API gajerun hanyoyi ne na zaɓi ga mutanen da suke aiki da kayan aikin AI.

Shin kayan aikin AI zai iya biyan kuɗin masu gwaji?

A'a. Yana iya mayar da hanyar biyan kuɗi, amma biyan yana faruwa a browser ɗinka. Hanyar kyauta ita ce gwada wasu apps a https://testerexchange.com/earn.

Shin Tester Exchange haɗin gwiwa ne na hukuma na waɗannan kayan aikin?

A'a. Sabis ne mai zaman kansa. Waɗannan shafuka suna nuna yadda ake haɗa goyon bayan MCP ko HTTP na yau da kullun na kayan aiki da Tester Exchange ne kawai.
