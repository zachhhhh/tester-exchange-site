Wa ke buƙatar yin closed testing

Help Center na Google ya ce buƙatar tana shafar asusun developer na kashin kai da aka ƙirƙira bayan 13 Nuwamba 2023. Har sai waɗannan manhajojin sun ci ta, ba su cancanci rarrabawa a Google Play ba.

Idan an ƙirƙiri asusunka kafin wannan ranar, ko asusun ƙungiya ne (kasuwanci da aka tabbatar da lambar D-U-N-S), buƙatar closed testing ba ta shafe ka ba. Kowace manhaja a asusun kashin kai da abin ya shafa tana bin wannan tsari kafin ta sami production access.

  • Asusun kashin kai, aka ƙirƙira bayan 13 Nuw 2023: ana buƙatar closed test.
  • Asusun kashin kai, aka ƙirƙira kafin 13 Nuw 2023: ba a buƙata.
  • Asusun ƙungiya: ba a buƙata, amma kana buƙatar lambar D-U-N-S da website na ƙungiya da aka tabbatar. Duba jagorar asusun ƙungiya da na kashin kai.

Dokar a jumla ɗaya

Yi closed test da aƙalla testers 12 da suka ci gaba da opt in na aƙalla kwana 14 ba tare da tsayawa ba, sannan ka nemi production access daga Play Console Dashboard.

Kalmomin Google suna da muhimmanci. Dole kwanaki 14 su zama a jere. Testers da suka yi opt in, suka yi gwaji ƙasa da kwana 14, sannan suka yi opt out ba a ƙirga su. Kuma aƙalla testers 12 dole su kasance a opt in a ranar da kake nema.

Adadin ya kasance testers 20 har zuwa Disamba 2024, lokacin da Google ya rage shi zuwa 12. Kwanaki 14 ba su canza ba.

Abin da ake ƙirgawa a matsayin tester

  • Google Account na gaske da ya yi opt in. Kasancewa a jerin email ɗinka ko Google Group bai isa ba. Kowane mutum dole ya buɗe link na opt-in ya taɓa don zama tester.
  • A closed track. Mutanen da suka yi opt in zuwa internal test ɗinka ba su cancanci closed tests ba har sai sun bar internal test. Internal testing ba a ƙirga shi cikin buƙatar.
  • Opt in a kowane lokaci. Idan wani ya fita ya sake shiga, ƙirgar kwanakinsa na a jere tana sake farawa.
  • Yin amfani da manhajar. Google bai wallafa mafi ƙarancin adadin buɗewa ba, amma fom ɗin production yana tambaya yadda testers suka yi engagement da ko amfaninsu ya yi daidai da abin da kake tsammani a production. Testers da ke sauke sau ɗaya su ɓace su ne babban dalilin da ake ƙin aikace-aikace.

Jadawalin lokaci na gaske

  1. Rana ta 0: ƙirƙiri closed track, ƙara testers (jerin email ko Google Group), ɗora app bundle ka aika release don duba.
  2. Sa'o'i zuwa 'yan kwanaki bayan haka: an wallafa release ɗin kuma link na opt-in yana aiki. Google ya lura link na gwaji na farko na iya ɗaukar sa'o'i da dama kafin ya fara aiki.
  3. Kwana 1 zuwa 14: aƙalla testers 12 suna ci gaba da opt in suna amfani da manhajar. Fitar da aƙalla sabuntawa ɗaya bisa ra'ayoyinsu.
  4. Rana ta 15 ko daga baya: danna Apply for production a Dashboard ka amsa tambayoyin.
  5. Kusan mako ɗaya bayan haka: Google ya ce duba yawanci yana ɗaukar kwana bakwai ko ƙasa, amma wani lokaci yana iya ɗaukar tsawon lokaci. Za ka samu email idan an gama.
  6. Bayan amincewa: shafin Production yana buɗewa. Production release ɗinka na farko har yanzu yana wuce duban manhaja na yau da kullum.

Shirya don kusan makonni uku zuwa huɗu daga ɗorawa ta farko ta closed test zuwa fitowa ga jama'a, idan babu abin da ya yi kuskure.

Fom ɗin production access

Fom ɗin Google yana da sassa uku. Sashe na ɗaya yana game da closed test ɗinka: yadda ya kasance sauƙi a nemo testers, yadda suka yi engagement, da taƙaitaccen ra'ayoyinsu da yadda ka tattara su. Sashe na biyu yana game da manhajarka: masu amfani da aka nufa, darajar da take bai wa masu amfani, da saukewa nawa kake tsammani a shekarar farko. Sashe na uku yana game da shirin production: abin da ka canza bayan gwajin da yadda ka yanke shawarar manhajar ta shirya.

Rubuta amsoshin kafin ka buɗe fom ɗin. Google yana gargaɗi cewa amsoshinka suna ɓacewa idan ka bar shafin kafin danna Next ko Apply. Jagorarmu kan tambayoyin production access da kayan aikin amsa kyauta a https://testerexchange.com/tools/production-answers/ suna taimaka maka rubuta su daga ainihin lambobi.

Me yasa manhajoji ba sa ci

Help Center na Google ya lissafa dalilai biyu da za su sa ka ci gaba da closed test ɗinka: ƙasa da testers 12 masu opt in, ko rashin isasshen engagement daga testers. A zahiri developers suna cin karo da waɗannan matsalolin:

  • Neman mutane 12 daidai. Mai tafiya ɗaya a rana ta 10 sai ka koma 11.
  • Testers da suke sauke manhajar ba su sake buɗe ta ba.
  • Asusu da yawa a waya ɗaya, ko asusu da ba mutane na gaske ba.
  • Babu sabuntawa kuma babu ra'ayoyi cikin kwanaki 14, don haka babu abin da za a bayyana a fom.
  • Gajerun amsoshi na gama-gari a cikin tambayoyin.

Idan ka riga ka samu amsar "more testing required", karanta jagorar rashin isasshen testing engagement da jagorar abin da za ka yi idan an ƙi production access.
