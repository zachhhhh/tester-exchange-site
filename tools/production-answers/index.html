Production access answer generator

Play Console's "Apply for production" form has three parts: your closed test, your app, and production readiness. Enter what happened in your test to get a draft for each written question, plus guidance for the two questions where you pick an option. Drafts only use what you enter; anything you leave blank becomes a [bracket] to fill in. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Drafts are in English, which reviewers read everywhere.

Your draft answers

Edit these drafts so they match exactly what happened. Google can see your real test data, so honest, specific answers work best.

Part 1: About your closed test

How easy was it to recruit testers?

Here Play Console asks you to pick an option, not to write text. Choose the one that honestly fits.

Tester engagement during your closed test

Google wants to know whether testers used all the features, and whether their use matched how real users will use the app.

Summary of tester feedback, and how you collected it

Name the main themes testers raised, and say how you collected the feedback.

Part 2: About your app or game

Target audience

Be specific: who, where, and what need the app serves. This answer isn't shown publicly.

How your app provides value (for games: what makes it unique)

Say what users can do with the app and why it helps them.

Estimated installs in the first year

Here Play Console asks you to pick a range. An honest, modest estimate is fine.

Part 3: About your production readiness

Changes you made based on the closed test

List real fixes, and mention the new builds you uploaded to the closed track.

How you decided the app is ready for production

Say what you checked before applying, such as fixed bugs, no crashes and the main flows working.

Before you apply

  • Google counts testers who stay opted in to your closed test for 14 days in a row; a tester who leaves and rejoins starts over. Your Play Console Dashboard shows when you can apply.
  • Fix crashes, broken features and missing screens first: Google says review is not a troubleshooting step.
  • If your app needs a login, add working test credentials in Play Console so reviewers can open every feature.
  • Check that your target age group and store listing match the app's real content.
  • Review usually takes 7 days or less. If Google asks for more testing, keep the closed test running and apply again.
